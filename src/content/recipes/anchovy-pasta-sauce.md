---
title: Anchovy Pasta Sauce
description: A family favorite from an old copy of The Gourmet Cookbook. Garlic and anchovies melted into olive oil, simmered with tomato and basil, and spooned over pasta.
category: Basics
cuisine: Italian
prepMinutes: 5
cookMinutes: 35
servings: About 1 cup of sauce
date: 2026-11-08
tags: [pasta, sauce, family-recipe]
ingredients:
  - 1 clove garlic, finely chopped
  - ¼ cup olive oil
  - 12 anchovy fillets, cut into pieces
  - ⅔ cup tomato purée
  - Salt, to taste
  - Freshly ground black pepper, to taste
  - Sweet basil, to taste
  - Cooked pasta, for serving
  - Chopped fresh parsley, for serving
  - Grated Parmesan or Romano cheese, for serving
steps:
  - Warm the olive oil in a small saucepan over low heat. Add the garlic and cook gently until it just starts to turn golden.
  - Add the anchovy pieces and stir until they're heated through and start to melt into the oil.
  - Stir in the tomato purée. Season with pepper and basil, and add salt carefully, since the anchovies are already salty.
  - Stir everything together well, put the lid on and let it simmer on low for 30 minutes, stirring now and then.
  - Pour the sauce over hot cooked pasta and finish with chopped parsley and grated Parmesan or Romano.
tips:
  - Don't let the anchovies put you off. They melt away into the sauce and leave a rich, savory flavor rather than a fishy one. The kids in the family loved it.
