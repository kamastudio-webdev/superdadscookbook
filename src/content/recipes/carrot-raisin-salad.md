title: Carrot Raisin Salad
description: Freshly grated carrots with plumped golden raisins and bits of pineapple in a creamy, lightly sweet lemon dressing.
category: Sides
prepMinutes: 15
cookMinutes: 0
servings: 2 to 3 servings
date: 2026-11-08

adaptedFrom:
  name: Ina Garten, Food Network
  url: https://www.foodnetwork.com/recipes/barefoot-carrot-salad-recipe-1937776
ingredients:
  - ⅓ cup golden raisins
  - 1 lb carrots, peeled
  - 2 tablespoons fresh lemon juice
  - ¼ cup sour cream
  - ¼ cup mayonnaise
  - 3 tablespoons sugar
  - ½ teaspoon salt
  - ⅓ cup fresh pineapple, cut into small dice
steps:
  - Put the raisins in a small bowl and pour boiling water over them to cover. Let them soak for 5 minutes so they plump up, then drain them well.
  - While the raisins soak, grate the carrots. With a food processor, use the grating disc and lay the carrot halves sideways in the feed tube so you get long shreds; work in batches. A box grater works too.
  - Put the grated carrots in a medium bowl, pour the lemon juice over them and toss.
  - In a small bowl, whisk the sour cream, mayonnaise, sugar and salt until smooth.
  - Pour the dressing over the carrots, add the pineapple and drained raisins, and toss until everything is coated. Serve right away or keep it cold in the fridge.
tips:
  - Laying the carrots on their sides in the food processor tube gives longer, prettier shreds than standing them upright.
  - The lemon juice keeps the carrots bright and balances the sweet dressing, so don't skip it.
