title: Beef Bourguignon
description: Ina Garten's rich beef stew with bacon, carrots, mushrooms and pearl onions, slow-braised in red wine and Cognac and spooned over garlic-rubbed toast.
category: Dinner

servings: 6 servings

ingredients:
  - section: Stew
    items:
      - 1 tablespoon good olive oil
      - 8 ounces applewood-smoked bacon (dry-cured, center-cut), diced
      - 2½ pounds beef chuck, cut into 1-inch cubes
      - Kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper
      - 1 pound carrots, cut on an angle into 1-inch chunks
      - 2 yellow onions, sliced
      - 2 teaspoons chopped garlic (about 2 cloves)
      - ½ cup Cognac
      - 1 bottle (750 ml) good dry red wine, such as Côtes du Rhône or Pinot Noir
      - 1 can (2 cups) beef broth
      - 1 tablespoon tomato paste
      - 1 teaspoon fresh thyme leaves (or ½ teaspoon dried)
      - 4 tablespoons unsalted butter, softened, divided
      - 3 tablespoons all-purpose flour
      - 1 pound frozen whole small onions
      - 1 pound fresh mushrooms, stems removed, caps thickly sliced
  - section: For serving
    items:
      - Country bread or sourdough, toasted or grilled
      - 1 garlic clove, cut in half
      - ½ cup chopped fresh parsley (optional)
steps:
  - Heat the oven to 250°F.
  - Warm the olive oil in a large Dutch oven over medium heat. Add the bacon and cook for about 10 minutes, stirring now and then, until lightly browned. Lift it out with a slotted spoon onto a large plate, leaving the fat in the pot.
  - Pat the beef cubes dry with paper towels and season them with salt and pepper. Brown them in the hot fat in batches, in a single layer, for 3 to 5 minutes per batch, turning so every side gets browned. Move each batch to the plate with the bacon.
  - Add the carrots and sliced onions to the pot with 1 tablespoon of salt and 2 teaspoons of pepper. Cook for 10 to 15 minutes, stirring now and then, until the onions are lightly browned. Stir in the garlic and cook 1 minute more.
  - Pour in the Cognac. Stand back and carefully light it with a long match to burn off the alcohol. The flames die down on their own in a few seconds.
  - Return the beef, bacon and any juices from the plate to the pot. Pour in the whole bottle of wine and enough beef broth to almost cover the meat, then stir in the tomato paste and thyme.
  - Bring it to a simmer, cover with a tight-fitting lid and put it in the oven for about 1¼ hours, until the beef and carrots are very tender when you poke them with a fork.
  - In a small bowl, mash 2 tablespoons of the butter and the flour together with a fork into a paste. Stir it into the stew, then add the frozen onions.
  - Melt the other 2 tablespoons of butter in a skillet over medium heat and cook the mushrooms for about 10 minutes, until lightly browned. Add them to the stew.
  - Set the pot on the stove and bring the stew to a boil, then turn the heat down and simmer for 15 minutes, until the sauce thickens. Taste and add salt and pepper if it needs it.
  - Toast or grill the bread and rub one side of each slice with the cut garlic clove. Lay a slice in each bowl, ladle the stew over it and sprinkle with parsley.
tips:
  - Before lighting the Cognac, turn off the stove's exhaust fan and keep sleeves, hair and anything flammable well back.
  - Dry the beef well before browning. Wet meat steams instead of getting a deep brown crust.
