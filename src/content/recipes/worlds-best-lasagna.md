ingredients:
  - section: Meat sauce
    items:
      - 1 pound sweet Italian sausage
      - ¾ pound lean ground beef
      - ½ cup minced onion
      - 2 cloves garlic, crushed
      - 1 (28-ounce) can crushed tomatoes
      - 2 (6.5-ounce) cans tomato sauce
      - 2 (6-ounce) cans tomato paste
      - ½ cup water
      - 2 tablespoons white sugar
      - 2 tablespoons chopped fresh parsley
      - 1½ teaspoons dried basil
      - 1 teaspoon salt, or to taste
      - 1 teaspoon Italian seasoning
      - ½ teaspoon fennel seeds
      - ¼ teaspoon ground black pepper
  - section: Ricotta layer
    items:
      - 16 ounces ricotta cheese
      - 1 egg
      - 2 tablespoons chopped fresh parsley
      - ½ teaspoon salt
  - section: Layers
    items:
      - 12 lasagna noodles
      - ¾ pound mozzarella cheese, sliced
      - ¾ cup grated Parmesan cheese
steps:
  - In a Dutch oven over medium heat, cook the sausage, ground beef, onion and garlic, breaking the meat into small pieces, until nicely browned.
  - Stir in the crushed tomatoes, tomato sauce, tomato paste and water. Add the sugar, 2 tablespoons of parsley, the basil, 1 teaspoon salt, the Italian seasoning, fennel seeds and pepper.
  - Cover and let the sauce simmer for about 1½ hours, giving it a stir every so often.
  - When the sauce is almost done, bring a large pot of lightly salted water to a boil and cook the noodles for 8 to 10 minutes. Drain them and rinse with cold water so they don't stick together.
  - In a mixing bowl, stir the ricotta, egg, the other 2 tablespoons of parsley and ½ teaspoon salt together.
  - Heat the oven to 375°F.
  - "Build the first layer in a 9 × 13-inch baking dish: spread 1½ cups of meat sauce on the bottom, lay 6 noodles lengthwise on top, spread on half of the ricotta mixture, then cover with one-third of the mozzarella slices. Spoon another 1½ cups of sauce over the mozzarella and sprinkle with ¼ cup Parmesan."
  - Repeat the layers with the remaining noodles, ricotta and sauce, then finish with the rest of the mozzarella and Parmesan on top.
  - Cover the dish with foil. Spray the underside of the foil with cooking spray, or tent it so it doesn't touch the cheese, so the cheese doesn't stick.
  - Bake for 25 minutes. Take off the foil and bake 25 minutes more, until bubbly and browned on top.
  - Let the lasagna sit for 15 minutes before cutting so the layers hold together.
tips:
  - The sauce can be made a day ahead and kept in the fridge. Warm it up before you start layering.
