title: Cream of Chicken Soup
description: A from-scratch cream of chicken soup with carrots, celery and onion, a bundle of fresh herbs, diced chicken and a finish of cream and a little sherry.

ingredients:
  - ½ cup (1 stick) unsalted butter
  - 1 medium Spanish onion, chopped
  - 2 stalks celery, leaves and all, chopped
  - 3 medium carrots, chopped
  - ½ cup plus 1 tablespoon flour
  - 7 cups chicken broth, homemade or low-sodium
  - 3 sprigs parsley
  - 3 sprigs fresh thyme
  - 1 bay leaf
  - 2¾ cups diced cooked chicken
  - ½ cup heavy cream
  - 2½ teaspoons dry sherry
  - 1 tablespoon kosher salt
  - Freshly ground black pepper
  - 2 tablespoons chopped flat-leaf parsley, for serving
steps:
  - Melt the butter in a large soup pot over medium heat. Add the onion, celery and carrots, cover, and cook for about 12 minutes, stirring now and then, until soft.
  - Sprinkle the flour over the vegetables and stir with a wooden spoon for 2 minutes.
  - Pour in the broth while whisking the whole time, and bring it to a boil.
  - Tie the parsley sprigs, thyme and bay leaf together with kitchen twine and drop the bundle into the pot. Turn the heat down and simmer for 15 minutes.
  - Stir in the chicken and bring the soup back to a boil, then take it off the heat.
  - Whisk in the cream, sherry and salt, and add pepper to taste. Pull out the herb bundle.
  - Ladle into bowls, sprinkle with the chopped parsley and serve right away.
tips:
  - Leftover roast chicken or a store-bought rotisserie chicken works well for the diced chicken.
