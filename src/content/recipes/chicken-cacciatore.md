---
title: Chicken Cacciatore
description: A simple family-style chicken cacciatore with floured chicken pieces browned in olive oil, then simmered with onion, tomatoes, green pepper, garlic and a splash of white wine.
category: Dinner
cuisine: Italian
prepMinutes: 15
cookMinutes: 65
servings: 4 to 6 servings
date: 2026-11-08
tags: [chicken, one-pan, family-recipe]
ingredients:
  - 1 large onion
  - 4 tablespoons olive oil, divided
  - 3 to 3½ pounds chicken pieces
  - Flour, for coating the chicken
  - Salt and pepper
  - 2 cups chopped tomatoes, fresh or canned
  - 1 clove garlic, finely chopped
  - 1 cup coarsely chopped green pepper
  - ½ cup white wine
steps:
  - Coarsely chop the onion. Heat 2 tablespoons of the olive oil in a large, deep skillet with a lid over medium heat and cook the onion until it's lightly browned, about 5 minutes. Scoop the onion out and set it aside.
  - Season the chicken pieces with salt and pepper and coat them in flour, shaking off the extra.
  - Add the other 2 tablespoons of olive oil to the pan and brown the chicken well on all sides, about 10 minutes.
  - Add the tomatoes, garlic and green pepper, then put the onion back in the pan. Cover and simmer over low heat for 15 minutes.
  - Pour in the wine and season with salt and pepper to taste. Cover again and simmer for 30 minutes more, until the chicken is tender and cooked through (165°F).
tips:
  - Spoon the sauce over pasta, rice or crusty bread.
