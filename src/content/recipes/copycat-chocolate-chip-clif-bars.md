title: Copycat Chocolate Chip Clif Bars
description: Chewy, date-sweetened oat and crispy rice bars with mini chocolate chips, shaped by hand and baked low and slow. A double batch for the lunchbox stash.

servings: 16 bars

  name: Feasting on Fruit

ingredients:
  - section: Date mixture
    items:
      - 24 Medjool dates, pitted
      - ½ cup water
      - ⅔ cup coconut nectar or maple syrup
      - 2 tablespoons molasses
      - 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
  - section: Dry mix
    items:
      - 2 cups brown rice crisp cereal
      - 2 cups rolled oats
      - ½ cup vegan vanilla protein powder (or oat flour)
      - ½ cup oat bran
      - 4 tablespoons flaxseed meal (¼ cup)
      - ½ cup vegan mini chocolate chips
steps:
  - Heat the oven to 300°F and line a large baking sheet with parchment paper.
  - Put the dates, water, coconut nectar or maple syrup, molasses and vanilla in a blender and blend until you have a smooth paste.
  - In a large mixing bowl, stir together the rice cereal, oats, protein powder, oat bran and flaxseed meal.
  - Scrape the date paste into the bowl and mix until all the dry ingredients are coated and the dough sticks together. Stir in the chocolate chips.
  - Split the dough in half. On the lined baking sheet, press each half into a long log about 3 inches wide and ½ inch tall. Cut each log into 8 bars with a knife or bench scraper and nudge them a little apart.
  - Bake for 20 minutes.
  - Let the bars cool completely, then store them in an airtight container in the fridge.
tips:
  - This is a double batch. For 8 bars, halve every amount and shape one log.
  - No protein powder? Oat flour works in its place.
  - Use crispy brown rice cereal, not puffed rice, so the bars keep their crunch.
