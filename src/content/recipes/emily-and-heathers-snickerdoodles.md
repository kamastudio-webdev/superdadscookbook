ingredients:
  - section: Cookie dough
    items:
      - 1 cup butter, softened
      - "Sugar (TO DO: the card says \"1  cups\" with a gap. A fraction didn't print, maybe 1½ cups.)"
      - 2 eggs
      - 1 teaspoon vanilla
      - 2⅔ cups flour, not sifted
      - 1 teaspoon cream of tartar
      - "Baking soda (TO DO: the number in front of \"teaspoon\" didn't print, maybe ½ teaspoon.)"
      - ¼ teaspoon salt
  - section: Cinnamon sugar
    items:
      - 2 tablespoons sugar
      - 2 teaspoons cinnamon
steps:
  - Heat the oven to 400°F.
  - In a mixing bowl, beat the butter and sugar together until light and fluffy, about 2 minutes.
  - Beat in the eggs and vanilla until smooth.
  - Add the flour, cream of tartar, baking soda and salt, and stir until no dry flour is left.
  - In a small bowl, mix the 2 tablespoons of sugar with the cinnamon.
  - Scoop the dough by rounded teaspoonfuls and roll each one into a ball. Roll the balls in the cinnamon sugar until coated.
  - Set the balls 2 inches apart on ungreased baking sheets.
  - Bake for 8 to 10 minutes, until the edges are set. The centers will still look soft.
  - Let the cookies sit on the pan for a minute or two, then move them to a rack to cool.
