---
title: Date Protein Bars
description: Chewy oat bars sweetened with simmered dates and packed with chia, flax, coconut and peanut butter, with chocolate chips mixed in at the end.
category: Snacks
prepMinutes: 25
cookMinutes: 20
restMinutes: 90
restLabel: Cool
servings: 1 large pan of bars
date: 2026-11-06
tags: [make-ahead, snack, lunchbox]
ingredients:
  - 1 lb dates, pitted
  - 1 cup water
  - 6 cups oats
  - 1 cup coconut
  - 1 cup chocolate chips
  - ½ cup chia seeds
  - ⅔ cup flaxseed
  - 2 tablespoons cinnamon
  - 1 teaspoon salt
  - ⅔ cup coconut oil, softened
  - ⅔ cup peanut butter
  - 2 tablespoons vanilla
  - 1 cup corn syrup
steps:
  - Put the pitted dates and water in a saucepan over medium heat. When it boils, cover the pan, turn the heat to low and simmer until the dates are very soft, about 10 minutes. Let them cool for a few minutes.
  - Heat the oven to 325°F. Line a large rimmed baking sheet (about 13 × 18 inches) with parchment paper.
  - In the stand mixer, beat the softened dates and any water left in the pan with the coconut oil, peanut butter, corn syrup and vanilla until the dates break down into a thick paste.
  - Add the oats, coconut, chia seeds, flaxseed, cinnamon and salt. Mix on low until everything is evenly coated.
  - Mix in the chocolate chips last, just until they're spread through.
  - Press the mixture firmly and evenly into the pan. Pressing hard is what helps the bars hold together.
  - Bake for 20 minutes.
  - Let the pan cool to room temperature, then put it in the fridge for 1 hour so the bars set. Cut into bars.
tips:
  - "Honey version: leave out the dates, water and corn syrup, and use ½ cup honey and ½ cup agave instead. Skip the first step and mix the honey and agave in with the peanut butter."
  - Store the bars in a covered container in the fridge.
