---
title: Fruit Smoothie
description: A big blender batch of frozen fruit, orange juice, yogurt, avocado and honey, weighed right into the blender jar.
category: Drinks
prepMinutes: 10
cookMinutes: 0
servings: About 6 cups
date: 2026-11-07
tags: [blender, no-cook, kid-friendly]
ingredients:
  - 300 g frozen fruit (about 2 cups)
  - 400 g orange juice (about 1⅔ cups)
  - 8 oz water (1 cup)
  - 300 g yogurt (about 1¼ cups)
  - 1 avocado
  - 85 g honey (¼ cup)
steps:
  - Set the blender jar on a kitchen scale and zero it. Pour in the orange juice and water first so the blades move easily.
  - Zero the scale again and add the yogurt, then the honey.
  - Cut the avocado in half, take out the pit and scoop the flesh into the jar.
  - Add the frozen fruit last, right on top.
  - Blend on low for a few seconds to break up the fruit, then on high until completely smooth, about 1 minute. If it's too thick to move, add a splash more water.
  - Pour into glasses and serve right away.
