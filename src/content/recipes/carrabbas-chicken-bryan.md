ingredients:
  - section: Chicken
    items:
      - 6 boneless, skinless chicken breasts
      - Kosher salt
      - Freshly cracked black pepper
      - Olive oil, for brushing
      - 12 ounces goat cheese
  - section: Lemon butter sauce
    items:
      - 12 teaspoons (¼ cup) minced onion
      - 12 teaspoons (¼ cup) minced garlic
      - 24 tablespoons (1½ cups, or 3 sticks) butter, divided
      - 18 tablespoons (1 cup plus 2 tablespoons) white wine
      - 1½ cups fresh lemon juice
      - 18 sun-dried tomatoes, chopped
      - 6 to 12 tablespoons chopped fresh basil (about ⅓ to ¾ cup)
steps:
  - Brush the chicken on both sides with olive oil and season with salt and cracked pepper.
  - Grill the chicken until it reaches 165°F inside. Make the sauce while it grills.
  - Melt 6 tablespoons of the butter in a saucepan over medium heat. Cook the onion and garlic for 2 to 3 minutes, until soft and fragrant.
  - Pour in the wine and lemon juice, turn the heat down to medium-low and simmer for about 10 minutes, until it cooks down a bit.
  - Add the other 18 tablespoons of butter a few pieces at a time, stirring until each addition melts before adding more, so the sauce turns smooth and creamy.
  - Stir in the sun-dried tomatoes and basil and heat just until warm. Don't let it boil, or the butter sauce can separate.
  - When the chicken is almost done, top each breast with 2 ounces of goat cheese and let it warm on the grill until soft.
  - Set the chicken on plates and spoon the lemon butter sauce over the top.
tips:
  - Matt triples this recipe. The original is written for 2 chicken breasts, so every amount here is three times that, and the 12 ounces of goat cheese covers 6 breasts at 2 ounces each.
