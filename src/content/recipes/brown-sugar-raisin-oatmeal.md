---
title: Brown Sugar Raisin Oatmeal
description: Stovetop oats simmered for five minutes, sweetened with brown sugar and cinnamon, and finished with raisins.
category: Breakfast
prepMinutes: 5
cookMinutes: 10
restMinutes: 5
restLabel: Stand
servings: 4 servings
date: 2026-11-07
tags: [stovetop, kid-friendly, vegetarian]
ingredients:
  - 3½ cups water
  - 2 cups old-fashioned rolled oats
  - ½ teaspoon salt
  - ½ teaspoon cinnamon
  - 6 tablespoons brown sugar (¼ cup plus 2 tablespoons)
  - ½ cup raisins
steps:
  - Bring the water to a boil in a medium saucepan over high heat.
  - Turn the heat down to low and stir in the oats. Let them simmer for 5 minutes, stirring now and then so they don't stick to the bottom.
  - Stir in the salt, brown sugar and cinnamon.
  - Take the pan off the heat and let it stand for 5 minutes. The oatmeal thickens as it sits.
  - Stir in the raisins and serve.
