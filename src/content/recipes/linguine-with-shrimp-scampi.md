title: Linguine with Shrimp Scampi
description: Shrimp cooked quickly in garlic butter and olive oil, finished with lemon, parsley and a pinch of red pepper, and tossed with linguine.

ingredients:
  - Vegetable oil, for the pasta water
  - Kosher salt (1 tablespoon for the pasta water and 1½ teaspoons for the shrimp)
  - ¾ pound linguine
  - 3 tablespoons unsalted butter
  - 2½ tablespoons good olive oil
  - 1½ tablespoons minced garlic (about 4 cloves)
  - 1 pound large shrimp (about 16), peeled and deveined
  - ¼ teaspoon freshly ground black pepper
  - ⅓ cup chopped fresh parsley
  - Grated zest of ½ lemon
  - ¼ cup fresh lemon juice (about 2 lemons)
  - ¼ lemon, sliced thin into half-moons
  - ⅛ teaspoon red pepper flakes
steps:
  - Bring a large pot of water to a boil. Add a drizzle of vegetable oil, 1 tablespoon kosher salt and the linguine, and cook for 7 to 10 minutes, or as the package says, until tender with a little bite.
  - While the pasta cooks, melt the butter with the olive oil in a large (12-inch) heavy skillet over medium-low heat. Add the garlic and cook for 1 minute, stirring. Don't let it brown.
  - Add the shrimp, 1½ teaspoons kosher salt and the pepper. Cook, stirring often, just until the shrimp turn pink, about 5 minutes.
  - Take the pan off the heat and stir in the parsley, lemon zest, lemon juice, lemon slices and red pepper flakes.
  - Drain the linguine and put it back in the pot. Add the shrimp and all the sauce right away, toss well and serve.
tips:
  - Keep the heat low when the garlic goes in. Burnt garlic turns bitter and you'll have to start over.
  - Shrimp keep cooking after you take the pan off the heat, so pull them as soon as they turn pink.
