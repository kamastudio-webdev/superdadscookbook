---
title: Burger Pockets
description: Soft homemade bun dough wrapped around seasoned ground beef, onion and two kinds of cheese, then baked until golden. Dip them in special sauce. They freeze well for lunches.
category: Dinner
prepMinutes: 30
cookMinutes: 30
servings: 10 to 12 pockets
date: 2026-11-19
tags: [ground-beef, kid-friendly, freezer-friendly, make-ahead]
uses:
  - recipe: simple-burger-buns
    note: Make the dough through its 1-hour rise. Start it before the filling.
ingredients:
  - section: Filling
    items:
      - 1½ pounds ground beef
      - 1 onion, chopped
      - 4 to 6 garlic cloves, minced
      - 1 tablespoon steak seasoning
      - 2 teaspoons liquid smoke
      - 1 pint (16 ounces) salsa verde (optional)
  - section: To fill the pockets
    items:
      - 1 batch Simple Burger Buns dough, risen
      - 10 to 12 slices sharp cheddar cheese
      - 10 to 12 slices pepper jack cheese
  - section: Special sauce for dipping
    items:
      - 1 cup mayonnaise
      - ½ cup chili sauce
      - ½ teaspoon paprika
steps:
  - Make the Simple Burger Buns dough first. While it rises, make the filling.
  - In a large skillet over medium-high heat, cook the ground beef with the onion, garlic, steak seasoning and liquid smoke. Break up the meat as it cooks, until it's browned all the way through. Drain off extra grease if there's a lot.
  - For a tangy version, stir in the salsa verde and let the beef cook in it for a few minutes. Set the filling aside to cool a little while you roll out the dough.
  - Heat the oven to 400°F and line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
  - Divide the risen dough into 10 to 12 pieces and roll each one out into a circle.
  - Lay a slice of cheddar in the middle of each circle. Top it with a scoop of the beef (about ⅓ cup), then a slice of pepper jack.
  - Bring the edges of the dough up over the filling and pinch them together to seal. Set each pocket on the baking sheet with the pinched side down.
  - Bake for 18 to 25 minutes, until the tops are golden brown.
  - While they bake, stir the mayonnaise, chili sauce and paprika together for the special sauce. Serve the pockets warm with the sauce for dipping.
tips:
  - "For freezer lunches: let the baked pockets cool for about 15 minutes, then put them in sandwich bags and freeze. They keep for months. Reheat in the microwave or a 350°F oven until hot in the middle."
  - The special sauce is also really good with sweet potato fries.
  - "Breakfast pockets: fill them with scrambled eggs, cooked breakfast sausage or bacon, onion, garlic, bell peppers and sharp cheddar. Dip in salsa or hot sauce."
  - "Chicken bacon ranch pockets: fill them with shredded cooked chicken, crumbled crispy bacon, ranch dressing and sharp white cheddar. Dip in extra ranch."
  - These come from the Sunset Highway Farm bread class, where they're called Burger Bombs.
