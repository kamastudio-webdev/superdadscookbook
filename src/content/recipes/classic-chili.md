title: Classic Chili
description: A big pot of stovetop chili with ground beef, kidney beans and a homemade chili seasoning blend. This batch makes 12 servings.

  name: The Wholesome Dish

ingredients:
  - 2 tablespoons olive oil
  - 2 medium yellow onions, diced
  - 2 pounds 90% lean ground beef
  - 5 tablespoons chili powder
  - 4 tablespoons ground cumin (¼ cup)
  - 4 tablespoons granulated sugar (¼ cup)
  - 4 tablespoons tomato paste (¼ cup)
  - 2 tablespoons garlic powder
  - 3 teaspoons salt (1 tablespoon)
  - 1 teaspoon ground black pepper
  - ½ teaspoon ground cayenne pepper (optional)
  - 3 cups beef broth
  - 2 cans (15 ounces each) petite diced tomatoes, with their juice
  - 2 cans (16 ounces each) red kidney beans, drained and rinsed
  - 2 cans (8 ounces each) tomato sauce
steps:
  - Pour the olive oil into a large soup pot and heat it over medium-high for 2 minutes. Add the onions and cook for 5 minutes, stirring now and then, until they soften.
  - Add the ground beef and break it up with a wooden spoon. Cook for 6 to 7 minutes, stirring now and then, until it's browned.
  - Stir in the chili powder, cumin, sugar, tomato paste, garlic powder, salt, pepper and cayenne (if using) until the beef is evenly coated.
  - Add the broth, the diced tomatoes with their juice, the drained beans and the tomato sauce, and stir well.
  - Bring the chili to a low boil, then turn the heat down to low or medium-low. Let it simmer uncovered for 20 to 25 minutes, stirring once in a while.
  - Take the pot off the heat and let the chili rest for 5 to 10 minutes before serving.
tips:
  - Matt's copy is doubled. For 6 servings, use half of everything.
  - Leave out the cayenne for a milder pot.
  - Use a big pot. This doubled batch needs one that holds at least 6 quarts.
