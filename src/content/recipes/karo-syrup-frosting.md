title: Karo Syrup Frosting
description: A fluffy, glossy white frosting whipped from egg whites, sugar and light corn syrup. No cooking and no powdered sugar needed.
category: Basics
prepMinutes: 10
cookMinutes: 0
servings: 1 batch (frosts a 9 x 13-inch cake or a 9-inch layer cake)

ingredients:
  - 2 egg whites, at room temperature
  - ¼ teaspoon cream of tartar
  - ¼ teaspoon salt
  - ¼ cup sugar
  - ¾ cup light corn syrup (like Karo)
  - 1½ teaspoons vanilla
  - Food coloring (optional)
steps:
  - Put the egg whites, cream of tartar and salt in a small, clean mixing bowl. Beat on high speed until soft peaks form, meaning the tips curl over when you lift the beaters.
  - Keep beating and add the sugar a spoonful at a time, until the whites look smooth and shiny.
  - Slowly pour in the corn syrup and the vanilla while you beat. Keep going until the frosting is thick and holds stiff peaks that stand straight up.
  - Beat in a few drops of food coloring if you'd like a color.
  - Spread it on a completely cooled cake.
tips:
  - Any trace of yolk or grease in the bowl will stop the whites from whipping, so use a very clean bowl and beaters.
  - The egg whites aren't cooked. If you're serving young kids, older folks or anyone pregnant, use pasteurized egg whites from a carton.
