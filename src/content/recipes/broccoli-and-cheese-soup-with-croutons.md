title: Broccoli and Cheese Soup with Croutons
description: A smooth, creamy broccoli soup thickened with a little flour, finished with cheddar and butter, and topped with crunchy seasoned croutons.
category: Dinner
prepMinutes: 10
cookMinutes: 35
servings: 4 servings
date: 2026-11-08
tags: [soup, comfort-food, vegetables]
adaptedFrom:
  name: Emeril Lagasse, Food Network
  url: https://www.foodnetwork.com/recipes/broccoli-and-cheese-soup-with-croutons-3644621
ingredients:
  - section: Soup
    items:
      - 3 tablespoons unsalted butter, plus 2 tablespoons cold unsalted butter cut into pieces
      - 1 cup chopped yellow onions or sliced leeks (white parts only, rinsed well)
      - ½ teaspoon salt
      - ¼ teaspoon freshly ground white pepper
      - Pinch of nutmeg
      - ½ teaspoon minced garlic
      - ½ teaspoon chopped fresh thyme leaves
      - 3 tablespoons all-purpose flour
      - 3 cups chicken stock or low-sodium chicken broth
      - 1 (16-ounce) bag frozen broccoli, thawed and broken apart
      - ½ cup heavy cream
      - 1¼ cups shredded medium cheddar
  - section: Croutons
    items:
      - 1 cup French bread cubes (½ to ¾ inch)
      - 2 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil
      - ¼ teaspoon Emeril's Essence or Creole seasoning
steps:
  - "Make the croutons first: heat the oven to 400°F. Toss the bread cubes with the olive oil and seasoning, spread them on a small baking sheet and bake for about 6 minutes, stirring twice, until lightly golden. Let them cool a little."
  - Melt the 3 tablespoons butter in a medium pot over medium-high heat. Add the onions, salt, white pepper and nutmeg and cook, stirring, until soft, about 3 minutes. Add the garlic and thyme and stir for about 20 seconds, until fragrant.
  - Sprinkle in the flour and keep stirring for 2 minutes, until it's blended in and smells toasty.
  - Pour in the chicken stock a little at a time, whisking the whole time, and bring it to a boil. Turn the heat down and simmer until it thickens, about 5 minutes.
  - Add the broccoli and cook, stirring, until tender, about 10 minutes.
  - Take the pot off the heat and blend the soup smooth with an immersion blender. You can also blend it in batches in a regular blender and pour it back into the pot.
  - Stir in the cream and heat until it barely simmers. Turn the heat to low, add the cheddar and stir until it melts, then stir in the 2 tablespoons cold butter.
  - Ladle into bowls, scatter croutons over the top and serve right away.
tips:
  - If you use a regular blender, only fill it about halfway and hold the lid down with a towel. Hot soup can push the lid off.
