title: Chewy Chocolate Chip Cookies
description: Thick, soft-centered chocolate chip cookies made with melted butter, an extra egg yolk and a little cornstarch. Chilling the dough keeps them tall.
category: Dessert
prepMinutes: 15
cookMinutes: 14
restMinutes: 120
restLabel: Chill
servings: 20 cookies

tags: [cookies, baking, make-ahead]

  name: Sally's Baking Addiction

ingredients:
  - 2¼ cups (281 g) all-purpose flour, spooned into the cup and leveled
  - 1 teaspoon baking soda
  - 1½ teaspoons cornstarch
  - ½ teaspoon salt
  - ¾ cup (170 g) unsalted butter (1½ sticks), melted and cooled for 5 minutes
  - ¾ cup (150 g) packed brown sugar
  - ½ cup (100 g) granulated sugar
  - 1 large egg plus 1 egg yolk, at room temperature
  - 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
  - 1¼ cups (225 g) semi-sweet chocolate chips or chunks, plus a few extra for the tops (optional)
steps:
  - In a large bowl, whisk together the flour, baking soda, cornstarch and salt.
  - In a second bowl, stir the cooled melted butter with the brown sugar and granulated sugar until smooth. Add the egg, egg yolk and vanilla and mix until well combined.
  - Pour the butter mixture into the flour mixture and stir until no dry streaks are left. The dough will be thick and soft. Fold in the chocolate chips.
  - Cover the bowl and refrigerate the dough for at least 2 hours, or overnight.
  - Heat the oven to 325°F and line baking sheets with parchment paper or silicone mats.
  - Scoop 2 heaping tablespoons of dough for each cookie and roll it into a tall, rough ball. Set the balls 3 inches apart on the sheets.
  - Bake for 13 to 14 minutes, until the edges are golden but the centers still look soft.
  - Let the cookies cool on the baking sheet for 10 minutes, then move them to a wire rack. While they're still warm, you can press a few extra chocolate chips into the tops.
tips:
  - Rolling the dough into tall balls instead of flat discs helps the cookies bake up thick.
  - Pull them out while the middles look a little underdone. They finish setting as they cool on the pan.
