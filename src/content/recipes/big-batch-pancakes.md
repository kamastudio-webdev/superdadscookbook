---
title: Big-Batch Pancakes
description: A fluffy, lightly sweet pancake batter made with plain milk and melted butter, sized to feed a big family in one go.
category: Breakfast
prepMinutes: 10
cookMinutes: 30
servings: About 30 pancakes
date: 2026-11-07
tags: [weekend, kid-friendly, big-batch, vegetarian]
ingredients:
  - 420 g all-purpose flour (about 3⅓ cups)
  - 4 tablespoons baking powder (¼ cup)
  - 2 teaspoons salt
  - 8 tablespoons sugar (½ cup)
  - 4 eggs, beaten
  - 4 cups milk
  - 8 tablespoons butter, melted (½ cup, or 1 stick), plus more for the pan
steps:
  - In a large bowl, whisk together the flour, baking powder, salt and sugar.
  - In a second bowl or a large measuring pitcher, whisk the beaten eggs with the milk. Let the melted butter cool for a minute, then whisk it in.
  - Pour the milk mixture into the flour mixture and stir just until no dry flour shows. A few lumps are fine; overmixing makes tough pancakes.
  - Heat a griddle or large skillet over medium heat and brush it lightly with butter. It's ready when a drop of water sizzles on it.
  - Pour about ¼ cup of batter for each pancake. Cook until bubbles cover the top and the edges look dry, 2 to 3 minutes, then flip and cook until golden underneath, 1 to 2 minutes more.
  - Keep finished pancakes warm on a baking sheet in a 200°F oven while you cook the rest.
tips:
  - "Smaller crowd? Halve everything: 210 g flour (about 1⅔ cups), 2 tablespoons baking powder, 1 teaspoon salt, 4 tablespoons sugar, 2 eggs, 2 cups milk and 4 tablespoons butter."
  - Leftover pancakes freeze well. Cool them, freeze in a single layer, then bag them and reheat in the toaster.
