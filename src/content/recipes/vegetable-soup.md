---
title: Vegetable Soup
description: A big pot of old-fashioned vegetable soup with carrots, celery, potatoes, turnips, tomatoes and cabbage, started in bacon fat or butter.
category: Dinner
prepMinutes: 30
cookMinutes: 75
servings: 1 large pot (about 6 quarts)
date: 2026-11-08
tags: [soup, vegetables, feeds-a-crowd, make-ahead]
ingredients:
  - 8 tablespoons bacon fat or butter (½ cup)
  - 1 cup diced carrots
  - 2 cups diced onions
  - 2 cups diced celery
  - 12 cups hot water or stock
  - 4 cups canned tomatoes
  - 2 cups peeled, diced potatoes
  - 2 cups peeled, diced turnips
  - 4 tablespoons chopped parsley (¼ cup)
  - 2 teaspoons salt
  - ½ teaspoon pepper
  - 2 cups chopped cabbage
steps:
  - Melt the bacon fat or butter in a big soup pot (8 quarts or larger) over medium heat. Add the carrots, onions and celery and cook, stirring now and then, until the onions are soft and see-through, about 10 minutes.
  - Pour in the hot water or stock and the tomatoes with their juice. Add the potatoes, turnips, parsley, salt and pepper, and bring the pot to a boil.
  - Turn the heat down so the soup bubbles gently, put the lid on and cook for 35 to 45 minutes, until a fork slides easily into the potatoes and turnips.
  - Stir in the cabbage and simmer until it's soft, about 10 to 15 minutes more. Taste and add more salt and pepper if it needs it.
tips:
  - This one comes from the family recipe binder, where it's credited to the Joy of Cooking.
  - Stock makes a richer soup than water. If you use salted store-bought broth, hold back some of the salt and add it at the end to taste.
  - Like most soups, it tastes even better the next day.
