title: Mama Stamberg's Cranberry Relish
description: NPR's famous Thanksgiving relish. Raw cranberries and onion ground with sugar, sour cream and horseradish, then frozen ahead for a sweet, tangy, bright pink side.
category: Sides
prepMinutes: 10
cookMinutes: 0

servings: 1½ pints (about 3 cups)
date: 2026-11-08

adaptedFrom:
  name: NPR
  url: https://www.npr.org/2006/11/23/4176014/mama-stambergs-cranberry-relish-recipe
ingredients:
  - 2 cups raw cranberries, rinsed
  - 1 small onion, peeled
  - ½ cup sugar
  - ¾ cup sour cream
  - 2 tablespoons prepared horseradish
steps:
  - Rinse the cranberries and pick out any soft ones. Cut the onion into chunks.
  - Grind the cranberries and onion together until finely chopped. A meat grinder works, or pulse them in a food processor until the pieces are small but not puréed.
  - Scrape the mixture into a bowl and stir in the sugar, sour cream and horseradish until it's evenly mixed and bright pink.
  - Spoon it into a plastic container, put the lid on and freeze it. You can do this days ahead.
  - On Thanksgiving morning, move the container to the fridge to thaw. Serve it cold; it's fine if it's still a little icy.
tips:
  - Make it the week before the holiday. It's one less thing to do on the big day.
