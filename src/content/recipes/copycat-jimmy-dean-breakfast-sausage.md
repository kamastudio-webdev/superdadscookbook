title: Copycat Jimmy Dean Breakfast Sausage
description: Season your own breakfast sausage at home with your pick of four blends (sage, hot, maple or original). This batch makes 3 pounds.
category: Breakfast
prepMinutes: 10
cookMinutes: 10
restMinutes: 60
restLabel: Chill
servings: 12 servings (3 pounds)
date: 2026-11-07
tags: [pork, make-ahead, copycat]
adaptedFrom:
  name: Budget101
  url: https://www.budget101.com/recipes/3886-copycat-jimmy-dean-breakfast-sausage/
ingredients:
  - section: Meat
    items:
      - 3 pounds (48 ounces) ground pork, or ground turkey
  - section: Sage seasoning (option 1)
    items:
      - 1 tablespoon salt (3 teaspoons)
      - ¾ teaspoon garlic salt
      - 1½ teaspoons dried parsley
      - 2¼ teaspoons ground sage
      - ¾ teaspoon coarse ground black pepper
      - ¾ teaspoon dried thyme
      - ¾ teaspoon crushed red pepper flakes
      - ¾ teaspoon ground coriander
      - ¾ teaspoon MSG (optional)
      - ⅜ teaspoon oregano (¼ teaspoon plus ⅛ teaspoon)
  - section: Hot seasoning (option 2)
    items:
      - 1 tablespoon salt (3 teaspoons)
      - 1½ teaspoons cayenne pepper, or more if you like it hotter
      - ¾ teaspoon rubbed sage
      - ¾ teaspoon coarse ground black pepper
      - ¾ teaspoon crushed red pepper flakes
      - ¾ teaspoon coriander
      - ¾ teaspoon MSG (optional)
  - section: Maple seasoning (option 3)
    items:
      - 9 tablespoons maple syrup (½ cup plus 1 tablespoon)
      - 1 tablespoon salt (3 teaspoons)
      - 1½ teaspoons MSG (optional)
      - ¾ teaspoon coriander
      - ¾ teaspoon black pepper
  - section: Original seasoning (option 4)
    items:
      - 2¼ teaspoons salt
      - 1½ teaspoons dried parsley
      - ¾ teaspoon ground sage
      - ¾ teaspoon ground black pepper
      - ¾ teaspoon dried thyme
      - ¾ teaspoon crushed red pepper
      - ¾ teaspoon ground coriander
      - 1½ teaspoons MSG, such as Accent
steps:
  - Choose one of the four seasoning options. Stir its seasonings together in a small bowl first so they spread evenly through the meat.
  - Put the ground pork in a large bowl (or the stand mixer bowl with the paddle). Add the seasonings, plus the maple syrup if you're making the maple flavor, and mix with your hands or on low speed until everything is evenly worked in.
  - If you can, cover the bowl and refrigerate it for at least 1 hour so the flavors blend.
  - Shape the sausage into patties. Fry them in a skillet over medium heat for about 4 to 5 minutes per side, until browned and cooked through (160°F inside).
tips:
  - Fry a small spoonful first and taste it, then adjust the salt or heat before you shape the rest.
  - Ground turkey works in place of the pork. Cook turkey sausage to 165°F.
  - The source recipe is written for 1 pound of pork, and Matt's copy is tripled. For 1 pound, use a third of each seasoning.
