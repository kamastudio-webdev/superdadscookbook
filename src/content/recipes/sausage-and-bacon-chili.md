title: Sausage and Bacon Chili
description: Matt's take on the Neelys' famous chili, with bacon, two pounds of ground sausage, a double helping of black and kidney beans, beer and smoky spices.
category: Dinner
cuisine: Tex-Mex
prepMinutes: 15
cookMinutes: 120
servings: 6 to 8 servings
date: 2026-11-08
tags: [chili, one-pot, feeds-a-crowd]
adaptedFrom:
  name: Patrick and Gina Neely, Food Network
  url: https://www.foodnetwork.com/recipes/patrick-and-gina-neely/pats-famous-beef-and-pork-chili-recipe-1949426
ingredients:
  - 6 slices thick-cut applewood smoked bacon, cut into ½-inch pieces
  - 4 garlic cloves, finely chopped
  - 2 medium onions, finely chopped
  - 1 red bell pepper, chopped
  - 1 yellow bell pepper, chopped
  - 3 tablespoons chili powder
  - 1 tablespoon ground cumin
  - 1 tablespoon chipotle chili powder
  - 2 teaspoons dried oregano
  - 1 tablespoon smoked paprika
  - 1 teaspoon salt
  - Freshly ground black pepper
  - 2 pounds ground sausage
  - 1 cup beer (the original suggests Budweiser)
  - 2 (15-ounce) cans black beans, drained and rinsed
  - 2 (15-ounce) cans kidney beans, drained and rinsed
  - 1 (24-ounce) can crushed tomatoes
  - 1 (24-ounce) can diced tomatoes, with their juice
  - Lime wedges, for serving
steps:
  - Cook the bacon in a large, heavy Dutch oven over medium heat, stirring now and then, until it's lightly crisp.
  - Add the garlic, onions and both bell peppers, then the chili powder, cumin, chipotle powder, oregano, smoked paprika, salt and some black pepper. Cook, stirring, until the vegetables are soft and the spices smell toasty, about 5 to 7 minutes.
  - Add the sausage and break it up with a wooden spoon. Cook until no pink is left.
  - Pour in the beer and stir, scraping up anything stuck to the bottom of the pot. Add the black beans, kidney beans, crushed tomatoes and diced tomatoes with their juice.
  - Turn the heat to low and simmer for 1½ hours, stirring every so often.
  - Taste and add more salt and pepper if it needs it. Ladle into bowls and serve with lime wedges for squeezing over the top.
tips:
  - "Matt's changes to the original: no ground beef, 2 pounds of ground sausage instead of 1 pound of ground pork, double the black beans and kidney beans, and 1 teaspoon of salt."
  - If the sausage gives off a lot of fat, spoon some off before you add the beer.
