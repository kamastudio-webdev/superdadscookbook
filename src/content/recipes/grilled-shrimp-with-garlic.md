title: Grilled Shrimp with Garlic (Gambas al Ajillo)
description: Skewered shrimp brushed with an ancho-thyme-garlic marinade, grilled hot and fast, then finished with garlic oil and crispy garlic chips.

prepMinutes: 20
cookMinutes: 10
servings: 4 servings

ingredients:
  - ¾ cup olive oil, divided
  - 3 tablespoons chopped fresh thyme leaves, divided
  - 1½ tablespoons ancho chili powder
  - 12 garlic cloves, coarsely chopped
  - 24 large shrimp, peeled and deveined
  - Salt and freshly ground black pepper
  - 6 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
  - Fresh oregano leaves, for garnish
  - Wooden skewers, soaked in water
steps:
  - Heat the grill to medium.
  - In a small bowl, whisk ¼ cup of the olive oil with 2 tablespoons of the thyme, the ancho powder and the chopped garlic. Thread the shrimp onto the skewers and brush them with this marinade.
  - "Make the garlic chips: pour the remaining ½ cup oil into a small saucepan, add the sliced garlic and cook over medium-low heat until the slices turn light golden. Scoop them out with a slotted spoon onto a paper towel-lined plate. Save the oil."
  - Turn the grill up to high. Season the shrimp with salt and pepper and grill until golden brown on each side, about 1½ minutes per side.
  - Slide the shrimp off the skewers onto a platter. Drizzle with some of the garlic oil, scatter the garlic chips and the last tablespoon of thyme over the top, and garnish with oregano leaves.
tips:
  - Matt doubles the garlic. His note on the printout says to add nine more cloves, so this version uses 18 instead of the original 9.
  - Watch the garlic chips closely. They go from golden to bitter quickly, so pull them as soon as they're light golden.
