title: Old-Fashioned Fantasy Fudge
description: The classic chocolate fudge made with marshmallow cream and walnuts. One batch fills a 9x13-inch pan with about 3 pounds of fudge, and the tips have the amounts for a triple batch.

ingredients:
  - 3 cups white sugar
  - ¾ cup margarine
  - ⅔ cup evaporated milk
  - 1 bag (12 ounces) semisweet chocolate chips
  - 1 jar (7 ounces) marshmallow cream
  - 1 cup chopped walnuts
  - 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
steps:
  - Grease a 9x13-inch pan.
  - In a large, heavy saucepan over medium heat, stir the sugar, margarine and evaporated milk until the sugar dissolves.
  - Bring it to a full rolling boil and boil for 5 minutes, stirring the whole time so it doesn't scorch.
  - Take the pan off the heat. Stir in the chocolate chips until they melt and the mixture is smooth, then stir in the marshmallow cream. Mix in the walnuts and vanilla.
  - Spread the fudge into the pan and smooth the top. Let it cool for at least 1 hour, then cut it into squares.
tips:
  - "Triple batch: 9 cups sugar, 2¼ cups margarine, 2 cups evaporated milk, 36 ounces chocolate chips, 21 ounces marshmallow cream, 3 cups walnuts and 3 teaspoons vanilla. Use a very large, heavy pot and three 9x13-inch pans."
  - Don't cut the 5-minute boil short. That's what makes the fudge set up firm.
