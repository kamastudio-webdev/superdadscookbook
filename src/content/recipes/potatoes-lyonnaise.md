---
title: Potatoes Lyonnaise
description: Sliced boiled potatoes fried golden in butter and tossed with browned onions and fresh parsley. An easy side for a roast.
category: Sides
cuisine: French
prepMinutes: 10
cookMinutes: 25
servings: About 4 servings
date: 2026-11-08
tags: [side-dish, potatoes, leftovers]
ingredients:
  - 3 cups sliced boiled potatoes
  - Butter, for the pan
  - 1 small onion, peeled and minced
  - Finely chopped parsley, for sprinkling
steps:
  - Melt the butter in a large skillet over medium heat. Lay in the potato slices and fry until golden brown on the bottom, then turn them and brown the other side.
  - Lift the potatoes out onto a plate and leave the butter in the pan.
  - Add the onion to the butter and cook, stirring, until golden brown.
  - Put the potatoes back in the pan, toss them with the onion and cook until they're hot all the way through.
  - Sprinkle with parsley and serve.
tips:
  - Leftover boiled potatoes are perfect for this.
  - From the family recipe binder, where it's paired with Braised Pork Loin in Chianti.
