---
title: Chicken Breast Gismondo
description: Parmesan-crusted chicken breasts sautéed in butter, served on a bed of buttery lemon-nutmeg spinach and topped with sautéed mushrooms.
category: Dinner
prepMinutes: 20
cookMinutes: 30
servings: 4 servings
date: 2026-11-08
tags: [chicken, spinach, family-recipe]
ingredients:
  - 2 large whole chicken breasts (4 halves)
  - Flour, seasoned with salt and pepper
  - 2 eggs, beaten
  - 1 tablespoon water
  - ¼ cup grated Parmesan
  - ½ cup bread crumbs
  - ¾ cup butter, divided (½ cup for the chicken, 2 tablespoons for the mushrooms, 2 tablespoons for the spinach)
  - 1½ lb spinach
  - ½ lb mushrooms, sliced
  - 1 tablespoon lemon juice
  - ¼ teaspoon nutmeg
  - Salt
steps:
  - Set out three shallow dishes. Put the seasoned flour in the first. Whisk the eggs and water together in the second. Mix the Parmesan and bread crumbs in the third.
  - Coat each piece of chicken in the flour, dip it in the egg, then press it into the Parmesan crumbs, patting so the coating sticks.
  - Melt ½ cup of the butter in a large skillet over medium heat. Cook the chicken, turning once, until golden brown on both sides and cooked through (165°F inside), about 6 to 8 minutes per side. Move it to a warm plate.
  - While the chicken cooks, rinse the spinach well. Put it in a large pot with just the water still clinging to the leaves (don't add water or salt), cover tightly and cook briefly, stirring now and then, until it's just wilted and still bright green, 2 to 4 minutes.
  - Melt 2 tablespoons of the butter in the skillet and cook the mushrooms until browned and tender, about 5 minutes.
  - Drain the spinach well and toss it with the last 2 tablespoons of butter, the lemon juice, nutmeg and a pinch of salt.
  - Pile the spinach on a warmed platter, lay the chicken on top and pour the mushrooms over it.
