---
title: Alfredo Sauce
description: A rich, garlicky homemade Alfredo made with milk and cream, thickened with a little flour and finished with Parmesan and Romano.
category: Basics
cuisine: Italian
prepMinutes: 5
cookMinutes: 15
servings: About 3½ cups of sauce
date: 2026-11-07
tags: [sauce, pasta, vegetarian]
ingredients:
  - 3 oz butter (6 tablespoons)
  - 1 tablespoon minced garlic
  - 2 tablespoons flour
  - 1½ cups milk
  - 1½ cups heavy cream
  - ½ cup Parmesan cheese
  - ½ cup Romano cheese
  - Salt and pepper, to taste
steps:
  - Melt the butter in a medium saucepan over medium heat. Add the garlic and sauté for 1 minute, stirring, until it smells good. Don't let it brown.
  - Sprinkle in the flour and whisk for about 1 minute, until it's smooth and bubbling.
  - Slowly pour in the milk while whisking, then whisk in the cream. Keep whisking and cook until the sauce bubbles gently and thickens enough to coat the back of a spoon, about 5 minutes.
  - Turn the heat to low. Add the Parmesan and Romano a handful at a time, stirring until each addition melts and the sauce is smooth.
  - Season with salt and pepper and serve right away over hot pasta.
tips:
  - That's plenty of sauce for a 1-pound box of fettuccine.
  - Freshly grated cheese melts smoother than the shaker kind.
  - If the sauce gets too thick as it sits, stir in a splash of milk.
