---
title: Homemade Bread
description: A soft, everyday white loaf made start to finish in the bread machine, with powdered milk and a little lard for a tender crumb.
category: Breads
prepMinutes: 10
cookMinutes: 180
restMinutes: 30
restLabel: Cool
servings: 1 loaf (1½ lb)
date: 2026-11-06
tags: [baking, bread-machine]
ingredients:
  - 255 ml water (about 1 cup plus 1 tablespoon)
  - 3 tablespoons sugar
  - 2 tablespoons powdered milk
  - ½ tablespoon salt (1½ teaspoons)
  - 35 g lard (about 2½ tablespoons)
  - 416 g flour (about 3⅓ cups)
  - ½ tablespoon yeast (1½ teaspoons)
steps:
  - Pour the water into the bread machine pan first, then add the sugar, powdered milk, salt and lard.
  - Spoon the flour on top so it covers the water completely.
  - Make a small well in the top of the flour and put the yeast in it, so the yeast stays away from the water and salt until the machine starts.
  - Lock the pan into the machine, choose the Basic or White bread setting, the 1½ lb loaf size and a medium crust, then press Start. Most machines take about 3 hours.
  - When the machine beeps, lift the pan out right away with oven mitts and turn the loaf out onto a cooling rack. If the kneading paddle is stuck in the bottom, pull it out.
  - Let the loaf cool for at least 30 minutes before slicing so the inside finishes setting.
tips:
  - Every machine is a little different. If your manual lists the ingredients in a different order, follow the manual.
  - Weighing the flour on a kitchen scale gives the most even loaf. If you use cups, spoon the flour into the cup and level it off instead of scooping.
