ingredients:
  - section: Roast pork
    items:
      - 1 pound pork tenderloin
      - 2 tablespoons orange juice
      - 2 tablespoons olive oil
      - 1 tablespoon lime juice
      - 1 tablespoon light brown sugar
      - 2 teaspoons kosher salt
      - ½ teaspoon freshly ground black pepper
      - ½ teaspoon ground cumin
      - ½ teaspoon smoked paprika
      - 2 garlic cloves, smashed
  - section: Sandwiches
    items:
      - 4 Cuban sandwich loaves (8 to 10 inches long), split in half lengthwise
      - 1 stick (½ cup) unsalted butter, softened
      - 1 cup yellow mustard
      - 1 pound sliced honey-glazed ham
      - 4 large dill pickles, thinly sliced crosswise
      - The sliced roast pork
      - 8 ounces sliced Swiss cheese (16 slices)
      - Kosher salt
steps:
  - Heat the oven to 450°F and line a sheet pan with foil.
  - Put everything for the roast pork except the tenderloin into a mini food processor and blend until smooth.
  - Put the tenderloin in a medium bowl, pour the marinade over it and turn it to coat. Cover with plastic wrap and leave it on the counter for 30 minutes.
  - Set the pork on the sheet pan and pour the marinade over it. Roast for 20 to 25 minutes, until an instant-read thermometer in the center reads 140°F.
  - While the pork roasts, get the bread ready. Spread 1 tablespoon of softened butter over the crust side of each top and bottom piece, then lay them butter-side down on a cutting board. Spread 2 tablespoons of mustard over the cut side of each piece.
  - Move the pork to a cutting board and let it rest for 5 minutes. Slice it on an angle about ¼ inch thick.
  - Build each sandwich on a bottom half with a quarter of the ham, then a quarter of the pickles, the roast pork and the cheese. Add a pinch of salt and close it with the top half.
  - Heat the panini press. Working a couple at a time, press the sandwiches for 5 to 6 minutes, until the bread is golden brown and the cheese has melted.
  - Cut each sandwich in half on the diagonal and serve.
tips:
  - No panini press? Cook the sandwiches in a large skillet over medium heat with a heavy pan set on top, flipping once, until both sides are golden and the cheese melts.
  - The pork keeps cooking a few degrees while it rests. The USDA recommends cooking pork to 145°F.
