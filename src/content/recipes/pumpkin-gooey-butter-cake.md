title: Pumpkin Gooey Butter Cake
description: A buttery cake-mix crust under a soft, spiced pumpkin and cream cheese layer that stays a little gooey in the middle. Serve squares with whipped cream.
category: Dessert
prepMinutes: 20
cookMinutes: 50
restMinutes: 30
restLabel: Cool
servings: 6 to 8 servings
date: 2026-11-08
tags: [pumpkin, fall, thanksgiving, cake-mix]
adaptedFrom:
  name: Paula Deen
  url: https://pauladeen.com/pumpkin-gooey-butter-cake
ingredients:
  - section: Crust
    items:
      - 1 box (18¼ ounces) yellow cake mix
      - 1 egg
      - 8 tablespoons butter (1 stick), melted
  - section: Pumpkin filling
    items:
      - 1 package (8 ounces) cream cheese, softened
      - 1 can (15 ounces) pumpkin
      - 3 eggs
      - 1 teaspoon vanilla
      - 8 tablespoons butter (1 stick), melted
      - 1 box (16 ounces) powdered sugar
      - 1 teaspoon cinnamon
      - 1 teaspoon nutmeg
  - section: For serving
    items:
      - Whipped cream
steps:
  - Heat the oven to 350°F and lightly grease a 9 x 13-inch baking pan.
  - For the crust, beat the cake mix, egg and melted butter with an electric mixer until it comes together into a thick, soft dough. Press it in an even layer over the bottom of the pan.
  - For the filling, beat the cream cheese and pumpkin in a large bowl until smooth. Add the eggs, vanilla and melted butter and beat until blended.
  - Add the powdered sugar, cinnamon and nutmeg and beat on low, then medium, until the filling is smooth.
  - Spread the filling over the crust.
  - Bake for 40 to 50 minutes, until the edges are golden and set but the center still wobbles a little. Don't overbake. The middle is meant to stay gooey.
  - Let it cool for at least 30 minutes, then cut into squares and top each one with whipped cream.
tips:
  - Most cake mix boxes today are about 15¼ ounces instead of 18¼. Use a standard box.
  - "Pineapple version: skip the pumpkin and beat a drained 20-ounce can of crushed pineapple into the cream cheese filling."
  - "Banana version: use 2 ripe bananas in place of the pumpkin."
  - "Peanut butter version: use chocolate cake mix for the crust, and beat 1 cup creamy peanut butter into the filling instead of the pumpkin."
