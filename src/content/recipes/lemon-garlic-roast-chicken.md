title: Lemon and Garlic Roast Chicken
description: Ina Garten's whole roast chicken stuffed with thyme, lemon and garlic, covered with bacon while it roasts, and served with a quick white wine pan gravy.
category: Dinner
prepMinutes: 20
cookMinutes: 100
servings: 3 to 4 servings
date: 2026-11-08

adaptedFrom:
  name: Food Network (Ina Garten)

ingredients:
  - 1 roasting chicken (5 to 6 pounds)
  - Kosher salt
  - Freshly ground black pepper
  - 1 large bunch fresh thyme
  - 4 lemons
  - 3 heads garlic, cut in half crosswise
  - 2 tablespoons butter, melted
  - ½ pound sliced bacon
  - 1 cup white wine
  - ½ cup chicken stock
steps:
  - Heat the oven to 425°F.
  - Pull out the giblets, rinse the chicken inside and out, trim off any extra fat and leftover pinfeathers, and pat the outside dry. Set it in a large roasting pan.
  - Sprinkle the inside of the chicken generously with salt and pepper. Set aside a few thyme sprigs for the platter, then stuff the rest of the bunch into the cavity along with 1 lemon cut in half and 2 of the garlic halves.
  - Brush the outside with the melted butter and season it again with salt and pepper. Tie the legs together with kitchen string and tuck the wing tips under the body.
  - Cut 2 more lemons into quarters and scatter them around the chicken with the rest of the garlic halves. Lay the bacon slices over the chicken so it's covered.
  - Roast for 1 hour. Take the bacon off the top and set it aside, then roast for about 30 minutes more, until the juices run clear when you cut between a leg and thigh (165°F in the thickest part of the thigh).
  - Move the chicken to a platter and cover it loosely with foil while you make the gravy.
  - Spoon off all but 2 tablespoons of fat from the roasting pan. Set the pan on the stove over medium-high heat, pour in the wine and chicken stock and bring it to a boil, scraping up the browned bits. Turn the heat down and simmer for about 5 minutes, until it has cooked down by about half.
  - Slice the chicken and arrange it on the platter with the bacon, roasted garlic and saved thyme sprigs. Slice the last lemon and tuck it around the chicken. Serve with the gravy.
tips:
  - Squeeze the soft roasted garlic out of its skin and spread it on the chicken or on bread.
