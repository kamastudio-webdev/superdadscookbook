---
title: German Pancake
description: A big oven pancake that puffs up tall and golden in a hot buttered pan. Seven eggs, a cup each of flour and milk, and 30 minutes in the oven.
category: Breakfast
prepMinutes: 10
cookMinutes: 30
servings: 6 servings
date: 2026-11-07
tags: [baked, kid-friendly, vegetarian]
ingredients:
  - 7 eggs
  - 1 cup flour
  - 1 cup milk
  - 1 teaspoon salt
  - 1 teaspoon vanilla
  - 3 tablespoons butter
steps:
  - Put the butter in a 9 × 13-inch baking dish and set it in the oven while the oven heats to 375°F. Check it after 5 minutes. You want the butter melted and sizzling, not browned.
  - While the oven heats, put the eggs, milk, flour, salt and vanilla in a blender and blend until smooth and frothy, about 30 seconds. (Or whisk hard in a bowl until no lumps are left.)
  - With oven mitts, take out the hot pan and tilt it so the melted butter covers the bottom. Pour in the batter right away and put the pan back in the oven.
  - Bake for 30 minutes, until puffed up and golden brown around the edges. Keep the oven door closed while it bakes so it rises well.
  - It sinks as it cools, which is normal. Cut it into squares and serve warm.
tips:
  - Good toppings are powdered sugar, syrup, a squeeze of lemon or fresh fruit.
