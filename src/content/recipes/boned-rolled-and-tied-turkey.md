title: Boned, Rolled, and Tied Turkey with Cranberry Compote
description: Chef Todd English's holiday turkey, boned and rolled around a creamy ground turkey and pork stuffing, roasted with a maple glaze and served in neat slices with a spiced cranberry compote.

prepMinutes: 90
cookMinutes: 170
restMinutes: 10
restLabel: Rest
servings: 8 to 10 servings
date: 2026-11-08
tags: [turkey, thanksgiving, holiday, stuffing]
adaptedFrom:
  name: Martha Stewart (Chef Todd English)
  url: https://www.marthastewart.com/318250/boned-rolled-and-tied-turkey
ingredients:
  - section: Turkey and stuffing
    items:
      - 1 turkey, 12 to 14 pounds (a butcher can bone it for you)
      - 3 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil, divided
      - 1 medium onion, coarsely chopped
      - 3 medium garlic cloves, minced
      - 3 tablespoons chopped fresh rosemary, divided, plus 3 large sprigs
      - 1 tablespoon chopped fresh sage
      - 1 tablespoon Dijon mustard
      - 1 pound ground turkey
      - 1 pound ground pork (or more ground turkey or chicken)
      - 1 cup dried bread crumbs
      - 3 large eggs
      - 1 cup heavy cream
      - 3½ teaspoons salt, divided
      - 1¾ teaspoons freshly ground pepper, divided
      - ¼ cup maple syrup
      - Watercress, for the platter
  - section: Cranberry compote
    items:
      - 1 tablespoon extra-virgin olive oil
      - 1 medium red onion, finely chopped
      - 1-inch piece fresh ginger, peeled and finely chopped
      - 2 cups fresh cranberries
      - ½ cup fresh-squeezed orange juice
      - ¼ cup golden raisins
      - ¼ cup brandy
      - 2 tablespoons packed light brown sugar
      - 2 tablespoons balsamic vinegar
      - 1 tablespoon pure maple syrup
      - 1 tablespoon red wine vinegar
      - 2 cups cranberry juice
      - 1 cinnamon stick
      - ½ teaspoon freshly grated nutmeg
      - ¼ teaspoon cayenne pepper
      - ½ teaspoon salt
steps:
  - "Make the compote first, since it needs to cool: heat the oil in a large skillet over medium heat and cook the red onion and ginger for 1 minute. Add the cranberries, orange juice, raisins, brandy, brown sugar, balsamic vinegar, maple syrup and red wine vinegar. Cook until it's syrupy and has cooked down by half, about 10 minutes."
  - Add the cranberry juice, cinnamon stick, nutmeg, cayenne and salt and simmer until thick, 12 to 15 minutes. Take out the cinnamon stick and set the compote aside to cool.
  - "Bone the turkey, or have the butcher do it. Set it breast-side down and cut through the skin along the backbone from neck to tail. Working on one side, cut and peel the meat and skin away from the carcass, keeping the skin in one piece. Cut out the long, curved bone near the wing, and cut through the wing and thigh joints so they come away from the carcass but stay attached to the skin. Keep cutting the breast meat off the bone up to the ridge of the breastbone. Turn the bird around and do the other side, then gently lift the carcass away from the breast meat. Save the carcass for stock."
  - "Finish the wings and legs: cut off the wing tip and middle section (save them for stock), leaving the biggest wing bone. Cut through the tendons around the end of that bone, scrape the meat down it, and pull the bone out. Do the same with each leg bone, cutting it free of the skin, and trim out any tough sinews. Push the boned legs and wings so the skin faces out, then butterfly the thick breast meat so the meat covers the skin in an even layer. Cover with plastic wrap and pound gently until it's an even thickness."
  - "Heat the oven to 375°F. Make the stuffing: heat a large skillet over medium heat, add 1 tablespoon of the olive oil, then the onion, garlic, 1 tablespoon of the chopped rosemary, the sage and the mustard. Cook, stirring now and then, for about 10 minutes, until the onion is soft and clear but not browned. Scrape it into a large bowl and let it cool."
  - Add the ground turkey, ground pork, bread crumbs, eggs, cream, 1½ teaspoons of the salt and ¼ teaspoon of the pepper to the bowl and mix well.
  - Lay the turkey skin-side down and season the meat with 1 teaspoon salt, ½ teaspoon pepper and the remaining 2 tablespoons of chopped rosemary. Spread the stuffing down the middle and roll the turkey up around it into a long log. Hold the seam closed with toothpicks or wooden skewers and tie it with kitchen twine every 2 inches.
  - Drizzle the outside with the remaining 2 tablespoons of olive oil and season with the last 1 teaspoon of salt and ½ teaspoon of pepper. Slide the rosemary sprigs under the twine along the top. Set the roll on a rack over a large roasting pan.
  - Roast until an instant-read thermometer in the center reads 150°F, 2 to 2½ hours. Take it out, brush it all over with the maple syrup and roast about 20 minutes more, until it reads 160°F to 165°F.
  - Let it rest for 10 minutes, then move it to a cutting board, snip off the twine and cut it crosswise into slices ½ to 1 inch thick. Arrange the slices on a platter with watercress and serve with the cranberry compote.
tips:
  - Boning a whole turkey takes time and a sharp boning knife. Ask the butcher to do it when you order the turkey, and you can skip straight to the stuffing.
  - The compote can be made a day or two ahead and kept in the fridge.
