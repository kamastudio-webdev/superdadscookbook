title: Vanilla Cupcakes
description: Soft, fluffy vanilla cupcakes that bake up with even, flat tops, ready for any frosting.

ingredients:
  - 1¼ cups all-purpose flour
  - 1¼ teaspoons baking powder
  - ½ teaspoon salt
  - ½ cup (1 stick) unsalted butter, softened
  - ¾ cup sugar
  - 2 large eggs, at room temperature
  - 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
  - ½ cup buttermilk, at room temperature
steps:
  - Heat the oven to 350°F and line a 12-cup muffin pan with paper liners.
  - Whisk the flour, baking powder and salt together in a bowl.
  - With a mixer on medium-high, beat the butter and sugar for a full 5 minutes, until pale and fluffy. Scrape the bowl now and then.
  - Beat in the eggs one at a time, then the vanilla.
  - Turn the mixer to medium. Add the flour mix in three parts, alternating with the buttermilk, and mix just until smooth.
  - Fill each liner about two-thirds full. Bake 20 to 23 minutes, until a toothpick in the center comes out clean.
  - Cool in the pan for 5 minutes, then move the cupcakes to a rack and let them cool completely before frosting.
tips:
  - No buttermilk? Plain kefir works just as well.
  - The 5 minutes of beating the butter and sugar is what makes these light, so don't rush it.
