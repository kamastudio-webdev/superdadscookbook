title: Jalapeño, Pepper Jack and Bacon Corn Pudding
description: Jiffy corn pudding with a kick. A creamy, custardy corn bake loaded with crisp bacon, fresh jalapeños, green chiles and pepper jack, finished with hot honey and flaky salt. You can mix it the night before.
category: Sides
prepMinutes: 25
cookMinutes: 70
restMinutes: 15
restLabel: Rest
servings: 6 to 8 servings (one 9 × 13-inch pan)
date: 2026-11-26
tags: [corn, thanksgiving, holiday, bacon, make-ahead]
adaptedFrom:
  name: Here's Your Bite
  url: https://heresyourbite.substack.com/p/corn-pudding-the-savory-edition-5a3
ingredients:
  - section: Base
    items:
      - 1 box (8.5 ounces) Jiffy Corn Muffin Mix
      - 1 stick unsalted butter, melted
      - 2 large eggs, beaten
      - 1 can (15 ounces) whole kernel corn, drained
      - 1 can (15 ounces) cream-style corn
      - 1 cup full-fat sour cream (or full-fat Greek yogurt)
      - 1 to 2 tablespoons hot honey
      - ½ teaspoon salt
      - ½ teaspoon black pepper
  - section: Add-ins
    items:
      - 1 pound bacon
      - 2 jalapeños, seeded and finely diced
      - 1 can (4 ounces) mild diced green chiles, drained
      - 1½ to 2 cups shredded pepper jack cheese
  - section: To finish
    items:
      - Hot honey, for drizzling
      - Flaky salt
      - Chopped fresh chives
steps:
  - Cook the bacon until it's truly crisp, drain it on paper towels and crumble it. Set about a quarter of it aside for the top.
  - Heat the oven to 350°F. Grease a 9 × 13-inch baking dish with butter or nonstick spray.
  - In a large bowl, whisk the eggs, sour cream, melted butter and hot honey until smooth. Stir in both cans of corn.
  - Stir in the rest of the bacon, the jalapeños, the green chiles and 1 cup of the pepper jack. Season with the salt and pepper.
  - Gently fold in the corn muffin mix just until there are no dry streaks. Don't overmix.
  - Spread the batter evenly in the dish and scatter the rest of the pepper jack over the top.
  - Bake for about 70 minutes, starting to check at 60. It's done when the edges are set, the top is lightly golden and the middle still jiggles slightly, like a custard, without looking runny. If the top browns too fast, lay a sheet of foil loosely over it for the rest of the bake.
  - Sprinkle with flaky salt and let it rest for 10 to 15 minutes so it can firm up.
  - Top with the saved bacon, the chives and a drizzle of hot honey. Serve warm.
tips:
  - "Make it ahead: mix everything the day before, spread it in the greased dish, cover it and refrigerate overnight. Take it out 30 to 45 minutes before baking, and give it a few extra minutes in the oven if it's still cold in the middle."
  - The creator's newer version of the recipe uses 12 ounces of bacon and 1½ cups of pepper jack instead of 1 pound and up to 2 cups. Either way works.
  - For more heat, add a pinch of cayenne to the batter, a splash of the juice from a jar of pickled jalapeños, or slices of fresh jalapeño on top before baking. For less, swap the green chiles for ¼ cup of chopped pickled jalapeños, which are milder.
  - For a smoky flavor, char the jalapeños in a hot, dry skillet before you chop them.
  - Mixing some sharp cheddar or Monterey Jack in with the pepper jack gives a creamier melt.
  - The add-ins are flexible. Try scallions, or leave out the bacon.
  - It goes with turkey, ham, barbecue or chili, and it's good warm or at room temperature.
  - To reheat, cover with foil and warm at 300°F for 10 to 20 minutes. The foil keeps it from drying out.
