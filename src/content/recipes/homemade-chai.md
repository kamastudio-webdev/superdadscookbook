---
title: Homemade Chai
description: Black tea boiled hard with cinnamon sticks, cloves, cracked cardamom, peppercorns and ginger, then sweetened and strained.
category: Drinks
cuisine: Indian
prepMinutes: 5
cookMinutes: 15
servings: About 2 cups
date: 2026-11-07
tags: [tea, warm-drink, spiced]
ingredients:
  - 3 cups water
  - 3 cinnamon sticks
  - 9 whole cloves
  - 12 cardamom pods, cracked
  - 9 black peppercorns
  - 1½ teaspoons ground ginger
  - 9 tea bags (black tea)
  - ½ cup sugar
  - Milk or cream, to taste
steps:
  - Crack the cardamom pods by pressing each one with the flat side of a knife until it splits open.
  - Put the water, cinnamon sticks, cloves, cardamom, peppercorns, ginger, tea bags and sugar in a medium saucepan and stir.
  - Bring it to a boil over high heat and let it boil for 10 minutes. Stay close, and turn the heat down a little if it starts to foam up the sides.
  - Pour it through a fine-mesh strainer into mugs or a jar, and throw out the spices and tea bags.
  - Stir in milk or cream to taste.
