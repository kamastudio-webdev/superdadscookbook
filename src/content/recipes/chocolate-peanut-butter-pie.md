title: Chocolate Peanut Butter Pie
description: An Oreo cookie crust filled with a fluffy, no-bake peanut butter and cream cheese filling. Very rich, so slice it thin.

cookMinutes: 10
restMinutes: 60
restLabel: Chill
servings: 8 servings

  name: Food Network (Ree Drummond)

ingredients:
  - section: Crust
    items:
      - 25 chocolate sandwich cookies (like Oreos), filling and all
      - 4 tablespoons butter, melted
  - section: Filling
    items:
      - 1 cup creamy peanut butter
      - 1 package (8 ounces) cream cheese, softened
      - 1¼ cups powdered sugar
      - 1 tub (8 ounces) whipped topping (like Cool Whip), thawed
steps:
  - Heat the oven to 350°F.
  - Crush the whole cookies into fine crumbs, either in a food processor or in a zip-top bag with a rolling pin. Pour the melted butter over the crumbs and stir with a fork until all of them look damp.
  - Press the crumbs firmly over the bottom and up the sides of a 9-inch pie pan. Bake for 5 to 7 minutes, until the crust feels set. Let it cool completely, about 20 minutes.
  - While the crust cools, beat the peanut butter and cream cheese with a mixer until smooth. Beat in the powdered sugar. Add the whipped topping and beat until the filling is smooth and fluffy, scraping down the sides of the bowl as you go.
  - Spoon the filling into the cooled crust and smooth the top with a knife or spatula.
  - Refrigerate for at least 1 hour before cutting.
tips:
  - This pie is very rich. Thin slices go a long way.
  - Keep leftovers covered in the fridge.
