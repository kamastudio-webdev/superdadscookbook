ingredients:
  - section: Chicken
    items:
      - 8 chicken breast halves
      - 1 cup pickle juice
      - ½ cup water
      - 1 cup milk
      - 2 large eggs
      - 2 to 3 cups oil, for frying
  - section: Breading
    items:
      - 2 cups all-purpose flour
      - 6 tablespoons powdered sugar
      - 1 teaspoon paprika
      - 2 teaspoons freshly ground black pepper
      - 1 teaspoon chili powder
      - 1 teaspoon salt
      - 1 teaspoon baking powder
      - 2 to 4 teaspoons cayenne pepper (optional, only if you want them spicy)
  - section: Sauce
    items:
      - 1 cup mayonnaise
      - 2 teaspoons Dijon mustard
      - 6 teaspoons (2 tablespoons) yellow mustard
      - 4 teaspoons barbecue sauce (a hickory one is best)
      - 4 tablespoons (¼ cup) honey
      - 1 teaspoon garlic powder
      - 1 teaspoon paprika
      - 2 teaspoons lemon juice
  - section: To serve
    items:
      - 8 hamburger buns
      - Pickle slices, lettuce, tomato and cheese slices
steps:
  - Pour the pickle juice and water into a gallon zip-top bag. Add the chicken, seal the bag and let it soak in the fridge for 30 minutes.
  - While the chicken soaks, stir all of the sauce ingredients together in a bowl until smooth. Cover and keep it in the fridge until you're ready to build the sandwiches.
  - In a wide, shallow bowl, whisk together the flour, powdered sugar, paprika, black pepper, chili powder, salt and baking powder. Add the cayenne now if you're using it. In a second bowl, beat the milk and eggs together.
  - Pour 2 to 3 cups of oil into a large, heavy saucepan and heat it over medium-high to 350°F.
  - Lift a piece of chicken out of the pickle brine, dip it in the egg mixture, then roll it in the flour mix. Dip it in the egg again and give it a second coat of flour for an extra-crunchy crust. Repeat with the rest of the chicken.
  - Fry 2 or 3 pieces at a time for 3 to 4 minutes per side, until deep golden brown and 165°F in the thickest part. Move them to a paper towel-lined plate to drain.
  - Toast the buns. Spread the sauce on both cut sides, then add the chicken, cheese, lettuce, tomato and pickles.
tips:
  - Let the oil come back up to 350°F between batches. If it runs too cool, the breading soaks up oil and turns greasy.
  - Extra sauce makes a great dip for fries or chicken nuggets.
