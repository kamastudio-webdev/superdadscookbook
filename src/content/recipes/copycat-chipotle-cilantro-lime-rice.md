title: Copycat Chipotle Cilantro Lime Rice
description: Fluffy, non-sticky basmati rice cooked like pasta, then tossed with fresh cilantro and a mix of lime and lemon juice, just like the burrito bowl favorite.

cookMinutes: 20
servings: 12 servings (about ½ cup each)

ingredients:
  - 2 cups basmati rice, not rinsed (or any long-grain white rice, rinsed)
  - 1 bay leaf
  - Salt (about 2 teaspoons for the pot, plus about ¼ teaspoon to finish)
  - 1 teaspoon olive oil or rice bran oil (optional)
  - 2 tablespoons fresh cilantro, minced (or use parsley, or leave it out)
  - 2 tablespoons fresh lime juice (from 1 to 2 limes)
  - 2 tablespoons fresh lemon juice (from 1 to 2 lemons)
steps:
  - Bring 8 cups of water to a boil in a large pot. Add the basmati rice, bay leaf, oil if you're using it, and about 2 teaspoons of salt. Stir and let it come back to a boil.
  - Boil the rice uncovered, like pasta, for 10 to 12 minutes. Start tasting at 10 minutes. It should be tender with a little chew, not mushy.
  - Pull out the bay leaf. Pour the rice into a fine-mesh strainer, rinse it with hot water, and tip it into a large bowl.
  - Stir in the cilantro, lime juice and lemon juice. Taste and add more salt if needed (about ¼ teaspoon). Serve hot or at room temperature.
tips:
  - "Other long-grain white rice: rinse it first. Bring 4 cups of water to a boil, add the rice, bay leaf, oil and about 2 teaspoons of salt, and return to a boil. Turn the heat to low, cover, and cook for 15 minutes. Remove the bay leaf and stir in the cilantro and juices."
  - "Rice cooker: add rinsed long-grain rice with the amount of water your rice cooker's directions call for, plus the bay leaf, about 1 teaspoon of salt and the oil. Cook it as usual, then remove the bay leaf and stir in the cilantro, juices and about ¼ teaspoon more salt."
  - "Brown rice: use the same steps with longer times: about 40 minutes for basmati on the stove, 45 minutes for long-grain on the stove, or 60 to 70 minutes in a rice cooker. 2 cups of brown rice makes about 16 half-cup servings."
