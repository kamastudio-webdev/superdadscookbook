title: Skid Row Stroganoff
description: Peg Bracken's famously easy ground-beef stroganoff, with onion, mushrooms, cream of chicken soup and sour cream spooned over noodles cooked in beef bouillon.
category: Dinner
prepMinutes: 10
cookMinutes: 30
servings: 4 servings
date: 2026-11-08

adaptedFrom:
  name: Peg Bracken, The I Hate to Cook Book (via Food.com)
  url: https://www.food.com/recipe/skid-row-stroganoff-247292
ingredients:
  - 8 ounces noodles, uncooked
  - 1 beef bouillon cube
  - 1 garlic clove, minced
  - ⅓ cup chopped onion
  - 2 tablespoons cooking oil
  - 1 pound ground beef
  - 2 tablespoons flour
  - 1 teaspoon salt
  - ½ teaspoon paprika
  - 2 cans (3 ounces each) mushrooms
  - 1 can condensed cream of chicken soup (don't add water)
  - 1 cup sour cream
  - Chopped parsley, for serving
steps:
  - Bring a large pot of water to a boil and drop in the bouillon cube. Cook the noodles in it until tender, then drain.
  - While the noodles cook, heat the oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the garlic and onion and crumble in the ground beef. Cook, breaking up the meat, until it's browned with no pink left.
  - Sprinkle the flour, salt and paprika over the meat, add the mushrooms and stir well. Let it cook for 5 minutes.
  - Stir in the cream of chicken soup and let it simmer for 10 minutes, stirring now and then.
  - Turn the heat to low and stir in the sour cream. Heat it just until hot. Don't let it boil, or the sour cream can curdle.
  - Spread the noodles on a platter, spoon the stroganoff over the top and scatter plenty of chopped parsley around it.
tips:
  - Cooking the noodles in bouillon gives them a beefy flavor of their own, so don't skip the cube.
