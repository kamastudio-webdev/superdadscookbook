title: Creamy Bacon Potato Soup
description: A one-pot potato soup started in bacon drippings, with half of it blended smooth so every bowl is creamy but still has tender chunks of potato.
category: Dinner
prepMinutes: 20
cookMinutes: 40
servings: 6 servings
date: 2026-11-08
tags: [soup, one-pot, comfort-food]
adaptedFrom:
  name: Sugar Spun Run

ingredients:
  - 6 slices uncooked bacon, cut into small pieces
  - 3 tablespoons butter (salted or unsalted)
  - 1 medium yellow onion, chopped (about 1½ cups)
  - 3 large garlic cloves, minced
  - ⅓ cup all-purpose flour
  - 2½ pounds gold potatoes (about 6 large), peeled and cut into pieces no bigger than 1 inch
  - 4 cups chicken broth
  - 2 cups milk
  - ⅔ cup heavy cream
  - 1½ teaspoons salt, plus more to taste
  - 1 teaspoon ground black pepper
  - ¼ to ½ teaspoon ancho chili powder
  - ⅔ cup sour cream
  - "Toppings (optional): shredded cheddar, chopped chives, extra sour cream and bacon"
steps:
  - Put the bacon pieces in a large Dutch oven or soup pot over medium heat. Cook, stirring now and then, until crisp and brown, 6 to 8 minutes. Scoop the bacon onto a paper-towel-lined plate and leave the drippings in the pot.
  - Add the butter and onion to the drippings. Cook over medium heat, stirring, until the onion is soft, 3 to 5 minutes. Stir in the garlic and cook for about 30 seconds, just until it smells good.
  - Sprinkle the flour over the onions and stir (a whisk helps) until there's no dry flour left and it looks like a smooth paste.
  - Add the potatoes, broth, milk, cream, salt, pepper and ancho chili powder. Stir well, scraping the bottom of the pot.
  - Bring the soup to a boil and let it bubble for about 10 minutes, until a fork slides easily into the potato pieces.
  - Turn the heat down to a simmer. Blend about half the soup (roughly 5 cups) until smooth. An immersion blender right in the pot is easiest. If you use a regular blender, work in batches, leave the lid's center cap off and cover the hole with a folded towel, since the soup is very hot. Pour the blended soup back into the pot.
  - Stir in the sour cream and the cooked bacon, saving a little bacon for the tops if you like. Let the soup simmer for 15 minutes, stirring now and then.
  - Taste and add more salt if needed. Ladle into bowls and top with cheddar, chives, a spoonful of sour cream and bacon.
tips:
  - Broth brands vary a lot in saltiness. If yours is salty, start with 1 teaspoon of salt and add more at the end.
  - New to ancho chili powder? Start with ¼ teaspoon, taste, and go up to ½ teaspoon if you like the gentle smoky heat.
  - For a completely smooth soup, blend all of it in batches. Blending only half keeps some potato chunks for texture.
