ingredients:
  - section: Chicken
    items:
      - 1 lb chicken breast, cut into bite-size pieces
      - Oil from a jar of sun-dried tomatoes, for the pan
      - 3 teaspoons Italian seasoning (1 tablespoon)
      - 1 teaspoon paprika
      - ¼ teaspoon red pepper flakes
  - section: Pasta and sauce
    items:
      - 2 tablespoons butter
      - 1 shallot, finely chopped
      - 2 cloves garlic, minced
      - 1 teaspoon Italian seasoning
      - 2 cups water
      - "Pasta (TO DO: how much, and what shape?)"
      - 1 cup cream
      - 1 teaspoon mustard
      - "Spinach (TO DO: how much?)"
      - Juice of ½ lemon
      - ¼ cup Parmesan cheese, grated
      - "Sun-dried tomatoes in oil, chopped (TO DO: how much?)"
steps:
  - Toss the chicken pieces with the 3 teaspoons of Italian seasoning, the paprika and the red pepper flakes until they're evenly coated.
  - Spoon a little of the oil from the sun-dried tomato jar into a large, deep skillet and heat it over medium-high. Add the chicken in a single layer and cook, turning the pieces as they brown, until no pink is left in the middle (165°F), about 6 to 8 minutes. Move the chicken to a plate.
  - Turn the heat down to medium and melt the butter in the same pan. Add the shallot and garlic and cook for 2 to 3 minutes, stirring, until soft and fragrant but not browned.
  - Stir in the 1 teaspoon of Italian seasoning and the water, scrape up the browned bits from the bottom of the pan, and bring it to a boil.
  - "Add the pasta and cook it right in the pan, stirring often so it doesn't stick, until it's tender and most of the water is soaked up. TO DO: confirm the pasta amount and about how long it cooks."
  - Pour in the cream and let it simmer for a couple of minutes, until it thickens a little.
  - Stir in the mustard, spinach, lemon juice, Parmesan and sun-dried tomatoes. Keep stirring until the spinach wilts and the cheese melts into the sauce.
  - Put the chicken and any juices from the plate back in the pan, stir, and let it heat through for a minute before serving.
