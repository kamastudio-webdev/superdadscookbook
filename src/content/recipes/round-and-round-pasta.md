---
title: Round and Round Pasta
description: Wagon wheel pasta tossed with carrots, zucchini, cherry tomatoes and sliced Italian sausage in a light lemon mascarpone dressing. Every ingredient is cut into rounds.
category: Dinner
cuisine: Italian
prepMinutes: 10
cookMinutes: 20
servings: 6 to 8 servings
date: 2026-11-08
tags: [pasta, kid-friendly, sausage, weeknight]
adaptedFrom:
  name: Giada De Laurentiis, Food Network
  url: https://foodnetwork.co.uk/recipes/round-and-round-pasta
ingredients:
  - section: Pasta
    items:
      - 1 lb rotelle (wagon wheel) pasta
      - ¼ cup extra-virgin olive oil, divided
      - 2 shallots, thinly sliced
      - 3 medium carrots, cut into ¼-inch rounds
      - ½ teaspoon kosher salt, divided
      - ¼ cup fresh orange juice
      - 3 medium zucchini, ends trimmed, cut into ½-inch rounds
      - 2 cups cherry tomatoes, cut in half
      - 1 lb sweet Italian sausage, already cooked, cooled and cut into ½-inch rounds
  - section: Dressing
    items:
      - ½ cup mascarpone cheese (4 oz), at room temperature
      - 1 tablespoon lemon juice
      - Grated zest of 1 large lemon
      - 1 teaspoon salt, plus more to taste
steps:
  - Bring a big pot of salted water to a boil. Cook the rotelle, stirring now and then, until it's tender but still has a little bite, 8 to 10 minutes. Drain it, tip it into a large bowl and toss it with 2 tablespoons of the olive oil so it doesn't stick.
  - While the pasta cooks, warm the other 2 tablespoons of oil in a large nonstick skillet over medium heat. Add the shallots, carrots and ¼ teaspoon of the salt, and cook until the carrots start to soften, about 4 minutes.
  - Pour in the orange juice and add the zucchini and the last ¼ teaspoon of salt. Let it come to a boil and cook for about 2 minutes, until the juice gets a little syrupy and the zucchini is just tender. Scrape it all into the bowl with the pasta.
  - In a small bowl, whisk the mascarpone, lemon juice, lemon zest and 1 teaspoon salt until smooth.
  - Spoon the dressing over the pasta, add the cherry tomatoes and sausage, and toss until everything is coated. Taste and add more salt if it needs it.
tips:
  - If your sausage is raw, brown it in the skillet first until it's cooked through (160°F inside), let it cool for a few minutes, then slice it. Use the same skillet for the vegetables.
  - Take the mascarpone out of the fridge when you start the water so it whisks smooth.
