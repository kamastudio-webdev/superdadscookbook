title: Jalapeño Strawberry Jam
description: Sweet strawberry jam with minced jalapeños for a gentle pepper flavor, canned in half-pint jars. Great for Christmas gifts and over cream cheese with crackers.

ingredients:
  - 4 cups crushed strawberries
  - 1 cup finely minced jalapeño peppers
  - ¼ cup lemon juice
  - 1 package (2 ounces) powdered fruit pectin
  - 7 cups white sugar
  - 8 half-pint canning jars, with new lids and rings
steps:
  - Set a rack in the bottom of a large stockpot, fill it about halfway with water and start heating it to a boil. This is your canner.
  - Wash the jars, then boil the jars and lids for at least 5 minutes to sterilize them. Leave them in the hot water until you're ready to fill them.
  - In a large saucepan, stir together the strawberries, jalapeños, lemon juice and pectin. Bring it to a boil over high heat, stirring often.
  - Pour in all the sugar at once and stir until it dissolves. Bring it back to a full rolling boil that you can't stir down, and boil hard for 1 minute, stirring the whole time. Take the pan off the heat.
  - Ladle the hot jam into the hot jars, leaving ¼ inch of space at the top. Slide a thin spatula or butter knife around the inside of each jar to let out air bubbles.
  - Wipe the rim of each jar with a damp paper towel so the lid can seal, then put on the lids and screw on the rings.
  - Using a jar lifter, lower the jars onto the rack in the boiling water, about 2 inches apart. Add more boiling water if needed so the jars are covered by at least 1 inch.
  - Bring the water back to a full boil, put the lid on the pot and process the jars for 10 minutes.
  - Lift the jars out and set them a few inches apart on a towel or wooden board. Leave them alone overnight to cool and seal.
  - The next day, press the center of each lid. If it doesn't flex up and down, it's sealed. Store sealed jars in a cool, dark cupboard. Keep any jar that didn't seal in the fridge and use it first.
tips:
  - As written, the jam is sweet with only mild heat. For more kick, add extra jalapeños or a couple of habaneros.
  - Frozen strawberries work too. Thaw them, then crush. Canned jalapeños, drained and chopped, can stand in for fresh.
  - Wear gloves when you mince the peppers, and don't touch your eyes.
  - Above 1,000 feet, check a canning guide for the extra processing time your altitude needs.
  - Refrigerate jars after opening.
