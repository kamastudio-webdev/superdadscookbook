  - section: Lemon filling
    items:
      - 1 cup sugar
      - ¼ cup flour
      - A sprinkle of salt
      - 1 cup water, divided (¼ cup and ¾ cup)
      - 3 egg yolks

      - ¼ cup lemon juice
      - 2 teaspoons grated lemon rind
  - section: To finish

steps:
  - Heat the oven to 325°F.
  - Set up a double boiler. Bring an inch or two of water to a simmer in a saucepan, and set a heatproof bowl or the top pan of a double boiler over it without letting the bottom touch the water.
  - In the top of the double boiler (off the heat for now), mix the sugar, flour, salt and ¼ cup of the water until smooth.
  - In a small bowl, beat the egg yolks, then stir in the remaining ¾ cup of water. Stir this into the sugar mixture.
  - Set the bowl over the hot water and cook, stirring constantly, until the filling is thick.
  - Cover and let it cook over the hot water for 10 minutes more.
  - Take it off the heat and stir in the butter, lemon juice and lemon rind until the butter melts.
  - Pour the filling into the baked pie shell.
  - Spread the meringue over the hot filling in swirls, making sure it touches the crust all the way around so it doesn't shrink.
  - Bake for 20 minutes, until the meringue is golden.
  - Let the pie cool on a rack for about 2 hours before slicing, then keep it in the fridge.
