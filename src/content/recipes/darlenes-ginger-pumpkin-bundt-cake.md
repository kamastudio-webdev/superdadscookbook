---
title: Darlene's Ginger Pumpkin Bundt Cake (or Donuts)
description: Darlene's recipe. Gingerbread and yellow cake mixes plus pumpkin, pudding and sour cream make a moist, spiced bundt cake or a few dozen baked donuts.
category: Dessert
prepMinutes: 15
cookMinutes: 50
servings: 1 large bundt cake plus a very small cake, or about 2½ to 3 dozen donuts
date: 2026-11-06
tags: [fall, baking, family-recipe]
ingredients:
  - 1 box gingerbread mix
  - 1 box yellow cake mix
  - 2 cups canned pumpkin
  - 1¼ cups water
  - 1 tablespoon pumpkin pie seasoning
  - 1 small box instant vanilla pudding mix
  - 5 eggs
  - ½ cup vegetable oil
  - 1 cup sour cream
  - Lemon or vanilla glaze, for drizzling (optional)
steps:
  - Preheat the oven to 350°F.
  - Mix all the ingredients in the order listed, adding the eggs one at a time. Mix well.
  - Pour the batter into a greased and floured bundt pan. There will be a little batter left over to make a small cake.
  - Bake the bundt cake for 45 to 50 minutes, until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean.
  - When the cake is done, let it cool in the pan for 15 to 20 minutes before turning it out onto a cake plate.
  - Cool completely, then drizzle with a lemon or vanilla glaze if you like.
tips:
  - "To make donuts instead: spray a donut pan with cooking spray (like Pam) and fill it with batter. Bake at 350°F for 15 minutes, let them cool slightly, then move them to a cooling rack."
