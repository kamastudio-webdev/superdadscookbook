title: Triple Lemon Cake
description: Four layers of tangy lemon sour cream cake filled with homemade lemon curd and lemon buttercream, then frosted all over.
category: Dessert
prepMinutes: 60
cookMinutes: 35
servings: 12 to 14 slices
date: 2026-11-07
tags: [baking, layer-cake, lemon, birthdays]
adaptedFrom:
  name: Life Love and Sugar
  url: https://www.lifeloveandsugar.com/triple-lemon-cake/
ingredients:
  - section: Cake
    items:
      - 2 cups all-purpose flour
      - 1½ cups sugar
      - ¾ teaspoon baking powder
      - 1 teaspoon baking soda
      - ½ teaspoon salt
      - 3 eggs
      - ⅔ cup vegetable oil
      - ⅔ cup fresh lemon juice
      - ¾ cup sour cream
  - section: Lemon curd
    items:
      - ¼ cup fresh lemon juice (from 1 to 2 lemons)
      - 2 teaspoons finely grated lemon zest
      - ⅓ cup sugar
      - 4 egg yolks
      - 3 tablespoons butter (unsalted works well)
  - section: Lemon buttercream
    items:
      - 1½ cups butter
      - 1½ cups shortening
      - 12 cups (3 pounds) powdered sugar
      - 3 teaspoons (1 tablespoon) finely grated lemon zest
      - 6 tablespoons fresh lemon juice
      - ¾ teaspoon vanilla
      - 1 to 2 teaspoons water, plus more as needed
steps:
  - Make the lemon curd first so it has time to chill. Put all of the curd ingredients in a double boiler, or in a metal bowl set over a pot of gently simmering water.
  - Whisk the curd the whole time it heats. It's ready when it coats the back of a spoon. It will be noticeably thicker but still loose, not truly thick. Refrigerate it until cool and thick.
  - Heat the oven to 350°F and grease two 8-inch round cake pans.
  - In a large bowl, whisk the flour, sugar, baking powder, baking soda and salt together.
  - Add the eggs, oil and lemon juice and beat until smooth. Add the sour cream and beat until smooth again.
  - Divide the batter between the pans and bake for about 25 minutes, until a toothpick in the center comes out clean. Let the cakes cool completely.
  - For the buttercream, beat the butter and shortening together until smooth. Add 6 cups of the powdered sugar 1 cup at a time, beating after each one.
  - Mix in the lemon juice, lemon zest and vanilla, then beat in the remaining 6 cups of powdered sugar.
  - Beat in water, starting with ½ teaspoon, until the frosting is as soft as you want it.
  - Slice each cake in half horizontally to make four thin layers. Stack them on a cake plate, spreading lemon buttercream on two of the layers and lemon curd on the other two.
  - Frost the outside of the cake with the rest of the buttercream.
tips:
  - Before spreading curd on a layer, pipe a ring of buttercream around the edge. It keeps the soft curd from squeezing out the sides.
  - A long serrated knife makes it easiest to split the cakes into even layers.
