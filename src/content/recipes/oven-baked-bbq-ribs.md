ingredients:
  - 4 to 5 pounds pork spare ribs or baby back ribs
  - ¼ cup Dijon mustard
  - 2 teaspoons liquid smoke (optional)
  - 1 cup dry spice rub (your favorite)
  - 1 cup barbecue sauce, store-bought or homemade
steps:
  - Line a baking sheet with foil and set a wire cooling rack on top. Lay the ribs on the rack in a single layer so hot air can reach every side.
  - Stir the mustard and liquid smoke (if using) together and brush it over both sides of the ribs. Sprinkle the rub all over and pat it on so it sticks to the meat.
  - Move an oven rack to a few inches below the broiler and turn the broiler on. Broil the ribs meaty side up for about 5 minutes, until the rub bubbles and the ribs are evenly browned.
  - Set the oven to 300°F and move the pan to the middle rack. Bake spare ribs for 2½ to 3 hours or baby back ribs for 1½ to 2 hours. Halfway through, cover the ribs with foil so they don't dry out.
  - About 30 minutes before they're done, uncover the ribs, brush them with the barbecue sauce, cover them with the foil again and finish baking.
  - Let the ribs rest, still covered, for about 10 minutes. Cut between the bones and serve.
tips:
  - For deeper flavor, season the ribs the day before. Wrap them in plastic wrap and keep them in the fridge, then broil and bake the next day.
  - The times above are for spare ribs. Baby back ribs are done about an hour sooner.
