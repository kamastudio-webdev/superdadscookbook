ingredients:
  - 1 pound lean ground beef (at least 80% lean)
  - 1½ cups chopped onion
  - 1 teaspoon seasoned salt
  - 1 bag (12 ounces) frozen mixed vegetables
  - 1 cup Bisquick mix (or our homemade mix)
  - 2 cups milk
  - 4 eggs
  - 2 cups shredded Cheddar cheese (8 ounces)
steps:
  - Heat the oven to 400°F. Coat a 13 × 9-inch (3-quart) glass baking dish with cooking spray.
  - Cook the beef, onion and seasoned salt in a 10-inch skillet over medium-high heat, stirring often, until no pink is left in the beef. Drain off the fat.
  - Spread the beef mixture over the bottom of the baking dish and scatter the frozen vegetables evenly on top.
  - In a medium bowl, whisk the Bisquick mix, milk and eggs until smooth. Pour it evenly over the beef and vegetables.
  - Bake for 30 minutes. Sprinkle the cheese over the top and bake about 10 minutes more, until a knife pushed into the center comes out clean.
  - Cut into squares to serve.
tips:
  - One batch of our homemade Bisquick mix makes exactly the 1 cup this needs.
