ingredients:
  - section: Spicy tomato sauce
    items:
      - ⅓ cup extra-virgin olive oil
      - 3 garlic cloves, minced
      - 1 cup black olives, pitted and roughly chopped
      - 1 teaspoon red pepper flakes
      - 3 tablespoons capers, drained
      - 2 (28-ounce) cans whole tomatoes (San Marzano if you can find them)
      - ¼ cup fresh basil leaves, torn
      - Kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper
  - section: Breading
    items:
      - 1 cup all-purpose flour
      - 3 eggs
      - 4 cups dried bread crumbs
      - 1 tablespoon garlic powder
      - 1 tablespoon dried oregano
      - Salt and freshly ground black pepper
  - section: Eggplant
    items:
      - 4 pounds medium eggplants, ends trimmed, cut lengthwise into ½-inch slices
      - Extra-virgin olive oil, for frying
  - section: Ricotta filling
    items:
      - 2 pounds whole milk ricotta
      - ½ cup grated Parmigiano-Romano
      - ½ cup chopped fresh basil
      - 2 eggs
      - Salt and freshly ground black pepper
  - section: Topping
    items:
      - 2 pounds shredded mozzarella
      - ¾ cup grated Parmigiano-Romano
      - Butter, for the baking dish
steps:
  - "Make the sauce: warm the olive oil in a large saucepan over medium heat and cook the garlic for 1 minute. Add the olives, red pepper flakes and capers and cook for 2 minutes, stirring often."
  - Crush the tomatoes with your hands as you drop them into the pan, then pour in their juice. Bring to a gentle simmer, stir in the torn basil, season with salt and pepper and let it bubble for 10 minutes. Take it off the heat.
  - "Set up a breading line next to the stove in three shallow dishes: the flour seasoned with salt and pepper, then the 3 eggs beaten with a pinch of salt and pepper, then the bread crumbs mixed with the garlic powder, oregano and a good amount of salt and pepper."
  - Pour olive oil into a large skillet to about ¼ inch deep and heat it over medium heat. Coat a few eggplant slices in flour, then egg, then crumbs, and lay them in the hot oil in a single layer without crowding.
  - Fry until the slices are tender and well browned on both sides, then drain them on paper towels. Keep going until all the eggplant is cooked, adding more oil to the pan as it runs low.
  - Heat the oven to 350°F and butter a large, deep baking dish. In a bowl, stir the ricotta with ½ cup of the Parmigiano, the chopped basil and the 2 eggs, and season with salt and pepper.
  - Spread a little sauce over the bottom of the dish. Add a layer of eggplant, spread half the ricotta mixture over it, spoon on more sauce and scatter a third of the mozzarella on top.
  - Repeat with another layer of eggplant, the rest of the ricotta, more sauce and another third of the mozzarella. Finish with the last of the eggplant, the rest of the sauce and the rest of the mozzarella, then sprinkle the ¾ cup Parmigiano over everything.
  - Bake for about 1 hour, until golden and bubbling. Let it sit for 20 minutes before cutting so the layers hold together.
tips:
  - Frying the eggplant takes the longest. You can make the sauce and fry the slices earlier in the day, then assemble and bake at dinnertime.
  - This makes a big, heavy pan. Use your deepest baking dish, or a roasting pan, and set it on a baking sheet to catch drips.
