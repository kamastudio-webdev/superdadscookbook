title: Pulled Pork Barbecue
description: Pork shoulder rubbed with paprika, mustard and brown sugar, slow-roasted until it falls apart, then tossed in a tangy cider-vinegar mustard sauce and piled on buns.
category: Dinner
cuisine: Southern
prepMinutes: 20
cookMinutes: 375
restMinutes: 60
restLabel: Season
servings: 12 sandwiches
date: 2026-11-08
tags: [barbecue, feeds-a-crowd, sandwiches]
adaptedFrom:
  name: Tyler Florence, Food Network
  url: https://www.foodnetwork.com/recipes/tyler-florence/pulled-pork-barbecue-recipe-1953137
ingredients:
  - section: Dry rub
    items:
      - 3 tablespoons paprika
      - 1 tablespoon garlic powder
      - 1 tablespoon brown sugar
      - 1 tablespoon dry mustard
      - 3 tablespoons coarse sea salt
  - section: Pork
    items:
      - 1 pork shoulder or Boston butt roast (5 to 7 pounds)
  - section: Cider-vinegar barbecue sauce
    items:
      - 1½ cups cider vinegar
      - 1 cup yellow or brown mustard
      - ½ cup ketchup
      - ⅓ cup packed brown sugar
      - 2 garlic cloves, smashed
      - 1 teaspoon kosher salt
      - 1 teaspoon cayenne
      - ½ teaspoon freshly ground black pepper
      - ¾ cup water, for the roasting pan
      - Pan drippings from the pork
  - section: For serving
    items:
      - 12 hamburger buns
      - Coleslaw
      - Pickle spears
steps:
  - Stir the rub ingredients together in a small bowl and rub the mix all over the pork. Cover it and refrigerate for at least 1 hour, or overnight.
  - Heat the oven to 300°F. Put the pork in a roasting pan and roast for about 6 hours, until it's falling-apart tender. An instant-read thermometer in the thickest part should read at least 170°F.
  - While the pork roasts, make the sauce. Put the vinegar, mustard, ketchup, brown sugar, garlic, salt, cayenne and black pepper in a saucepan over medium heat. Simmer gently, stirring, for 10 minutes, until the sugar dissolves. Turn off the heat and set it aside.
  - Move the cooked pork to a large platter and let it rest for about 10 minutes. Meanwhile, set the roasting pan on the stove over medium heat, add the ¾ cup water and scrape up all the browned bits with a wooden spoon. Boil until it's reduced by about half, then pour it into the sauce and simmer the sauce for 5 minutes.
  - While the pork is still warm, shred it with two forks, using one to hold the meat and the other to pull it apart. Put the shredded pork in a bowl, pour half the sauce over it and stir until it's all coated.
  - Pile the pork on the bottom halves of the buns and top with coleslaw. Serve with pickle spears and the rest of the sauce on the side.
tips:
  - Our Cole Slaw is a good one for topping these sandwiches.
  - Put the rub on the night before and the pork is ready to go straight into the oven in the morning.
