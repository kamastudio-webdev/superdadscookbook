ingredients:
  - 2¼ cups milk
  - 6 tablespoons white vinegar
  - 360 g all-purpose flour (3 cups)
  - 6 tablespoons white sugar
  - 1 tablespoon baking powder
  - 1½ teaspoons baking soda
  - 1½ teaspoons salt
  - 3 eggs
  - 6 tablespoons butter, melted
  - Cooking spray, for the pan
steps:
  - Stir the vinegar into the milk in a medium bowl and let it sit for 5 minutes. It will thicken a little and look slightly curdled. That's the sour milk you want.
  - In a large bowl, whisk the flour, sugar, baking powder, baking soda and salt together.
  - Whisk the eggs and melted butter into the soured milk.
  - Pour the flour mixture into the milk mixture and whisk until the batter is smooth with no lumps.
  - Heat a large skillet or griddle over medium heat and coat it with cooking spray. Pour about ¼ cup of batter for each pancake.
  - Cook until bubbles pop up all over the top, then flip with a spatula and cook until the other side is golden brown. Spray the pan again as needed and repeat with the rest of the batter.
tips:
  - "Matt makes this as a triple batch, and that's what's listed here. For a single batch (about 8 pancakes), use ¾ cup milk, 2 tablespoons vinegar, 1 cup (120 g) flour, 2 tablespoons sugar, 1 teaspoon baking powder, ½ teaspoon baking soda, ½ teaspoon salt, 1 egg and 2 tablespoons melted butter."
  - They're great with a homemade blueberry sauce on top.
  - Keep finished pancakes warm on a baking sheet in a 200°F oven while you cook the rest.
