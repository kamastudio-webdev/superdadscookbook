---
title: Homemade Bisquick Mix
description: Make your own baking mix from flour, baking powder, salt and a little butter in about 10 minutes, and use it anywhere a recipe calls for Bisquick.
category: Basics

servings: 1 cup

ingredients:
  - 1 cup all-purpose flour
  - 1½ teaspoons baking powder
  - ¼ teaspoon salt
  - 1 tablespoon cold butter
steps:
  - Sift the flour into a bowl. Add the baking powder and salt and whisk until they're completely mixed in.
  - Cut the butter into the flour with a pastry cutter (or two forks) until it disappears into fine, even crumbs with no lumps of butter left.
  - Use it right away in place of 1 cup of store-bought Bisquick.
tips:
  - Shortening or oil work instead of butter. If you use oil or melted butter, drizzle it in slowly while you whisk so it spreads evenly through the flour.
  - Need more? The recipe doubles or triples easily. Because it has butter in it, keep any extra in a covered container in the fridge.
