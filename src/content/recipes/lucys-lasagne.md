---
title: Lucy's Lasagne
description: Lucy's family lasagne, with a beef and tomato sauce simmered for hours and layers of ricotta, sharp cheddar, mozzarella and Parmesan. Makes two pans.
category: Dinner
cuisine: Italian
prepMinutes: 30
cookMinutes: 200
restMinutes: 15
restLabel: Rest
servings: 2 pans (12 × 8 inches each)
date: 2026-11-08
tags: [pasta, casserole, feeds-a-crowd, family-recipe]
ingredients:
  - section: Meat sauce
    items:
      - 2 (28-ounce) cans tomatoes
      - 4 (8-ounce) cans tomato sauce
      - 2 teaspoons salt
      - 3 teaspoons oregano (1 tablespoon)
      - ¼ teaspoon pepper
      - 2 teaspoons onion salt
      - 2 cups minced onions
      - 2 garlic cloves, minced
      - ⅓ cup salad oil (vegetable oil)
      - 2 pounds ground chuck
  - section: Layers
    items:
      - 2 teaspoons salt, for the noodle water
      - 1 pound lasagne noodles (the card says not Ronzoni)
      - 1½ pounds ricotta
      - 1 pound mozzarella
      - 2 cups grated sharp cheddar
      - 1 cup grated Parmesan
steps:
  - Put the tomatoes, tomato sauce, salt, oregano, pepper and onion salt in a large saucepan or pot and start it simmering, uncovered, over medium heat.
  - Meanwhile, heat the oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Cook the onions and garlic, stirring, until lightly browned, about 8 to 10 minutes. Add the ground chuck, break it up and cook until no pink is left.
  - Stir the meat mixture into the tomato sauce. Let it simmer, uncovered, for 2½ hours, or until it's nice and thick. Stir every so often so it doesn't stick.
  - Toward the end of the simmering, cook the noodles as the package says, with the 2 teaspoons salt in the water, stirring now and then. Drain them and pull them apart so they don't stick together.
  - Heat the oven to 350°F. You'll need two 12 × 8 × 2-inch baking dishes.
  - Spoon several spoonfuls of sauce over the bottom of each dish. Lay noodles over the sauce in a crisscross (woven) pattern.
  - Spread half the ricotta over the noodles, split between the two dishes. Sprinkle on half the cheddar, a third of the mozzarella and half the Parmesan, again split between the dishes.
  - Make a second layer the same way, with sauce, crisscrossed noodles, the rest of the ricotta and cheddar, another third of the mozzarella and the rest of the Parmesan. Finish with a layer of sauce, then top with the last third of the mozzarella.
  - Bake for 50 minutes, until bubbly. Take the dishes out of the oven and let them stand for 15 minutes before cutting.
tips:
  - The long, slow simmer is what makes the sauce thick and rich, so don't rush it.
