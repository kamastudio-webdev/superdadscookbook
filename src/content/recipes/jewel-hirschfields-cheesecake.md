title: Jewel Hirschfield's Cheesecake
description: The cheesecake Jewel Hirschfield made in Detroit and sold to restaurants. Whipped egg whites make the cream cheese filling light and fluffy, over a cinnamon Zwieback crust.
category: Dessert
prepMinutes: 30
cookMinutes: 60

restLabel: Cool
servings: 12 slices

ingredients:
  - section: Crust
    items:
      - Zwieback toast, the top layer of 1 box
      - "Sugar (TO DO: how much? The card says \"Add cup sugar\" with no number. A fraction may not have printed, for example ½ cup.)"
      - 1 teaspoon cinnamon
      - ¼ cup butter, melted, plus more for the pan
  - section: Filling
    items:
      - 6 eggs
      - 1 scant cup sugar (just under 1 cup)
      - 1 pinch salt
      - "Vanilla (TO DO: how much? The card says \"teaspoon vanilla\" with no number in front.)"
      - 3 large packages cream cheese (8 ounces each), softened
      - 1 cup coffee cream (light cream)
      - Juice of 1 lemon
      - Cinnamon, for sprinkling on top
steps:
  - Heat the oven to 325°F.
  - Grind the Zwieback into fine crumbs in a food processor, or seal it in a zip-top bag and crush it with a rolling pin. Stir in the crust sugar and the 1 teaspoon of cinnamon, then mix in the melted butter until all the crumbs look damp.
  - Butter the bottom and sides of a springform pan. Press the crumbs firmly and evenly over the bottom. Put the pan in the fridge while you make the filling.
  - Separate the eggs, putting the yolks in a large bowl and the whites in a clean, dry mixing bowl.
  - Beat the yolks with the scant cup of sugar until well blended. Stir in the pinch of salt and the vanilla.
  - Beat in the cream cheese until the mixture is smooth with no lumps.
  - Add the coffee cream and the lemon juice a little at a time, switching back and forth between the two, and beat well.
  - With clean beaters, beat the egg whites until they're thick and hold stiff peaks. Gently fold them into the cream cheese mixture with a spatula until no white streaks are left.
  - Pour the filling into the chilled crust and sprinkle the top lightly with cinnamon.
  - Bake for 1 hour.
  - Turn off the oven and leave the cheesecake inside, with the door closed, for 1 hour. Then take it out and let it finish cooling to room temperature on the counter.
  - Cover and refrigerate until cold before running a knife around the edge and removing the side of the pan.
  - "TO DO: fill in the crust sugar and the vanilla amounts (marked in the ingredients), the springform pan size, and how long to bake for the 300°F water-bath option."
tips:
  - "Water bath option from the card: bake at 300°F with the springform pan set in a larger pan of hot water. Wrap the outside of the springform in foil first so no water leaks in."
  - Coffee cream is light cream, richer than half-and-half but lighter than whipping cream.
