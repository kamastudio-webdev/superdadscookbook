---
title: Russian Creme
description: A silky, make-ahead dessert of sweetened cream and sour cream set with gelatin, chilled in wine glasses and topped with fresh fruit. From a newspaper clipping by Toni VanKirk.
category: Dessert
prepMinutes: 15
cookMinutes: 5
restMinutes: 240
restLabel: Chill
servings: 6 to 8 servings
date: 2026-11-08
tags: [no-bake, make-ahead, chilled, family-recipe]
ingredients:
  - ¾ cup sugar
  - ½ cup water
  - 1 envelope unflavored gelatin (about 2¼ teaspoons)
  - 1 cup whipping cream
  - 1½ cups sour cream
  - 1 teaspoon vanilla
  - Fresh fruit, for the top
steps:
  - In a small saucepan, stir the sugar, water and gelatin together until well blended. Let it sit for 5 minutes so the gelatin can soften.
  - Set the pan over medium heat and bring it to a boil, stirring the whole time. Take it off the heat.
  - Slowly stir in the whipping cream.
  - In a medium bowl, stir together the sour cream and vanilla.
  - Pour the hot cream mixture into the sour cream a little at a time, whisking constantly, until it's completely smooth.
  - Divide it among 6 to 8 wine glasses or dessert dishes.
  - Cover and refrigerate for 4 to 6 hours, until set.
  - Top with fresh fruit just before serving.
tips:
  - Berries or sliced strawberries look pretty on top and their tartness goes well with the rich cream.
