title: French Silk Pie
description: A tall, silky chocolate pie in an Oreo cookie crust, topped with fresh whipped cream. The eggs are cooked on the stove, so there are no raw eggs.
category: Dessert
prepMinutes: 30
cookMinutes: 20
restMinutes: 360
restLabel: Chill
servings: 8 servings (one 9-inch pie)
date: 2026-11-07
tags: [pie, chocolate, holiday, make-ahead]
adaptedFrom:
  name: Handle the Heat
  url: https://handletheheat.com/french-silk-pie/
ingredients:
  - section: Crust
    items:
      - 20 Oreo cookies (230 g), regular-stuffed, filling left in
      - 3 tablespoons unsalted butter, melted
  - section: Filling
    items:
      - 1⅓ cups (266 g) granulated sugar
      - 4 large eggs
      - 8 ounces (227 g) bittersweet baking chocolate, melted
      - 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
      - 10 tablespoons (142 g) unsalted butter (1¼ sticks), at room temperature
      - 1⅓ cups heavy cream, cold
      - 4 teaspoons powdered sugar
  - section: Topping
    items:
      - 1 cup heavy cream, cold
      - 2 tablespoons powdered sugar
      - 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
      - Chocolate shavings or cocoa powder, for garnish (optional)
steps:
  - Heat the oven to 350°F and coat a 9-inch deep-dish pie plate with cooking spray.
  - Pulse the Oreos in a food processor until they're fine crumbs. Add the melted butter and pulse until the crumbs look damp. Press the crumbs firmly over the bottom and up the sides of the pie plate. Bake for 10 minutes, until set and fragrant, then let the crust cool completely on a wire rack.
  - "For the filling, whisk the sugar and eggs together in a small saucepan. Set it over medium-low heat and cook, whisking the whole time, until the mixture reaches 160°F on a thermometer and coats the back of a metal spoon. Take it off the heat and stir in the melted chocolate and vanilla until smooth. Let it cool to room temperature (about 75°F), stirring now and then."
  - With an electric mixer, beat the softened butter until light and fluffy, 2 to 3 minutes. Slowly add the cooled chocolate mixture, then turn the mixer to high and beat for 5 minutes, until fluffy.
  - In a clean bowl, beat the 1⅓ cups of cold cream until it starts to thicken. Add the 4 teaspoons of powdered sugar and keep beating until stiff peaks form. Gently fold the whipped cream into the chocolate mixture.
  - Spread the filling into the cooled crust and smooth the top. It will be piled high. Refrigerate for 6 hours to set.
  - Just before serving, beat the 1 cup of cold cream with the 2 tablespoons of powdered sugar and the vanilla on high until stiff peaks form. Spread it over the pie and top with chocolate shavings or a dusting of cocoa powder.
tips:
  - You can make the pie up to 2 days ahead. Keep it in the fridge without the whipped cream topping, and add the topping the day you serve it.
  - Cooking the egg mixture to 160°F is what makes it safe to eat, so an instant-read thermometer is worth using here.
  - Make sure the chocolate mixture has cooled before adding it to the butter, or the butter will melt and the filling won't get fluffy.
