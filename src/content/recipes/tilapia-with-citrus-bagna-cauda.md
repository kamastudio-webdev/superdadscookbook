ingredients:
  - section: Citrus bagna cauda
    items:
      - 3 tablespoons unsalted butter
      - 2 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil
      - 4 anchovy fillets, minced
      - 1½ teaspoons minced garlic
      - 2 tablespoons orange juice
      - 2 tablespoons thinly sliced fresh basil leaves
      - 1 teaspoon lemon zest
      - 1 teaspoon orange zest
      - Salt
  - section: Fish
    items:
      - 6 skinless tilapia fillets (6 ounces each)
      - 2 teaspoons extra-virgin olive oil
      - Salt and freshly ground black pepper
steps:
  - "Make the sauce: put the butter and 2 tablespoons olive oil in a heavy medium saucepan over low to medium heat and stir often until the butter melts. Add the anchovies and stir until they melt into the butter, about 2 minutes."
  - Add the garlic and cook just until you can smell it, about 30 seconds. Take the pan off the heat and stir in the orange juice, basil and both zests. Add salt to taste.
  - Heat the oven to 200°F so you can keep the first batch of fish warm.
  - Season both sides of the fillets with salt and pepper and brush them with the 2 teaspoons olive oil.
  - Heat a large nonstick skillet over medium-high heat. Cook half the fillets until they're just opaque in the center, about 3 minutes per side. Move them to a platter, cover with foil and keep them warm in the oven while you cook the rest.
  - Spoon the sauce over and around the fish and serve.
tips:
  - The sauce keeps for a day in the fridge. Cool it, cover it, and warm it gently before serving.
  - The anchovies melt away into the butter, so the sauce tastes rich and savory rather than fishy.
