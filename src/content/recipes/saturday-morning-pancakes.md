title: Saturday Morning Buttermilk Pancakes
description: Fluffy, golden pancakes from pantry staples. Mix the dry ingredients the night before and breakfast is 20 minutes away.

ingredients:
  - 2 cups all-purpose flour
  - 2 tablespoons sugar
  - 2 teaspoons baking powder
  - ½ teaspoon baking soda
  - ½ teaspoon salt
  - 2 cups buttermilk
  - 2 large eggs
  - 4 tablespoons butter, melted, plus more for the pan
  - 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
steps:
  - Whisk the flour, sugar, baking powder, baking soda and salt together in a large bowl.
  - In a second bowl, whisk the buttermilk, eggs, melted butter and vanilla.
  - Pour the wet ingredients into the dry ones and stir with a spatula just until no dry flour shows. Small lumps are fine; stirring too much makes tough pancakes. Let the batter rest 5 minutes while the pan heats.
  - Heat a griddle or large nonstick skillet over medium heat and brush it lightly with butter. It's ready when a drop of water sizzles on it.
  - Pour about ¼ cup of batter for each pancake. Cook until bubbles cover the top and the edges look set, 2 to 3 minutes.
  - Flip and cook until golden underneath, 1 to 2 minutes more. Keep finished pancakes warm on a baking sheet in a 200°F oven while you cook the rest.
tips:
  - "No buttermilk? Stir 2 tablespoons of lemon juice or white vinegar into 2 cups of milk and let it sit for 5 minutes."
  - Add blueberries or chocolate chips by sprinkling them onto each pancake right after you pour it, instead of stirring them into the bowl.
  - The first pancake is the test pancake. If it browns too fast, turn the heat down a notch.
