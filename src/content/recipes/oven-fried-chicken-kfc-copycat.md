title: Oven-Fried Chicken (KFC Copycat)
description: Chicken soaked in buttermilk and rolled in a peppery, well-spiced flour and panko coating, then baked with melted butter instead of deep-fried.
category: Dinner
prepMinutes: 20
cookMinutes: 24
restMinutes: 30
restLabel: Soak
servings: 6 servings
date: 2026-11-08
tags: [chicken, copycat, baked, kid-friendly]
adaptedFrom:
  name: Kevin Is Cooking
  url: https://keviniscooking.com/my-oven-fried-chicken-kfc-copycat/
ingredients:
  - section: Chicken
    items:
      - 4 to 5 pounds chicken pieces
      - 1 cup buttermilk
      - 4 tablespoons butter, melted
  - section: Coating
    items:
      - 1 cup flour
      - 1 cup panko bread crumbs
      - 2 teaspoons white pepper
      - 1 teaspoon ground celery seed
      - 1 teaspoon Italian seasoning
      - 1 teaspoon kosher salt
      - 1 teaspoon garlic powder
      - 1 teaspoon black pepper
      - 1 teaspoon dry mustard
      - 1 teaspoon paprika
      - 1 teaspoon ground ginger
      - ½ teaspoon baking powder
      - ¼ teaspoon turmeric
      - ¼ teaspoon cayenne
steps:
  - Put the chicken pieces in a large zip-top bag and pour in the buttermilk. Press out the air, seal it and squish the bag around so every piece is coated. Refrigerate for at least 30 minutes, or up to a few hours.
  - Heat the oven to 425°F. Set a 9 × 13-inch metal baking pan in the oven to heat for at least 15 minutes while you make the coating.
  - In a medium bowl, whisk the flour, panko, baking powder and all the spices together until evenly mixed.
  - Lift each piece of chicken out of the buttermilk and let the extra drip off. Roll it in the coating, pressing so it sticks on every side. Keep one hand for the wet chicken and the other for the dry coating so your fingers don't get clumpy.
  - Carefully take the hot pan out of the oven and pour in the melted butter. Lay the chicken in it skin side down, leaving a little space between pieces.
  - Bake for 12 minutes, turn each piece over, then bake about 12 minutes more, until the thickest piece reads 165°F inside. Big pieces may need a few extra minutes.
  - Let the chicken rest for 5 minutes before serving.
tips:
  - Any mix of bone-in, skin-on pieces works (breasts, thighs, drumsticks and wings), or use all drumsticks.
  - Use a metal pan, not glass. Glass shouldn't go into a hot oven empty, and metal browns the bottom better.
  - For boneless breasts, bake about 10 minutes per side.
