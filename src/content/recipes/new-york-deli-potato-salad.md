title: New York Deli-Style Potato Salad
description: A small-batch, deli-style potato salad of red potatoes and sour pickles in a light, tangy mayo dressing with slivered onion.
category: Sides
prepMinutes: 10
cookMinutes: 20
servings: 2 to 3 servings
date: 2026-11-08
tags: [potato-salad, side-dish, cookout, vegetarian]
adaptedFrom:
  name: Just Vegetable Recipes
  url: http://www.justvegetablerecipes.com/rprint-p3-0041357.htm
ingredients:
  - 2 medium red-skin potatoes
  - 1 to 2 sour pickles
  - 1 medium onion, or 2 scallions
  - 3 tablespoons mayonnaise
  - 1 teaspoon vinegar
  - ½ teaspoon sugar
  - 1 teaspoon salt
steps:
  - Put the whole potatoes in a pot, cover them with cold water and bring to a boil. Simmer until a fork slides in easily, about 20 minutes. Drain, and when they're cool enough to handle, peel off the skins.
  - Cut the potatoes and pickles into bite-size cubes.
  - Slice the onion into thin slivers. If you're using scallions, slice them thin.
  - In a small bowl, stir together the mayonnaise, vinegar, sugar and salt.
  - Put the potatoes, pickles and onion in a bowl, add the dressing and mix gently until everything is coated.
  - Serve right away, or cover and keep it in the fridge until it's time to eat.
tips:
  - This makes a small bowl. Double or triple everything for a cookout.
