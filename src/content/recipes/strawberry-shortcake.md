---
title: Strawberry Shortcake
description: Old-fashioned buttermilk shortcake biscuits made with self-rising flour and Crisco, served warm with strawberries and fresh whipped cream.
category: Dessert
prepMinutes: 15
cookMinutes: 12
servings: About 8 shortcakes
date: 2026-11-08
tags: [strawberries, biscuits, summer, family-recipe]
ingredients:
  - section: Shortcakes
    items:
      - 2 cups self-rising flour
      - 7 to 8 tablespoons Crisco shortening
      - 1 teaspoon baking soda
      - 1 cup buttermilk, or a little more
      - Butter, for the pan
  - section: For serving
    items:
      - Fresh strawberries, sliced
      - Fresh whipped cream
steps:
  - Heat the oven to 400°F and butter a baking pan.
  - Put the flour in a large bowl and add the Crisco. Work it in with your fingers until the mixture looks like coarse crumbs.
  - Stir the baking soda into 1 cup of buttermilk. It will foam up a little.
  - Pour the buttermilk into the flour a little at a time, mixing with your hands, until it comes together into a soft dough. If it's too dry to hold together, add a bit more buttermilk.
  - Pat the dough out about ¾ inch thick and cut out biscuits with a biscuit cutter or a glass, or shape them by hand. Set them in the buttered pan.
  - Bake for 8 to 12 minutes, until the tops are lightly browned.
  - Split the shortcakes while they're warm and top them with strawberries and whipped cream.
tips:
  - "No self-rising flour? For each cup, use 1 cup all-purpose flour plus 1½ teaspoons baking powder and ¼ teaspoon salt."
