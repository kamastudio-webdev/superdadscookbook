title: Parmesan Herb Baked Wings
description: Chicken wings dipped in melted butter and rolled in Parmesan, parsley, oregano and paprika, then baked until golden. Online they go by "heroin wings" because they're so hard to stop eating.
category: Snacks
prepMinutes: 20
cookMinutes: 60
servings: 8 servings
date: 2026-11-08
tags: [chicken, wings, game-day, low-carb]

ingredients:
  - 4 pounds chicken wings
  - 1 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  - 2 tablespoons dried parsley
  - 1 tablespoon dried oregano
  - 2 teaspoons paprika
  - 1 teaspoon salt
  - ½ teaspoon pepper
  - ½ cup butter (1 stick)
steps:
  - Heat the oven to 350°F. Cover a shallow rimmed baking pan with foil. The cheese coating bakes onto the pan, so the foil saves you a lot of scrubbing.
  - Cut each wing at the joints into a drumette and a flat. Throw away the wing tips or save them for stock.
  - In a shallow bowl, mix the Parmesan, parsley, oregano, paprika, salt and pepper.
  - Melt the butter in a second shallow bowl or a small pan.
  - Working one piece at a time, dip the chicken in the butter, roll it in the Parmesan mixture until it's well coated, and lay it on the foil. Leave a little space between pieces.
  - Bake for 1 hour, until the coating is golden and crisp and the chicken is cooked through (165°F inside).
tips:
  - These disappear fast. Make a double batch on two pans if you're feeding a crowd.
