title: Layered Chocolate Peanut Butter Pie

ingredients:
  - section: Oreo crust
    items:
      - 24 Oreo cookies, filling and all (about 2 cups of crumbs)
      - 2 tablespoons granulated sugar
      - 4 tablespoons salted butter, melted
  - section: Chocolate peanut butter layer
    items:
      - ½ cup heavy cream
      - ¾ cup semisweet chocolate chips (or chopped semisweet chocolate)
      - ½ cup creamy peanut butter
  - section: Peanut butter filling
    items:
      - 1½ cups heavy whipping cream, cold
      - 1 package (8 ounces) full-fat cream cheese, softened
      - ¼ cup (4 tablespoons) salted butter, softened
      - 1 cup creamy peanut butter
      - 2 cups powdered sugar
      - 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
      - ⅛ teaspoon sea salt
  - section: Whipped cream topping
    items:
      - 2 cups heavy whipping cream, cold
      - ½ cup powdered sugar
      - 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
steps:
  - Heat the oven to 350°F. Grind the Oreos in a food processor until they're fine crumbs. Add the sugar and melted butter and pulse until the crumbs stick together when you pinch them.
  - Press the crumbs firmly over the bottom and up the sides of a 9-inch pie dish. The flat bottom of a measuring cup works well for this. Bake for 8 minutes, then set the crust in the freezer to cool while you make the chocolate layer.
  - In a small saucepan over medium heat, warm the ½ cup of cream until small bubbles show around the edge. Don't let it boil. Take it off the heat and whisk in the chocolate and the ½ cup of peanut butter until smooth and shiny.
  - Let the chocolate mixture cool for about 5 minutes, then pour it into the cold crust and spread it evenly. Freeze until it's completely firm, about 30 minutes.
  - "For the filling: beat the 1½ cups of cold cream in a large bowl until it holds stiff peaks. Put it in the fridge for now."
  - In a second bowl, beat the cream cheese, butter and 1 cup of peanut butter until smooth with no lumps. Beat in the 2 cups of powdered sugar, the vanilla and the salt.
  - With the mixer on low, or with a spatula, fold the whipped cream into the peanut butter mixture just until no white streaks are left. Spread it over the firm chocolate layer and put the pie in the fridge.
  - "For the topping: beat the 2 cups of cold cream with the ½ cup of powdered sugar and the vanilla until stiff peaks form. Spread or pipe it over the filling."
  - Refrigerate for at least 4 hours before slicing. Overnight is even better. For clean slices, dip a sharp knife in hot water and wipe it dry between cuts.
tips:
  - This pie is rich, so cut it into thin slices. A 9-inch pie gives about 16.
  - Any chocolate sandwich cookie works for the crust, and regular or Double Stuf Oreos are both fine.
  - Use full-fat cream cheese and a no-stir creamy peanut butter (the kind that stays thick at room temperature). Oily natural peanut butter makes the filling too soft.
  - Make it up to 2 days ahead. Leftovers keep covered in the fridge for up to 5 days.
  - To freeze, wrap the whole pie or single slices well and freeze for up to 1 month. Thaw a whole pie in the fridge overnight, or slices for about 3 hours (unwrap them first).
  - Short on time? The site's other Chocolate Peanut Butter Pie is quicker. It uses store-bought whipped topping and skips the chocolate layer.
