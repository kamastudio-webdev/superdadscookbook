title: Cinnamon Raisin Bagels
description: Soft, chewy bagels swirled with cinnamon and studded with raisins, boiled in a malt water bath and baked until browned.
category: Breads
prepMinutes: 35
cookMinutes: 25
restMinutes: 90
restLabel: Rise
servings: 8 to 10 bagels
date: 2026-11-07
tags: [baking, bagels, breakfast, freezer-friendly]
adaptedFrom:
  name: Healthy Happy Life
  url: https://healthyhappylife.com/cinnamon-raisin-bagels/
ingredients:
  - section: Dough
    items:
      - 2¼ teaspoons yeast
      - 1½ cups warm water
      - 4 tablespoons coconut sugar (or barley malt syrup, maple syrup or agave)
      - 2 teaspoons salt
      - 1 tablespoon ground cinnamon
      - 4 cups bread flour (500 g), plus a little more for the counter
  - section: Folded in after the first rise
    items:
      - 1 cup raisins
      - About 1 teaspoon more ground cinnamon, to taste
  - section: Water bath
    items:
      - 6 cups water
      - 2 tablespoons barley malt syrup (or maple syrup, agave or coconut sugar)
steps:
  - In a large mixing bowl, stir the yeast, warm water and coconut sugar together until the sugar dissolves. Let it sit for 5 to 10 minutes.
  - Add the salt, cinnamon and flour. Stir with a big wooden spoon, or use a mixer with a dough hook, until it comes together into a light, fluffy dough. Turn it out onto a lightly floured counter and knead for 2 to 5 minutes.
  - Put the dough back in the bowl, cover it with a clean dish towel and set it somewhere warm. Let it rise for 60 to 90 minutes.
  - Near the end of the rise, rinse the raisins in warm water and drain them. You don't need to soak them; a quick rinse softens them so they stick to the dough.
  - Scatter the raisins and a few shakes of extra cinnamon over the risen dough and knead them in right in the bowl.
  - Turn the dough out onto a floured counter and knead a few more times to work in any loose raisins. Cut it into 8 pieces for regular bagels or 10 for smaller ones.
  - Heat the oven to 425°F and line a baking sheet with parchment paper. Pour the 6 cups of water and the barley malt syrup into a large soup pot and set it over medium heat so it starts warming up.
  - Knead each piece of dough briefly in your hands and roll it into a ball. Poke your finger through the center and wiggle it around to stretch the hole. Set it aside and shape the rest.
  - Turn the heat under the pot to high. Once it's boiling, drop in 2 or 3 bagels at a time and boil for 1 to 2 minutes. Lift them out with a mesh strainer and set them on the baking sheet.
  - Bake for 25 minutes, until the tops are browned.
  - Cool the bagels on a wire rack and slice them once they're just warm.
tips:
  - Matt weighs the flour. 4 cups of bread flour is 500 g.
  - All-purpose or sprouted grain flour can stand in for the bread flour.
  - For shinier, more golden tops, brush the boiled bagels with egg wash (or a vegan egg wash) before baking.
  - To freeze, slice the bagels first so they can go straight from the freezer into the toaster.
