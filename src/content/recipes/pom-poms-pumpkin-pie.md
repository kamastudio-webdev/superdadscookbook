title: Pom-Pom's Pumpkin Pie
description: Pom-Pom's pumpkin pie. A classic spiced pumpkin custard with ginger, cinnamon and nutmeg, deepened with a little molasses and a splash of brandy.
category: Dessert
prepMinutes: 15
cookMinutes: 40
restMinutes: 120
restLabel: Cool
servings: 8 slices
date: 2026-11-08
tags: [pie, pumpkin, thanksgiving, fall, family-recipe]
ingredients:
  - 1 unbaked pie shell
  - 1 cup sugar
  - 1 tablespoon flour
  - ½ teaspoon salt
  - 1 teaspoon ginger
  - ¾ teaspoon cinnamon
  - ⅛ teaspoon nutmeg
  - 2 cups pumpkin purée
  - 1½ tablespoons molasses
  - 1½ tablespoons butter, melted
  - 3 eggs
  - 1¾ cups milk
  - 2 tablespoons brandy
steps:
  - Heat the oven to 425°F. Set the unbaked pie shell on a baking sheet so it's easy to move in and out of the oven.
  - In a large bowl, stir together the sugar, flour, salt, ginger, cinnamon and nutmeg until the spices are evenly mixed in.
  - Add the pumpkin, molasses and melted butter, and stir until the mixture is smooth with no streaks of sugar.
  - Pour the milk into a large measuring cup or bowl and slowly stir in the brandy.
  - In a small bowl, beat the eggs lightly with a fork, just until the yolks and whites are blended, then stir them into the milk.
  - Pour the milk mixture into the pumpkin mixture and whisk until it's completely smooth.
  - Pour the filling into the pie shell.
  - Bake for 40 minutes, until the edges are set and the center jiggles only slightly when you nudge the pan.
  - Let the pie cool on a rack for at least 2 hours before slicing so the filling can finish setting.
tips:
  - One 15-ounce can of pumpkin is about 1¾ cups, so you'll need a little from a second can to make 2 cups.
  - This makes a generous amount of filling. A deep-dish pie shell gives it room so it doesn't spill over.
  - Cover leftovers and keep them in the fridge.
