---
title: Braised Pork Loin in Chianti
description: A pork loin browned in a hot oven, then covered and braised in Chianti with garlic and parsley until tender, with the wine cooked down into a simple sauce.
category: Dinner
cuisine: Italian
prepMinutes: 10
cookMinutes: 120
servings: 8 to 10 servings
date: 2026-11-08
tags: [roast, sunday-dinner, one-pot]
ingredients:
  - 1 pork loin roast (4 to 5 pounds)
  - Salt and pepper
  - 2½ cups Chianti (or another dry red wine)
  - 2 garlic cloves, finely minced
  - 2 tablespoons chopped parsley
steps:
  - Heat the oven to 400°F. Rub the pork loin all over with salt and pepper and set it in a Dutch oven, fat side up.
  - Roast it uncovered for 30 minutes, until the outside is nicely browned.
  - Pour the Chianti into the pot and scatter the garlic and parsley around the meat. Put the lid on and turn the oven down to 350°F.
  - Roast for 1½ hours more, spooning the pan juices over the loin often. Take the lid off for the last 15 minutes so the top browns again.
  - Move the pork to a warm platter. The wine should have cooked down to about half. If there's more than that, set the pot on the stove over medium-high heat and boil the liquid until you have about 1 cup.
  - Slice the pork and pour the sauce over it, or serve the sauce on the side in a gravy boat.
tips:
  - This comes from the family recipe binder, where it's credited to the Gourmet Cookbook.
  - Serve it with Potatoes Lyonnaise, the side dish on the same page in the binder.
  - Let the roast sit for about 10 minutes before slicing so the juices stay in the meat.
