title: Chicken Parmigiana
description: Thin chicken breasts in a crisp panko crust, quickly pan-fried, then baked under homemade tomato sauce, fresh mozzarella and Parmesan.

ingredients:
  - section: Tomato sauce
    items:
      - 2 tablespoons olive oil
      - 1 large Spanish onion, finely chopped
      - 4 garlic cloves, mashed into a paste with a pinch of kosher salt
      - 2 cans (28 ounces each) plum tomatoes, blended smooth with their juice
      - 1 can (16 ounces) crushed tomatoes
      - 1 small can (6 ounces) tomato paste
      - 1 cup water
      - 1 bay leaf
      - 1 small bunch flat-leaf (Italian) parsley
      - 1 Cubano (Cubanelle) pepper, chopped
      - Salt and freshly ground black pepper
  - section: Chicken
    items:
      - 4 boneless, skinless chicken breasts, pounded thin
      - Salt and freshly ground black pepper
      - 2 cups all-purpose flour, seasoned with salt and pepper
      - 4 large eggs
      - 2 tablespoons water
      - 2 cups panko bread crumbs
      - 1 cup vegetable oil or pure olive oil
      - 1 pound fresh mozzarella, thinly sliced
      - ¼ cup freshly grated Parmesan
      - Fresh basil or parsley leaves, to finish
steps:
  - "Start the sauce: warm the olive oil in a medium saucepan over medium heat. Add the onion and garlic paste and cook, stirring, until soft, about 5 minutes."
  - Add the blended plum tomatoes, crushed tomatoes, tomato paste, water, bay leaf, parsley bunch and chopped pepper. Bring it to a boil and season with salt and pepper. Turn the heat down and let it simmer until a little thicker, about 30 minutes. Fish out the bay leaf and parsley stems before using.
  - While the sauce simmers, heat the oven to 400°F. Season the pounded chicken on both sides with salt and pepper.
  - Set out three shallow dishes. Put the seasoned flour in the first. In the second, beat the eggs with the 2 tablespoons of water and a little salt and pepper. Put the panko in the third.
  - Coat each chicken breast in flour and tap off the extra. Dip it in the egg and let the extra drip back into the dish, then press both sides into the panko.
  - Split the oil between 2 large skillets and heat over high heat until it shimmers and is almost smoking. Lay 2 breasts in each pan and fry until golden brown, about 2 minutes per side.
  - Move the chicken to a baking sheet. Top each piece with a few spoonfuls of sauce, a few slices of mozzarella, a pinch of salt and pepper and 1 tablespoon of Parmesan.
  - Bake for 5 to 7 minutes, until the cheese melts and the chicken is cooked through (165°F inside). Scatter basil or parsley leaves on top and serve with extra sauce.
tips:
  - The sauce makes a big pot, much more than 4 pieces of chicken need. Serve the rest over pasta on the side, or freeze it for another night.
  - Pound the chicken to an even ¼ to ½ inch so it cooks through in the short time in the pan and oven.
