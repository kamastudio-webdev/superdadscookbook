title: Stromboli
description: Two big rolled stromboli stuffed with spicy sausage and peppers, ham, pepperoni, olives, provolone and mozzarella, baked golden with a Parmesan crust.

cookMinutes: 35
restMinutes: 120
restLabel: Rise
servings: 6 servings (2 stromboli)

  name: Emeril Lagasse, Food Network

ingredients:
  - section: Pizza dough
    items:
      - 1 cup warm water (110°F)
      - 1 envelope (¼ ounce) active dry yeast
      - 1 teaspoon sugar
      - 1 tablespoon plus 1½ teaspoons extra-virgin olive oil, divided
      - 3 cups bleached all-purpose flour, plus more for the counter
      - 1 teaspoon salt
  - section: Filling
    items:
      - ½ pound hot Italian sausage, casings removed
      - 1 cup sliced yellow onions
      - ½ cup thinly sliced red bell pepper
      - ½ cup thinly sliced green bell pepper
      - 2 tablespoons thinly sliced jalapeños, stems and seeds removed
      - 2 tablespoons minced garlic
      - 1 teaspoon Italian seasoning
      - ½ pound sliced ham
      - ¼ pound thinly sliced pepperoni or salami
      - ½ cup sliced black olives
      - 2 cups grated provolone
      - 2 cups grated mozzarella
  - section: Topping
    items:
      - 1 large egg, beaten with 1 tablespoon water
      - 1 cup finely grated Parmesan
steps:
  - "Start the dough: in a large bowl, stir together the warm water, yeast, sugar and 1 tablespoon of the olive oil. Let it sit for about 5 minutes, until foamy."
  - Mix in 1½ cups of the flour and the salt with your hand or a wooden spoon. Work in the rest of the flour a little at a time until the dough is smooth but still a bit sticky. Knead it on a floured counter for 3 to 5 minutes.
  - Rub the remaining 1½ teaspoons oil around a clean bowl, add the dough and turn it to coat. Cover and let it rise in a warm spot for 1 to 1½ hours, until nearly doubled.
  - "While the dough rises, make the filling: brown the sausage in a large skillet over medium-high heat, breaking it up, about 5 minutes. Lift it out with a slotted spoon onto paper towels, and pour off all but 1 tablespoon of the fat."
  - Add the onions, both bell peppers and the jalapeños to the skillet and cook, stirring, until very soft, 4 to 5 minutes. Stir in the garlic and Italian seasoning for 1 minute, then take the pan off the heat, stir the sausage back in and let the mixture cool.
  - Heat the oven to 375°F and grease a large baking sheet.
  - Punch down the dough and cut it in half. On a lightly floured counter, roll one half into a rectangle about 10 by 14 inches. Spread half the sausage mixture over it, leaving a 1-inch border, then layer on half the ham, pepperoni, olives, provolone and mozzarella, overlapping them a little.
  - Brush egg wash along one long edge. Starting from the opposite long edge, roll the dough up into a log and pinch the seam and ends closed. Set it seam side down on the baking sheet. Make the second stromboli the same way, then let both rise for 20 to 30 minutes.
  - Brush the tops with egg wash and bake for about 20 minutes, until almost completely golden and starting to crisp. Sprinkle each one with Parmesan and bake about 5 minutes more, until the cheese melts and the crust is deep golden.
  - Let them rest for 10 minutes, then cut into thick slices.
tips:
  - Let the filling cool before you spread it on the dough. Warm filling makes the dough soft and hard to roll up.
  - Pinch the seams tightly so the cheese doesn't leak out in the oven.
