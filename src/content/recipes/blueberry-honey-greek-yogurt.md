---
title: Blueberry Honey Greek Yogurt
description: A big make-ahead batch of plain Greek yogurt stirred with thawed blueberries and honey, then weighed out into grab-and-go breakfast cups.
category: Breakfast
prepMinutes: 20
cookMinutes: 0
restMinutes: 480
restLabel: Thaw
servings: 19 servings (200 g each)
date: 2026-11-07
tags: [make-ahead, meal-prep, no-cook]
ingredients:
  - 6 lb plain Greek yogurt (for example, two 3-lb tubs)
  - 3 lb frozen blueberries
  - 400 g honey (about 1 cup plus 3 tablespoons)
steps:
  - The night before, move the bag of blueberries to the fridge to thaw.
  - Set your largest mixing bowl on a kitchen scale and write down what it weighs empty. (Our extra-large bowl weighs 1,136 g.)
  - Add the yogurt, the thawed blueberries and the honey, and fold everything together until the honey is mixed in and the berries are spread evenly through the yogurt.
  - Weigh the full bowl and subtract the empty bowl's weight. Divide that number by 200 to see how many servings you have, about 19.
  - Spoon 200 g (a generous ¾ cup) into each container, put the lids on and refrigerate.
tips:
  - Each serving has about 21 g of honey, which is 1 tablespoon.
  - "Single-tub version: a 64-oz tub of plain Greek yogurt, 22 oz blueberries and 231 g honey (about ⅔ cup) makes 11 servings of about 6 oz yogurt, 2 oz blueberries and 1 tablespoon honey each."
  - Keep the cups covered in the fridge and eat them within about 5 days.
