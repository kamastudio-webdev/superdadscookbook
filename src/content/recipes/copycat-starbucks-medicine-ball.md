ingredients:
  - ¾ cup water
  - ¾ cup lemonade (Simply Light works well)
  - 1 bag Teavana Jade Citrus Mint tea
  - 1 bag Teavana Peach Tranquility tea
  - 2 teaspoons honey
  - ⅛ teaspoon peppermint extract (optional)
steps:
  - Heat the water and lemonade together until steaming hot, either in a small saucepan on the stove or in a large microwave-safe mug.
  - Drop in both tea bags and let them steep for 2 to 3 minutes, then pull them out.
  - Pour into a mug if you used a pan. Stir in the honey and the peppermint extract, if using, and drink it warm.
tips:
  - Lemonade is already sweet, so taste before adding all the honey. Use more or less to suit you.
  - The peppermint extract gives a cool, sinus-clearing kick. Leave it out if you don't want the extra mint.
  - "No Teavana tea? Use one bag each of Bigelow Mint Medley, Celestial Seasonings Country Peach Passion and Celestial Seasonings Vitamin C Shine instead."
