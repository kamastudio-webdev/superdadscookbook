title: Big-Batch Chocolate Chip Cookies
description: Crisp edges, soft middles and enough to fill the cookie jar. The dough freezes well, so fresh cookies are never far off.

cookMinutes: 30
servings: about 4 dozen cookies

ingredients:
  - 2¼ cups all-purpose flour
  - 1 teaspoon baking soda
  - 1 teaspoon salt
  - 1 cup (2 sticks) unsalted butter, softened
  - ¾ cup granulated sugar
  - ¾ cup packed brown sugar
  - 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
  - 2 large eggs
  - 2 cups semisweet chocolate chips
steps:
  - Heat the oven to 375°F. Line two baking sheets with parchment paper.
  - Whisk the flour, baking soda and salt together in a bowl and set aside.
  - Beat the butter, granulated sugar and brown sugar with a mixer on medium speed until light and fluffy, about 2 minutes. Beat in the vanilla, then the eggs one at a time.
  - Mix in the flour mixture on low speed just until combined. Stir in the chocolate chips with a spatula.
  - Scoop rounded tablespoons of dough onto the baking sheets, about 2 inches apart.
  - Bake one sheet at a time for 9 to 11 minutes, until the edges are golden but the centers still look a little soft. Let the cookies sit on the pan for 2 minutes, then move them to a rack to cool. Repeat with the rest of the dough.
tips:
  - For thicker cookies, chill the dough for 30 minutes before scooping.
  - To freeze, scoop dough balls onto a sheet pan, freeze until firm, then store them in a freezer bag. Bake straight from frozen, adding 1 to 2 minutes.
  - Softened butter should dent when you press it, but not look shiny or melted.
