title: Copycat Olive Garden Pasta e Fagioli
description: A hearty, chili-like soup of ground beef, two kinds of beans, vegetables and tomatoes, finished with little ditali pasta, like the one at Olive Garden.
category: Dinner
cuisine: Italian
prepMinutes: 15
cookMinutes: 90
servings: 8 servings
date: 2026-11-08
tags: [soup, copycat, feeds-a-crowd]
adaptedFrom:
  name: Top Secret Recipes (Todd Wilbur)

ingredients:
  - 1 pound ground beef
  - 1 small onion, diced (1 cup)
  - 1 large carrot, cut into thin matchsticks (1 cup)
  - 3 celery stalks, chopped (1 cup)
  - 2 garlic cloves, minced
  - 2 (14.5-ounce) cans diced tomatoes
  - 1 (15-ounce) can red kidney beans, with their liquid
  - 1 (15-ounce) can great northern beans, with their liquid
  - 1 (15-ounce) can tomato sauce
  - 1 (12-ounce) can V8 juice
  - 1 tablespoon white vinegar
  - 1½ teaspoons salt
  - 1 teaspoon oregano
  - 1 teaspoon basil
  - ½ teaspoon pepper
  - ½ teaspoon thyme
  - ½ pound ditali pasta (half a 1-pound box)
steps:
  - Brown the ground beef in a large pot over medium heat, breaking it up as it cooks. Spoon or pour off most of the fat.
  - Add the onion, carrot, celery and garlic and cook, stirring now and then, for 10 minutes, until the vegetables start to soften.
  - Stir in everything else except the pasta, including the liquid from both cans of beans. Bring it up to a simmer, then let it bubble gently for 1 hour, stirring now and then.
  - When the soup has about 10 minutes left, bring 1½ to 2 quarts of water to a boil over high heat. Cook the ditali for about 10 minutes, until just tender but still a little firm in the middle. Drain.
  - Stir the pasta into the soup and simmer for 5 to 10 minutes more before serving.
tips:
  - The pasta keeps soaking up broth as it sits. If you're planning on leftovers, keep the cooked pasta in a separate container and add it to each bowl when you reheat.
