ingredients:
  - section: Pastry
    items:
      - 4½ cups all-purpose flour, plus extra for rolling
      - 1 tablespoon salt
      - 2 cups (4 sticks) unsalted butter, cold, cut into small pieces
      - ¾ cup ice water, plus more if needed
  - section: Chicken broth
    items:
      - 1 whole free-range chicken (4 to 5 pounds)
      - 3 quarts cool water
      - 3 carrots, cut into 2-inch pieces
      - 3 celery stalks, cut into 2-inch pieces
      - 1 onion, cut in half
      - 1 head garlic, cut in half crosswise
      - 2 turnips, cut in half
      - 4 fresh thyme sprigs, 2 fresh rosemary sprigs and 1 bay leaf, tied in a bundle with kitchen string
  - section: Filling
    items:
      - ½ cup (1 stick) unsalted butter
      - ½ cup all-purpose flour
      - Sea salt and freshly ground black pepper
      - 4 carrots, sliced into ½-inch rounds
      - 1 cup pearl onions, peeled (about ¾ pound)
      - 1 cup fresh sweet peas (about 1 pound)
      - 1 garlic clove, chopped
      - Leaves from 8 fresh thyme sprigs
      - Leaves from 1 fresh rosemary sprig, chopped
      - Juice of ½ lemon
  - section: To finish
    items:
      - 1 egg beaten with 3 tablespoons water, for brushing
      - Coarse salt
steps:
  - "Make the pastry: stir the flour and salt together in a very large bowl. Cut in the cold butter with a pastry blender or your fingertips until it looks like coarse crumbs. Add the ice water and work it in until the dough holds together without being wet or sticky. Squeeze a little in your hand; if it crumbles, add more ice water 1 tablespoon at a time. Wrap the ball of dough in plastic wrap and refrigerate it while you make the filling. You can make it the night before."
  - "Make the broth: put the chicken in a large stockpot and pour in the 3 quarts of cool water. Add the carrots, celery, onion, garlic, turnips and herb bundle. Bring it to a boil over medium-high heat, then let it simmer uncovered for 1 hour, skimming off the fat as it rises."
  - Lift the chicken onto a platter to cool. Pour the broth through a colander into another pot and throw away the vegetables and herbs. You should have about 2 quarts. When the chicken is cool enough to handle, pull the meat into shreds and discard the skin and bones.
  - "Start the filling: wipe out the stockpot and set it over medium-low heat. Melt the butter, and once the foam settles, add the flour. Stir constantly with a wooden spoon or whisk so it stays smooth, and cook until it turns a light golden blonde."
  - Slowly pour in the broth, whisking the whole time so no lumps form. Let it simmer for 10 minutes, whisking, until it's as thick as cream of chicken soup and no longer tastes floury.
  - Stir in the carrots, pearl onions, peas, garlic, thyme, rosemary and lemon juice. Simmer for 10 minutes so the vegetables start to soften, then season with salt and pepper. Stir in the shredded chicken, take the pot off the heat and let the filling cool to room temperature. It thickens a lot as it cools.
  - Heat the oven to 400°F. Take the dough out of the fridge and let it sit on the counter for 5 minutes so it rolls out more easily.
  - Dust the counter and rolling pin with flour and roll the dough into a large rectangle, about 14 × 20 inches. Carefully move it onto a large rectangular pizza stone or a sheet pan.
