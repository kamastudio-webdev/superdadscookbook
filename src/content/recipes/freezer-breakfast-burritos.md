---
title: Freezer Breakfast Burritos
description: Make a batch on Sunday and grab-and-go breakfasts are covered for the week. Each one reheats in about 2 minutes.
category: Breakfast
cuisine: Tex-Mex
prepMinutes: 20
cookMinutes: 25
servings: 10 burritos

ingredients:
  - 1 pound breakfast sausage
  - 3 cups frozen diced hash brown potatoes
  - 12 large eggs
  - ¼ cup milk
  - 1 teaspoon salt
  - ½ teaspoon black pepper
  - 2 cups shredded cheddar or Mexican-blend cheese
  - 10 large (10-inch) flour tortillas
  - Salsa or hot sauce, for serving (optional)
steps:
  - Brown the sausage in a large skillet over medium-high heat, breaking it into small crumbles, about 8 minutes. Move it to a plate lined with paper towels.
  - In the same skillet, cook the hash browns in the leftover fat over medium heat until browned and crisp, 8 to 10 minutes, stirring now and then. Add a tablespoon of oil if the pan looks dry. Move them to the plate with the sausage.
  - Whisk the eggs, milk, salt and pepper. Wipe out the skillet, add a little butter or oil, and scramble the eggs over medium-low heat until just set but still slightly soft, 4 to 5 minutes. They finish cooking when you reheat the burritos.
  - Let everything cool for 10 minutes so the tortillas don't get soggy.
  - Warm the tortillas in the microwave for 20 to 30 seconds so they fold without cracking. Fill each one with eggs, sausage, potatoes and cheese, fold in the sides, and roll it up tightly.
  - Wrap each burrito in foil or plastic wrap, put them all in a freezer bag, and freeze for up to 3 months.
tips:
  - "To reheat from frozen: take off the wrapping, wrap the burrito in a damp paper towel, microwave for 1 minute, flip it, then microwave 1 more minute until hot in the middle."
  - Want it crispy? After microwaving, toast it in a dry skillet for a minute per side.
  - Keep the salsa out until you eat. Freezing it inside makes the tortilla soggy.
