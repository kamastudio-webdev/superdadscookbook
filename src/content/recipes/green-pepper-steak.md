---
title: Green Pepper Steak
description: Thin strips of round steak browned with garlic, then simmered with green peppers, tomatoes, soy sauce and ginger in a glossy beef gravy. Serve it over rice.
category: Dinner
cuisine: Chinese
prepMinutes: 15
cookMinutes: 30
servings: 4 servings
date: 2026-11-08
tags: [beef, stovetop, family-recipe]
ingredients:
  - 1¼ pounds boneless round steak
  - ¼ cup olive oil
  - 1 garlic clove, finely minced
  - 2 tomatoes, cut into chunks
  - 4 medium green peppers, seeded and cut into strips
  - ⅓ cup soy sauce, plus extra for the table
  - Black pepper, to taste
  - ½ teaspoon sugar
  - 1¼ teaspoons ground ginger
  - 1¼ cups beef stock, divided
  - 2 tablespoons cornstarch
  - Cooked rice, for serving
steps:
  - Slice the steak on a diagonal, across the grain, into thin strips.
  - Heat the oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat until it shimmers. Add the steak and garlic and brown them quickly, stirring, for 2 to 3 minutes.
  - Stir in the tomatoes and green pepper strips.
  - Add the soy sauce, a few grinds of black pepper, the sugar, the ginger and ¾ cup of the beef stock. Stir, cover the pan, turn the heat down to medium-low and cook for 15 to 20 minutes, until the meat is tender.
  - In a small cup, stir the cornstarch into the remaining ½ cup of stock until smooth. Pour it into the skillet and bring it to a boil, then let it simmer for 2 minutes, stirring the whole time, until the sauce thickens and turns glossy.
  - Spoon the pepper steak over rice and put extra soy sauce on the table.
tips:
  - The steak is easier to slice thin if you put it in the freezer for 20 to 30 minutes first.
