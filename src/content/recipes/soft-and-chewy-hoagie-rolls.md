title: Soft and Chewy Hoagie Rolls
description: Homemade sub rolls made in the stand mixer, with butter worked into the dough for a soft, chewy crumb and a golden crust.
category: Breads
prepMinutes: 25
cookMinutes: 20
restMinutes: 105
restLabel: Rise
servings: 4 to 8 rolls
date: 2026-11-07
tags: [baking, bread, sandwiches, stand-mixer]
adaptedFrom:
  name: The Kitchen Whisperer
  url: https://www.thekitchenwhisperer.net/2012/07/02/super-soft-n-chewy-hoagie-rolls/
ingredients:
  - 3½ to 4 cups bread flour (448 to 512 g)
  - 1¼ cups plus 2 tablespoons to 1½ cups warm water, 110 to 115°F (308 to 355 g)
  - 2 tablespoons sugar
  - 1 packet instant yeast (2¼ teaspoons, 7 g)
  - 1 teaspoon kosher salt (6 g)
  - 4 tablespoons cold butter, cut into cubes
steps:
  - Fit the stand mixer with the dough hook. Put 2 cups of the flour, 1¼ cups plus 2 tablespoons of the warm water, the sugar and the instant yeast in the bowl. Mix on low for 4 minutes.
  - Add the salt, then add the rest of the flour 1 cup at a time, mixing for 5 to 6 minutes and bringing the mixer up to medium speed. The dough should turn soft and slack. If it's still stiff after 5 to 6 minutes, add more warm water 1 tablespoon at a time (up to 1½ cups of water in all) until it relaxes.
  - With the mixer running, add the cold butter one piece at a time, letting each piece almost disappear before adding the next. Keep mixing for 1 to 3 minutes, until the dough pulls cleanly away from the sides of the bowl and looks smooth and shiny.
  - Put the dough in a greased bowl, cover it and let it rise until doubled, about 1 hour.
  - Punch the dough down and turn it out onto a very lightly floured counter. Divide it into 4 to 8 pieces and shape each one into a long roll. Use as little flour as you can here, because extra flour makes the rolls tough.
  - Set the rolls on a parchment-lined baking sheet and cover them loosely with plastic wrap sprayed with cooking spray. Let them rise until almost doubled, 30 to 45 minutes. Don't let them go much longer or they can collapse in the oven.
  - Heat the oven to 375°F. If you like, cut a slash down the top of each roll with a bread lame or very sharp knife, and brush the tops with an egg wash or melted butter.
  - Bake for 16 to 23 minutes, until golden brown and 200°F in the center.
  - Let the rolls cool before slicing them with a serrated bread knife.
tips:
  - "Using active dry yeast instead? Wake it up first: stir the yeast and sugar into ¼ cup plus 2 tablespoons of the warm water in the mixer bowl and wait 5 to 10 minutes, until it's foamy. Then add 2 cups of flour and 1 more cup of warm water, mix on low for 4 minutes, and carry on from the second step."
  - How much flour and water you need depends on how humid your kitchen is, so go by how the dough feels rather than the exact amount.
  - An instant-read thermometer is the surest way to tell the rolls are done. Look for 200°F in the middle.
