---
title: Slow Cooker Beef Chili
description: Brown the beef in the morning, let the slow cooker do the rest, and dinner is ready when everyone gets home.
category: Dinner
prepMinutes: 20
cookMinutes: 360
servings: 8 servings
date: 2026-11-06
tags: [slow-cooker, make-ahead, freezer-friendly]
ingredients:
  - 2 pounds lean ground beef
  - 1 large onion, chopped
  - 3 cloves garlic, minced
  - 2 cans (15 ounces each) kidney beans, drained and rinsed
  - 1 can (28 ounces) crushed tomatoes
  - 1 can (15 ounces) diced tomatoes, with their juice
  - 1 cup beef broth
  - 3 tablespoons chili powder
  - 2 teaspoons ground cumin
  - 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  - 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
  - 1½ teaspoons salt
  - ½ teaspoon black pepper
  - Shredded cheddar, sour cream, sliced green onions and tortilla chips, for serving
steps:
  - Brown the beef and onion in a large skillet over medium-high heat, breaking up the meat, until no pink remains, about 10 minutes. Add the garlic for the last minute. Drain off the extra fat.
  - Move the beef mixture to a 6-quart slow cooker. Add the beans, crushed tomatoes, diced tomatoes, broth, chili powder, cumin, oregano, smoked paprika, salt and pepper, and stir well.
  - Cover and cook on low for 6 to 8 hours, or on high for 3 to 4 hours.
  - Taste and add more salt or chili powder if it needs it. Serve with your favorite toppings.
tips:
  - Freeze leftovers in single portions. They keep for 3 months and thaw overnight in the fridge.
  - Like it thicker? Take the lid off for the last 30 minutes.
  - For more heat, cook a chopped jalapeño with the onion or stir in a pinch of cayenne at the end.
