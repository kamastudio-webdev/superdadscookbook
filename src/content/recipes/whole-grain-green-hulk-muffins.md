title: Whole Grain Green Hulk Muffins
description: Bright green whole wheat muffins sweetened with ripe bananas, with baby spinach blended right into the batter. This is a double batch.
category: Breakfast
prepMinutes: 15
cookMinutes: 18
servings: 32 to 40 muffins
date: 2026-11-07
tags: [baking, muffins, whole-grain, kid-friendly, make-ahead]
adaptedFrom:
  name: Mel's Kitchen Cafe
  url: https://www.melskitchencafe.com/healthy-green-hulk-muffins/
ingredients:
  - 710 g white whole wheat flour (about 5 cups)
  - 4 teaspoons baking powder
  - 1 teaspoon baking soda
  - 1 teaspoon salt
  - 2 large eggs
  - 1½ cups (318 g) granulated sugar
  - 1½ cups buttermilk
  - ½ cup canola oil (or vegetable, avocado, grapeseed or melted coconut oil)
  - 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
  - 12 ounces (340 g) baby spinach
  - 4 very ripe bananas, mashed (about 454 g)
steps:
  - Heat the oven to 350°F. Line muffin tins with paper liners or grease them with nonstick spray. A double batch fills three to four 12-cup tins, so bake in rounds if you don't have that many.
  - In a large bowl, whisk the flour, baking powder, baking soda and salt together.
  - Put the eggs, sugar, buttermilk, oil, vanilla, spinach and bananas in a blender. Blend until completely smooth and bright green, with no flecks of spinach left. If your blender is small, blend it in two rounds.
  - Pour the green mixture into the flour mixture and fold it together just until combined. A few small dry streaks are fine. Stirring too much makes dry muffins.
  - Fill the muffin cups about two-thirds full. A #20 cookie scoop (about 3 tablespoons) makes this quick and neat.
  - Bake for 15 to 18 minutes, until the tops spring back when you touch them lightly and a toothpick in the center comes out clean or with a few moist crumbs.
  - Let the muffins cool in the pan for a few minutes, then lift them out onto a rack to cool all the way.
tips:
  - "Matt always makes a double batch, and that's what's listed here. For a single batch (16 to 20 muffins), use 355 g (2½ cups) flour, 2 teaspoons baking powder, ½ teaspoon baking soda, ½ teaspoon salt, 1 egg, ¾ cup (159 g) sugar, ¾ cup buttermilk, ¼ cup oil, 1 teaspoon vanilla, 6 ounces (170 g) spinach and 2 bananas (about 227 g)."
  - White whole wheat flour keeps these light. Regular red whole wheat flour works, but the muffins come out denser.
  - These freeze well. Cool them completely, then pack them in gallon freezer bags for quick breakfasts.
