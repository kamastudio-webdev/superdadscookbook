title: Creamy Potato Salad
description: Classic creamy potato salad with tender potatoes, crunchy celery and pickle, and a tangy sour cream and mustard dressing.
category: Sides
prepMinutes: 15
cookMinutes: 20
servings: 6 servings
date: 2026-11-06
tags: [cookouts, make-ahead, vegetarian]
adaptedFrom:
  name: Inspired Taste
  url: https://www.inspiredtaste.net/22809/simple-potato-salad-recipe/
ingredients:
  - 2 pounds small yellow, red or white potatoes
  - 1 tablespoon apple cider vinegar (wine or rice vinegar also work)
  - ½ cup sour cream
  - ¼ cup mayonnaise
  - 1 tablespoon yellow mustard (or Dijon)
  - ½ medium red onion, finely chopped (about ½ cup)
  - 3 celery stalks, finely chopped (about ½ cup)
  - 1 medium dill pickle, finely chopped (about ⅓ cup)
  - 2 hard-boiled eggs, peeled and chopped (optional)
  - ¼ cup chopped fresh herbs, like parsley, dill or chives
  - Salt and black pepper
steps:
  - Put the potatoes in a large pot and cover them with about 1½ inches of water. Add about 1 teaspoon of salt for every quart of water. Bring to a boil, then lower the heat and simmer 15 to 20 minutes, until a fork slides in easily.
  - While the potatoes cook, cover the chopped onion with cold water and let it soak for 10 minutes, then drain and rinse. This takes the harsh bite out of raw onion.
  - Drain the potatoes and put them straight into a bowl of ice water. When they're cool enough to handle but still a little warm inside, pull off the skins.
  - Cut the potatoes into bite-size chunks and put them in a large bowl. Sprinkle with the vinegar and a little salt.
  - In another bowl, whisk the sour cream, mayonnaise and mustard together.
  - Add the dressing, onion, celery, pickle, eggs and herbs to the potatoes. Fold everything together gently so the potatoes stay in chunks.
  - Season with salt and pepper. If you have time, refrigerate for at least 30 minutes before serving.
