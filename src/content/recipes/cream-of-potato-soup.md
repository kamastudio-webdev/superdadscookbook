---
title: Cream of Potato Soup
description: A simple family potato soup from the Joy of Cooking, with onions and celery softened in butter, simmered in chicken bouillon and thinned with half-and-half.
category: Dinner
prepMinutes: 15
cookMinutes: 35
servings: 4 to 6 servings
date: 2026-11-08
tags: [soup, comfort-food, family-recipe]
ingredients:
  - 4 medium potatoes
  - 4 medium onions
  - 8 ribs celery
  - 3 tablespoons butter, for cooking the vegetables
  - 4 tablespoons butter, to beat in at the end
  - Chicken bouillon (broth), enough to cover the potatoes
  - Half-and-half, to thin the soup
  - Salt and pepper
steps:
  - Peel and chop the onions, and chop the celery.
  - Melt the 3 tablespoons of butter in a large soup pot over medium heat. Add the onions and celery and cook, stirring now and then, for about 5 minutes.
  - While they cook, peel the potatoes and cut them into cubes. Add them to the pot and keep cooking, stirring often, until the onions and celery are soft, about 5 more minutes.
  - Pour in enough chicken bouillon to cover the vegetables. Bring it to a boil and cook until a fork slides easily into the potato cubes, 15 to 20 minutes.
  - Turn off the heat and beat in the 4 tablespoons of butter with a whisk or potato masher, until the butter melts and some of the potatoes break down and thicken the soup.
  - Stir in half-and-half a little at a time until the soup is as thick or thin as you like it. Warm it through without letting it boil, then season with salt and pepper to taste.
tips:
  - Low-sodium broth lets you control the salt. Taste before adding any.
