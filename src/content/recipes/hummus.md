---
title: Hummus
description: Smooth, garlicky hummus with plenty of lemon, made from canned chickpeas in a few minutes. Good with chips, veggies or on a sandwich.
category: Snacks
cuisine: Middle Eastern
prepMinutes: 10
cookMinutes: 0
servings: about 4 cups
date: 2026-11-06
tags: [no-cook, make-ahead, vegetarian]
ingredients:
  - 2 cans (15 oz each) chickpeas, drained
  - ½ cup lemon juice
  - 2 tablespoons minced garlic
  - 1 teaspoon salt
  - 1 cup tahini (256 g)
  - 2 to 4 tablespoons water
  - 1 teaspoon cumin
  - 2 tablespoons olive oil
steps:
  - Put the chickpeas, lemon juice, garlic, salt, tahini, cumin and olive oil in a food processor.
  - Blend until smooth, about 2 minutes, scraping down the sides once or twice.
  - With the processor running, add the water a tablespoon at a time (2 to 4 tablespoons in all) until the hummus is as smooth and creamy as you like it.
  - Taste and add a little more salt or lemon juice if it needs it. Serve right away, or cover and refrigerate for up to 5 days.
