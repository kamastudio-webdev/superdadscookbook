---
title: Chicken Tetrazzini
description: Shredded chicken, golden mushrooms and linguine in a creamy, nutmeg-scented sauce, topped with Parmesan and breadcrumbs and baked until bubbling.
category: Dinner
prepMinutes: 35
cookMinutes: 60
servings: 6 to 8 servings
date: 2026-11-07
tags: [casserole, pasta, chicken, comfort-food]
adaptedFrom:
  name: Food Network (Giada De Laurentiis)
  url: https://www.foodnetwork.com/recipes/giada-de-laurentiis/chicken-tetrazzini-recipe-1943960
ingredients:
  - 9 tablespoons butter, divided
  - 2 tablespoons olive oil, divided
  - 4 boneless, skinless chicken breasts
  - 2¼ teaspoons salt, divided
  - 1¼ teaspoons freshly ground black pepper, divided
  - 1 pound white mushrooms, sliced
  - 1 large onion, finely chopped
  - 5 cloves garlic, minced
  - 1 tablespoon chopped fresh thyme leaves
  - ½ cup dry white wine
  - ⅓ cup all-purpose flour
  - 4 cups whole milk, at room temperature
  - 1 cup heavy whipping cream, at room temperature
  - 1 cup chicken broth
  - ⅛ teaspoon ground nutmeg
  - 12 ounces linguine
  - ¾ cup frozen peas
  - ¼ cup chopped fresh Italian (flat-leaf) parsley
  - 1 cup grated Parmesan
  - ¼ cup dried Italian-style breadcrumbs
steps:
  - Heat the oven to 450°F. Rub 1 tablespoon of the butter over the inside of a 9 × 13-inch baking dish.
  - Set a large, deep nonstick skillet over medium-high heat and melt 1 tablespoon of butter with 1 tablespoon of oil. Season the chicken with ½ teaspoon of the salt and ½ teaspoon of the pepper. Cook until lightly golden and just cooked through, about 4 minutes per side. Move the chicken to a plate to cool for a few minutes, then shred it into bite-size pieces and put it in a large bowl.
  - Add another 1 tablespoon of butter and the last 1 tablespoon of oil to the same skillet. Cook the mushrooms over medium-high heat until the liquid they give off has cooked away and they're golden, about 12 minutes.
  - Stir in the onion, garlic and thyme and cook until the onion is soft and see-through, about 8 minutes. Pour in the wine and let it bubble until it's almost gone, about 2 minutes. Scrape everything into the bowl with the chicken.
  - Turn the heat down to medium-low and melt 3 tablespoons of butter in the skillet. Whisk in the flour and keep whisking for 2 minutes. Slowly whisk in the milk, cream and broth, then the nutmeg and the remaining 1¾ teaspoons salt and ¾ teaspoon pepper.
  - Turn the heat up to high, cover and bring the sauce to a boil. Take the lid off and let it simmer, whisking often, until it thickens a little, about 10 minutes.
  - While the sauce simmers, cook the linguine in a large pot of boiling salted water until tender but still a little firm, about 9 minutes. Drain it.
  - Add the drained linguine, the sauce, the peas and the parsley to the chicken bowl and toss until the pasta is evenly coated.
  - Spread the pasta mixture in the buttered dish. Stir the Parmesan and breadcrumbs together in a small bowl and sprinkle it over the top. Cut the last 3 tablespoons of butter into small pieces and scatter them over the crumbs.
  - Bake uncovered until the top is golden brown and the sauce bubbles around the edges, about 25 minutes.
tips:
  - Set the milk and cream out on the counter while you prep. Room-temperature dairy blends into the butter and flour smoothly instead of clumping.
  - The butter gets used a little at a time, so it helps to cut the 9 tablespoons into pats before you start.
