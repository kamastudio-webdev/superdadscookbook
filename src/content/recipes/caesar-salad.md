---
title: Caesar Salad
description: The family's old-school Caesar, tossed at the table with garlic-steeped olive oil, mashed anchovies, lemon, Parmesan and crunchy homemade croutons.
category: Sides
prepMinutes: 20
cookMinutes: 10
restMinutes: 480
restLabel: Steep garlic oil
servings: 6 servings
date: 2026-11-08
tags: [salad, side-dish, make-ahead]
ingredients:
  - section: Garlic oil
    items:
      - 1 clove garlic, peeled
      - 1 cup olive oil
  - section: Croutons
    items:
      - 4 slices stale bread (French bread is best)
      - ¼ cup of the garlic oil
  - section: Salad
    items:
      - 2 heads romaine lettuce
      - The rest of the garlic oil (about ¾ cup)
      - A dash of cayenne pepper
      - A dash of Tabasco
      - ½ teaspoon sugar
      - 6 anchovy fillets, diced and mashed
      - 1 teaspoon freshly ground black pepper
      - ½ teaspoon salt
      - 1 egg
      - 1 large lemon (about 3 tablespoons juice)
      - About ⅜ cup grated Parmesan cheese (6 tablespoons)
steps:
  - "The night before: crush the garlic clove, drop it into a jar with the olive oil and keep it in the fridge overnight."
  - Pull the leaves off the romaine, wash them well and dry them completely, handling them gently so they don't bruise. Put them in the fridge to get cold and crisp.
  - Strain the garlic out of the oil. Cut the crusts off the bread and cut it into small cubes.
  - Heat ¼ cup of the garlic oil in a skillet over medium heat. Add the bread cubes and keep turning them until they're golden brown on every side, about 5 minutes. Drain them on paper towels and set aside.
  - Pour the rest of the garlic oil back into the jar and add the cayenne, Tabasco, sugar and mashed anchovies. Close the jar and shake it well.
  - Tear the cold romaine into bite-size pieces and put it in a large salad bowl. Sprinkle the pepper and salt over it.
  - Pour the anchovy oil over the lettuce and toss until every leaf looks coated and shiny.
  - "Lower the egg into boiling water for 1 minute, then crack it right over the salad. (This is optional: the family version skips the boiling and adds the egg raw.)"
  - Squeeze the lemon juice over the egg and stir gently. The egg and lemon turn the dressing creamy.
  - Right before serving, scatter the Parmesan and croutons over the top and toss lightly.
tips:
  - This one comes from the family recipe binder, where it's credited to Mary and Vincent Price's A Treasury of Great Recipes.
  - If you're adding the egg raw or barely cooked, use a pasteurized egg, especially when serving little ones or anyone pregnant.
  - Getting the romaine fully dry matters. Wet leaves won't hold the dressing.
  - Toss the salad at the table just before eating, so the croutons stay crunchy.
