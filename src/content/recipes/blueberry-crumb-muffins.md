---
title: Blueberry Crumb Muffins
description: Tender blueberry muffins baked full to the top and finished with a buttery cinnamon-sugar crumb topping.
category: Breakfast
prepMinutes: 15
cookMinutes: 25
servings: 8 muffins
date: 2026-11-08
tags: [baking, muffins, kid-friendly]
adaptedFrom:
  name: Allrecipes
  url: https://www.allrecipes.com/recipe/6865/to-die-for-blueberry-muffins/
ingredients:
  - section: Muffins
    items:
      - 1½ cups all-purpose flour
      - ¾ cup white sugar
      - ½ teaspoon salt
      - 2 teaspoons baking powder
      - ⅓ cup vegetable oil
      - 1 egg
      - About ⅓ cup milk
      - 1 cup fresh blueberries
  - section: Crumb topping
    items:
      - ½ cup white sugar
      - ⅓ cup all-purpose flour
      - ¼ cup butter, cut into cubes
      - 1½ teaspoons ground cinnamon
steps:
  - Heat the oven to 400°F. Grease a muffin pan or line the cups with paper liners.
  - "Make the crumb topping first: in a small bowl, mash the sugar, flour, butter cubes and cinnamon together with a fork until it looks like coarse, sandy crumbs. Set it aside."
  - In a large bowl, whisk the flour, sugar, salt and baking powder.
  - Pour the oil into a 1-cup liquid measuring cup and crack the egg into it. Add milk until the liquid reaches the 1-cup line, then beat it with a fork.
  - Pour the egg mixture into the flour mixture and stir just until there's no dry flour left. A few lumps are fine. Gently fold in the blueberries.
  - Spoon the batter into the cups, filling each one right to the top. Sprinkle the crumb topping evenly over the muffins.
  - Bake for 20 to 25 minutes, until the tops are golden and a toothpick in the center of a muffin comes out clean.
  - Let the muffins cool in the pan for 5 minutes, then move them to a rack.
tips:
  - For big bakery-style muffins, double the batch and fill the cups all the way to the rim. You can also add extra blueberries.
  - Don't overmix after adding the milk mixture, or the muffins turn out tough.
