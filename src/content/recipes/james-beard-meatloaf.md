ingredients:
  - 2 pounds ground beef
  - 1 pound ground pork
  - 2 garlic cloves, finely chopped
  - 1 large onion, finely chopped
  - 1 teaspoon salt
  - 1 teaspoon pepper
  - 1 bay leaf, crumbled
  - ½ teaspoon dried thyme leaves, crumbled
  - 1 tablespoon finely chopped green pepper
  - ½ cup bread crumbs
  - 2 eggs
  - Sliced bacon or salt pork, enough to cover the bottom of the pan plus 2 to 4 more slices
  - A little butter, melted, for brushing
steps:
  - Heat the oven to 325°F.
  - Put everything except the bacon and butter in a large bowl. Work it together with your hands, squeezing and kneading, until it's completely blended and you can't see any streaks of plain meat.
  - Lay enough bacon or salt pork slices in the bottom of a baking pan to make a bed the size of the loaf.
  - Shape the meat into one long loaf, pressing it firmly so it holds together, and set it on the bacon.
  - Brush the top with melted butter and lay 2 to 4 more slices of bacon across it.
  - Bake for 1½ to 1¾ hours, spooning the pan drippings over the loaf every 20 to 30 minutes, until it's cooked through and reads 160°F in the center.
  - Let the meatloaf sit for 10 to 15 minutes before slicing so it holds together.
tips:
  - Press the meat firmly when you shape it. A tightly packed loaf slices cleanly instead of crumbling.
