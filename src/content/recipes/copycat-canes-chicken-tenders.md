ingredients:
  - section: Chicken
    items:
      - 3 boneless, skinless chicken breasts, cut into strips
      - 4 to 5 cups canola oil, for frying
  - section: Marinade
    items:
      - 1 cup buttermilk
      - 1 egg
      - 1 tablespoon garlic powder
  - section: Seasoning blend
    items:
      - 1 tablespoon black pepper
      - 1 tablespoon kosher salt
      - 1 tablespoon smoked paprika
      - 1 tablespoon garlic powder
      - 1 tablespoon onion powder
  - section: Flour mix
    items:
      - 1½ cups flour
      - 1 tablespoon cornstarch
      - 1 teaspoon baking soda
      - 1 cup Italian bread crumbs
      - All of the seasoning blend except 1 tablespoon
  - section: Dredge
    items:
      - ¾ cup buttermilk
      - The 1 tablespoon of seasoning blend you saved
  - section: Dipping sauce
    items:
      - ½ cup mayonnaise (104 g)
      - ¼ cup ketchup (68 g)
      - 1 teaspoon Worcestershire sauce
      - ½ teaspoon garlic powder
      - ½ teaspoon black pepper
      - 1 tablespoon white vinegar
steps:
  - Whisk the marinade ingredients together, add the chicken strips, cover and refrigerate for at least 2 hours.
  - Mix the seasoning blend. Set aside 1 tablespoon for the dredge, and stir the rest into the flour mix.
  - Whisk the dredge buttermilk with the saved tablespoon of seasoning. Set up a line of flour mix, then the buttermilk dredge, then the flour mix again, with a wire rack at the end.
  - In a large, heavy pot or deep skillet, heat the oil over medium-high heat to 350°F.
  - Lift each strip out of the marinade and coat it in the flour mix, dip it in the buttermilk, then coat it in the flour mix again. Set it on the rack.
  - Fry in small batches so the pot isn't crowded, 3 to 4 minutes per side, until deep golden and the chicken reaches 165°F inside. Drain on a clean rack or paper towels.
  - Stir the dipping sauce ingredients together and serve with the warm tenders.
tips:
  - Keep the oil close to 350°F with a thermometer. Too hot and the breading burns before the chicken cooks through; too cool and the tenders come out greasy.
