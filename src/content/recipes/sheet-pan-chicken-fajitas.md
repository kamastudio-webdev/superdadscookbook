title: Sheet-Pan Chicken Fajitas
description: Everything roasts on one pan, so dinner takes about 15 minutes of hands-on time and there's only one pan to wash.

ingredients:
  - 1½ pounds boneless, skinless chicken breasts
  - 3 bell peppers, any colors, sliced into strips
  - 1 large onion, sliced
  - 3 tablespoons olive oil
  - 2 teaspoons chili powder
  - 1 teaspoon ground cumin
  - 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
  - 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  - 1 teaspoon salt
  - ½ teaspoon black pepper
  - 1 lime
  - 8 small flour tortillas
  - Sour cream, salsa, shredded cheese and sliced avocado, for serving
steps:
  - Heat the oven to 425°F. Line a large rimmed baking sheet with foil or parchment paper for easy cleanup.
  - Slice the chicken into strips about ½ inch thick. Thinner strips cook faster and stay juicy.
  - In a small bowl, mix the chili powder, cumin, smoked paprika, garlic powder, salt and pepper.
  - Put the chicken, peppers and onion on the baking sheet. Drizzle with the olive oil, sprinkle with the spice mix, and toss with your hands until everything is coated. Spread it out in a single layer.
  - Roast for 20 to 25 minutes, stirring once halfway through, until the peppers are tender and the chicken reaches 165°F in the thickest piece.
  - Squeeze the lime over the pan. Wrap the tortillas in a damp paper towel and microwave for 30 seconds, then let everyone build their own at the table.
tips:
  - If the pan looks crowded, use two pans. Crowded vegetables steam instead of browning.
  - "For kids who don't like spice: before tossing, push a few chicken strips to one corner and season them with just salt and garlic powder."
  - Leftovers make great quesadillas the next day.
