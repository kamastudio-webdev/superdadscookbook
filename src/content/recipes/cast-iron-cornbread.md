---
title: Cast Iron Cornbread
description: Sweet, buttery cornbread baked in a well-buttered cast iron skillet, with a sprinkle of salt on top for crispy, golden edges.
category: Breads
prepMinutes: 10
cookMinutes: 25
servings: 1 (12-inch) skillet
date: 2026-11-06
tags: [cast-iron, side-dish, vegetarian]
ingredients:
  - 1⅓ cups flour
  - 1⅓ cups cornmeal
  - ¾ cup sugar
  - 2 tablespoons baking powder
  - 1 stick (½ cup) butter, melted, plus more for the pan
  - 1½ cups milk
  - 2 eggs
  - Salt, for the top
steps:
  - Preheat the oven to 400°F.
  - Mix all the ingredients together in a large bowl.
  - Butter a 12-inch cast iron skillet well.
  - Pour in the batter and sprinkle the top with salt.
  - Bake for 20 to 25 minutes, until golden and a toothpick in the center comes out clean.
