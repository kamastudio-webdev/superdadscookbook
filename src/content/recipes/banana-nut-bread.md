---
title: Banana Nut Bread
description: A tender, cinnamon-scented loaf made with oil and very ripe bananas, studded with toasted walnuts.
category: Breads
prepMinutes: 20
cookMinutes: 60
restMinutes: 10
restLabel: Cool
servings: 1 loaf (9 × 5 inches)
date: 2026-11-07
tags: [baking, quick-bread, bananas, make-ahead]
adaptedFrom:
  name: Food & Wine
  url: https://www.foodandwine.com/recipes/banana-nut-bread
ingredients:
  - ¾ cup coarsely chopped walnuts (3 ounces)
  - 1¼ cups unbleached all-purpose flour
  - 1 teaspoon baking soda
  - ½ teaspoon cinnamon
  - ½ teaspoon kosher salt
  - 1 cup sugar
  - 2 large eggs
  - ½ cup canola oil
  - 3 medium very ripe bananas, mashed (about 1¼ cups)
  - 1 teaspoon pure vanilla extract
  - Cooking spray, for the pan
steps:
  - Heat the oven to 350°F and spray a 9 × 5-inch loaf pan with cooking spray.
  - Spread the walnuts in a pie plate and toast them in the oven for 5 to 8 minutes, until they smell nutty. Set them aside to cool.
  - In a medium bowl, whisk the flour, baking soda, cinnamon and salt together.
  - In a large bowl, beat the sugar, eggs and oil with a mixer on high speed until pale and creamy. Add the mashed bananas and vanilla and beat until smooth.
  - Stir the flour mixture into the banana mixture until no dry streaks remain, then fold in the toasted walnuts.
  - Scrape the batter into the pan and bake on the middle rack for 50 to 60 minutes, until the top springs back when pressed and a toothpick in the center comes out clean.
  - Let the loaf cool in the pan for 10 minutes, then turn it out onto a rack and let it cool all the way.
tips:
  - Wrap the cooled loaf tightly in plastic wrap. It keeps in the fridge for up to 5 days or in the freezer for up to 2 months.
  - The browner and spottier the bananas, the sweeter and more flavorful the bread.
