ingredients:
  - 1½ cups butter, at room temperature
  - 8 ounces cream cheese, at room temperature
  - 3½ cups granulated sugar, plus 10 tablespoons for rolling
  - 2 eggs
  - 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
  - 4 teaspoons lemon extract
  - 4 tablespoons lemon juice
  - 4 tablespoons lemon zest
  - 2 teaspoons salt
  - 1 teaspoon baking soda
  - 6 cups flour
  - 2 cups white chocolate chips
  - 2 cups fresh blueberries
steps:
  - Line a few baking sheets with parchment paper or grease them.
  - Beat the butter, cream cheese and 3½ cups of the sugar for about 2 minutes, until light and fluffy. Add the eggs, vanilla, lemon extract, lemon juice and zest, and beat 1 more minute.
  - By hand, stir in the salt, baking soda and flour just until no dry flour is left. Gently fold in the white chocolate chips and blueberries, trying not to crush the berries.
  - Put the remaining 10 tablespoons of sugar in a small bowl. Scoop the dough into balls about ¼ cup each and roll them in the sugar.
  - Freeze the dough balls for at least 1 hour. Don't skip this step, or the cookies spread out flat in the oven.
  - Heat the oven to 350°F. Bake 13 to 16 minutes, just until the edges start to turn golden. Let the cookies rest on the pan for 10 minutes before moving them to a cooling rack.
tips:
  - Zest only the yellow part of the lemon. The white layer underneath is bitter.
  - Frozen blueberries work in a pinch. Fold them in while still frozen, and expect some purple streaks.
  - For a single batch of about 20 cookies, cut every amount in half.
  - If you make smaller cookies, check them a few minutes early.
