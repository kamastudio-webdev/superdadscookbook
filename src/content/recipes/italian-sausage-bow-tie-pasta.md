ingredients:
  - 1 package (12 ounces) bow tie pasta
  - 2 tablespoons olive oil
  - 1 pound mild Italian sausage, casings removed
  - ½ teaspoon red pepper flakes (optional)
  - ½ cup diced onion
  - 4 garlic cloves, minced
  - 1 can (28 ounces) Italian-style plum tomatoes, drained and roughly chopped
  - 1½ cups heavy cream
  - 1 cup packed spinach leaves, sliced into thin ribbons
  - Minced fresh parsley, for topping (optional)
steps:
  - Bring a large pot of water to a boil and cook the bow ties according to the package. Drain and set aside.
  - While the pasta cooks, heat the olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the sausage and red pepper flakes and cook, breaking the meat into crumbles with a spoon, until it's browned with no pink left, about 8 minutes.
  - Add the onion and garlic and cook, stirring, until the onion is soft, about 3 to 4 minutes.
  - Stir in the chopped tomatoes and the cream. Let it simmer, stirring now and then, until the sauce thickens, about 10 minutes.
  - Add the spinach and cook 3 minutes more, until it wilts.
  - Stir the drained pasta into the sauce until it's coated. Sprinkle with parsley if you like and serve.
tips:
  - Any Italian sausage works. Mild keeps it kid-friendly, but sweet, hot or turkey sausage are all good swaps.
  - If your sausage comes in links, slit the casings with a knife and squeeze the meat out.
  - Leftovers reheat well and make a good next-day lunch.
