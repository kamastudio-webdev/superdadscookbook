title: Mary Kulp's Macaroni Salad
description: Mary Kulp's recipe (Father John Kulp's mother). A cold, creamy macaroni salad with celery and carrot in a mayo and sour cream dressing.
category: Sides
prepMinutes: 20
cookMinutes: 10
servings: 1 big bowl
date: 2026-11-08
tags: [side-dish, pasta-salad, cookout, family-recipe]
draft: true
ingredients:
  - Elbow macaroni
  - Celery, finely diced
  - Carrots, grated
  - Onion, minced (optional)
  - Salt and pepper
  - Mayonnaise and sour cream, 2 parts mayonnaise to 1 part sour cream
  - A little milk
  - Minced fresh parsley
steps:
  - Cook the macaroni in salted boiling water according to the package directions. Drain it, rinse it under cold water until it's cool, and let it drain well.
  - Put the macaroni in a large bowl and add the celery, carrots and onion, if you're using it. Season with salt and pepper.
  - In a small bowl, stir together 2 parts mayonnaise to 1 part sour cream. Add a little milk, a spoonful at a time, until the dressing is smooth and loose enough to coat the pasta easily.
  - Stir the dressing into the macaroni mixture. Sprinkle the parsley over the top and mix well.
  - Cover and refrigerate until you're ready to serve.
  - "TO DO: amounts. The card gives no quantities: how much macaroni (a 1 lb box?), how much celery, carrot and onion, and how much dressing (for example 1 cup mayo to ½ cup sour cream), plus how many it serves."
tips:
  - Rinsing the cooked macaroni in cold water stops the cooking and keeps it from soaking up all the dressing.
