---
title: Spinach Banana Muffins
description: Bright green banana-oat muffins with a big handful of spinach blended right into the batter. This batch makes two dozen, and they keep well in the fridge or freezer.
category: Breakfast
prepMinutes: 10
cookMinutes: 20
servings: 24 muffins
date: 2026-11-07
tags: [kid-friendly, make-ahead, freezer-friendly, lunchbox]
adaptedFrom:
  name: Yummy Toddler Food
  url: https://www.yummytoddlerfood.com/super-green-banana-spinach-muffins/
ingredients:
  - 2 cups sliced ripe banana, about 300 g (4 small or 2 medium-large bananas)
  - 4 cups baby spinach, lightly packed, about 120 g
  - 1½ cups milk (dairy, or unsweetened plain nondairy milk)
  - ½ cup honey
  - 4 tablespoons butter, melted and cooled (or neutral oil or plain yogurt)
  - 4 eggs
  - 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
  - 2 cups rolled oats
  - 2 teaspoons baking soda
  - ¼ teaspoon salt
  - 2 cups whole-wheat flour
  - Mini chocolate chips (optional)
steps:
  - Heat the oven to 375°F. Grease two 12-cup standard muffin tins well with nonstick spray.
  - Put everything except the flour and chocolate chips in the blender. That's the banana, spinach, milk, honey, butter, eggs, vanilla, oats, baking soda and salt.
  - Blend until completely smooth, stopping to scrape down the sides as needed. The batter should be evenly green like a smoothie, with no flecks of spinach.
  - Add the flour and pulse a few times just until it's mixed in, or stir it in gently by hand.
  - Fill each muffin cup about ¾ full. Sprinkle a few mini chocolate chips on top if you're using them.
  - Bake for 18 to 20 minutes, until the tops feel firm and the edges are lightly browned. A toothpick poked into the center should come out clean.
  - Run a paring knife around any muffins that stick, then move them to a wire rack to cool. Serve warm, at room temperature or cold.
tips:
  - Matt's copy is doubled. For 12 muffins, use half of everything (1 cup banana, 2 cups spinach, ¾ cup milk, ¼ cup honey, 2 tablespoons butter, 2 eggs, 1 teaspoon vanilla, 1 cup oats, 1 teaspoon baking soda, ⅛ teaspoon salt and 1 cup flour).
  - Weighing is easiest. Matt figures about 150 g of sliced banana and 30 g of spinach per cup.
  - This is a lot of batter for one blender. If yours is smaller than a Vitamix, blend it in two batches.
  - Keep the muffins in the fridge, or freeze them and pull out a few at a time.
