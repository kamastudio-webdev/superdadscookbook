---
title: Simple Burger Buns
description: A quick, soft yeast dough you can make in a bread machine or a stand mixer. Shape it into 11 or 12 burger buns, or roll it out and stuff it to make Burger Pockets.
category: Breads
prepMinutes: 20
cookMinutes: 12
restMinutes: 60
restLabel: Rise
servings: 11 to 12 buns
date: 2026-11-19
tags: [bread, buns, yeast, bread-machine]
ingredients:
  - section: Dough
    items:
      - ¾ cup milk
      - ½ cup hot tap water
      - 2¼ teaspoons yeast (1 packet), active dry or instant
      - 1 tablespoon sugar
      - 1 large egg
      - 3⅓ cups flour (all-purpose or bread flour)
      - 1½ teaspoons salt
  - section: Topping (optional)
    items:
      - 1 large egg, beaten
      - Sesame seeds, for sprinkling
steps:
  - "With a bread machine: put all the dough ingredients in the pan, in the order your machine's manual lists, and run the dough cycle. When it finishes, skip ahead to shaping."
  - "With a stand mixer: stir the milk, hot water, yeast and sugar together in the mixer bowl. Let it sit for 10 minutes, until it looks foamy."
  - Add the egg and salt, then add the flour one cup at a time with the dough hook running on low. Knead for about 5 minutes. The dough should be smooth and a little tacky, but not so sticky that you can't handle it.
  - Cover the bowl and let the dough rise for 1 hour.
  - Heat the oven to 400°F. Divide the dough into 11 or 12 equal pieces. For buns, roll each piece into a smooth ball, set them on a greased or parchment-lined baking sheet and press them down a little. For Burger Pockets, roll the pieces out into circles instead and follow that recipe.
  - If you like, brush the buns with the beaten egg and sprinkle them with sesame seeds.
  - Bake for 10 to 12 minutes, until golden brown.
tips:
  - Use hot tap water, not boiling. Mixed with the cold milk, it ends up just warm, which is what the yeast likes.
  - This recipe comes from the Sunset Highway Farm bread class, where the same dough is used for both buns and stuffed burger pockets.
  - Want bigger buns? The site's Hamburger Buns recipe makes about 6 large ones.
