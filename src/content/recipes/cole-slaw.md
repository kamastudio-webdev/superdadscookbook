---
title: Cole Slaw
description: A crunchy red and green cabbage slaw in a sweet, tangy dressing with a little kick from horseradish, mustard and cayenne.
category: Sides
prepMinutes: 20
cookMinutes: 0
restMinutes: 30
restLabel: Chill
servings: 8 servings
date: 2026-11-08
tags: [side-dish, no-cook, make-ahead, cookout]
adaptedFrom:
  name: Emeril Lagasse
  url: https://www.emerils.com/125459/kicked-cole-slaw
ingredients:
  - section: Dressing
    items:
      - ¾ cup mayonnaise
      - ¼ cup mustard (1½ teaspoons coarse-ground mustard, the rest Dijon)
      - ¼ cup packed light brown sugar
      - 3 tablespoons apple cider vinegar
      - 2 tablespoons buttermilk or sour cream
      - 1 tablespoon prepared horseradish
      - 1½ teaspoons celery seeds
      - 1 teaspoon salt
      - ¼ teaspoon freshly ground black pepper
      - ⅛ teaspoon cayenne
  - section: Slaw
    items:
      - 3 cups shredded green cabbage (about ½ head)
      - 3 cups shredded red cabbage (about ½ head)
      - 1 green bell pepper, finely diced
      - 1 large carrot, peeled and shredded
      - ½ cup grated yellow onion
      - ¼ cup minced fresh parsley
steps:
  - Make the dressing first. In a medium bowl, whisk the mayonnaise, both mustards, brown sugar, vinegar, buttermilk or sour cream, horseradish, celery seeds, salt, pepper and cayenne. Keep whisking for about a minute, until the sugar no longer feels gritty.
  - Put the green and red cabbage, bell pepper, carrot, onion and parsley in a large bowl.
  - Pour the dressing over the vegetables and toss until every shred is coated. Taste it and add a pinch more salt, pepper or cayenne if you like.
  - Cover the bowl and put it in the fridge for about 30 minutes, so the slaw gets cold and the flavors come together. Toss once more before serving.
tips:
  - Matt's version adds horseradish, gives a sour cream option and uses a mix of coarse-ground and Dijon mustard.
  - "Half batch: ⅓ cup mayo, 2 tablespoons mustard (¾ teaspoon coarse-ground, the rest Dijon), 2 tablespoons brown sugar, 1½ tablespoons vinegar, 1 tablespoon buttermilk or sour cream, ½ tablespoon horseradish, ¾ teaspoon celery seeds, ½ teaspoon salt, ⅛ teaspoon pepper, a sprinkle of cayenne, 1½ cups each of green and red cabbage, ½ bell pepper, ½ carrot, ¼ cup onion and 2 tablespoons parsley."
  - Grate the onion on the small holes of a box grater so it melts into the dressing instead of leaving big raw bites.
