---
title: Beer Cheese Spread
description: A sharp, smooth cheddar spread blended with flat beer, garlic juice, dry mustard and a dash of cayenne. A family Christmas gift since 1985.
category: Snacks
prepMinutes: 15
cookMinutes: 0
servings: About 1½ cups
date: 2026-11-08
tags: [spread, appetizer, christmas, gift, family-recipe]
ingredients:
  - ½ lb cheddar cheese, grated (about 2 cups)
  - 1 teaspoon garlic juice
  - A dash of cayenne pepper
  - ½ teaspoon dry mustard
  - ½ cup flat, stale beer
steps:
  - Open the beer ahead of time and let it sit out until it goes flat, so it doesn't foam up when you blend it.
  - Put the grated cheddar, garlic juice, cayenne and dry mustard in a food processor or a mixing bowl.
  - Blend, pouring in the beer a little at a time, until the spread is completely smooth with no bits of cheese left. Scrape down the sides once or twice.
  - Pack it into a crock or a jar with a lid. Keep it in the warmest spot in the fridge, like the door or the top shelf, so it stays soft enough to spread.
tips:
  - Spooned into little crocks or jars, it makes a nice Christmas present for friends and co-workers.
  - Garlic juice comes bottled and is usually in the spice aisle.
  - Let the cheese sit out for a few minutes before blending. Slightly softened cheese turns smooth faster.
