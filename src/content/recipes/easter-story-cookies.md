---
title: Easter Story Cookies
description: A family Easter tradition. Kids help make pecan meringue cookies the night before Easter, with a Bible reading at each step, and in the morning the cookies are hollow, like the empty tomb.
category: Dessert
prepMinutes: 30
cookMinutes: 0
restMinutes: 480
restLabel: Overnight in the oven
servings: 1 cookie sheet
date: 2026-11-08
tags: [easter, holiday, kid-friendly, family-recipe]
ingredients:
  - section: Cookies
    items:
      - 1 cup whole pecans
      - 1 teaspoon vinegar
      - 3 egg whites
      - Pinch of salt
      - 1 cup sugar
  - section: You'll also need
    items:
      - A zipper-top bag
      - A wooden spoon
      - Wax paper
      - Tape
      - A Bible
steps:
  - Make these the evening before Easter. Heat the oven to 300°F and cover a cookie sheet with wax paper.
  - Put the pecans in the zipper bag and let the kids pound them with the wooden spoon until they're broken into small pieces. Explain that after Jesus was arrested, the Roman soldiers beat Him. Read John 19:1-3.
  - Let each child smell the vinegar, then put 1 teaspoon of it in the mixing bowl. Explain that when Jesus was thirsty on the cross, He was given vinegar to drink. Read John 19:28-30.
  - Add the egg whites to the vinegar. Eggs stand for life, and Jesus gave His life so that we could have life. Read John 10:10-11.
  - Sprinkle a little salt into each child's hand. Let them taste it, then brush the rest into the bowl. The salt stands for the salty tears of Jesus' followers, and for the bitterness of our own sin. Read Luke 23:27.
  - So far, nothing in the bowl sounds very tasty! Add the sugar. The sweetest part of the story is that Jesus died because He loves us, and He wants us to know Him and belong to Him. Read Psalm 34:8 and John 3:16.
  - Beat with a mixer on high speed for 12 to 15 minutes, until the whites stand up in stiff peaks. The white color stands for the purity of everyone whose sins have been washed clean by Jesus. Read Isaiah 1:18 and John 3:1-3.
  - Gently fold in the pecans. Drop the mixture by teaspoonfuls onto the wax paper. Each mound is like the rocky tomb where Jesus' body was laid. Read Matthew 27:57-60.
  - Put the cookie sheet in the oven, close the door and turn the oven OFF. Give each child a piece of tape to seal the oven door shut, the way Jesus' tomb was sealed. Read Matthew 27:65-66.
  - Go to bed! The kids may feel sad about leaving the cookies in the oven all night. Jesus' followers felt hopeless when the tomb was sealed. Read John 16:20 and 22.
  - On Easter morning, open the oven and give everyone a cookie. Look at the cracked tops, then take a bite. The cookies are hollow! On the first Easter, Jesus' followers were amazed to find the tomb open and empty. Read Matthew 28:1-9.
tips:
  - Egg whites whip best in a clean, dry bowl with no trace of yolk.
  - Don't open the oven before morning. The cookies finish drying in the leftover heat.
