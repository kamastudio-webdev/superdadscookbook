description: A restaurant-style chicken pot pie from St. Louis's Painted Giraffe, with poached chicken and vegetables in a creamy sauce, baked in individual bowls under puff pastry.

ingredients:
  - 6½ cups rich chicken stock or broth
  - 6 boneless, skinless chicken breasts (about 6 ounces each), diced
  - 1 stick (½ cup) butter
  - 2½ cups diced onions
  - 2¼ cups diced carrots
  - 1¾ cups diced celery
  - ½ pound medium mushrooms, quartered
  - 1 cup all-purpose flour
  - 1 bay leaf
  - 1 teaspoon salt
  - ¼ teaspoon white pepper (or ½ teaspoon black pepper)
  - ¾ cup heavy whipping cream, warmed
  - Store-bought puff pastry or pie dough
  - 2 egg whites
steps:
  - Heat the oven to 375°F.
  - Bring the stock to a simmer in a medium saucepan. Add the diced chicken and poach it just until it's cooked through with no pink in the middle. Lift the chicken out with a slotted spoon and set it aside. Keep the stock for the sauce.
  - Melt the butter in a large pot (about 10 quarts) over medium heat. Add the onions, carrots and celery and cook for a couple of minutes. Add the mushrooms and keep cooking until the onions turn see-through, without letting anything brown.
  - Sprinkle in the flour and stir it in gently. Turn the heat to low and cook for 6 to 8 minutes, stirring now and then and scraping the bottom with a wooden spoon so the flour doesn't scorch. Stir gently so the vegetables don't break apart.
  - Turn the heat up. Add the saved stock in three parts, whisking well after each one so no lumps form and letting it come back to a gentle simmer before adding the next.
  - Add the bay leaf, salt and pepper and cook over low heat for about 10 minutes. Stir in the poached chicken and the warm cream, then fish out the bay leaf.
  - Spoon the filling evenly into 6 ovenproof bowls or casserole dishes (16 ounces each). Lay a piece of puff pastry or pie dough over each one.
  - Whisk the egg whites with a splash of water and brush it over the pastry. Set the bowls on a sheet pan and bake until the tops are puffed and golden brown, usually 20 to 25 minutes.
  - Let them cool for 5 to 10 minutes before serving. The filling stays very hot.
tips:
  - Warm the cream before adding it so the sauce stays smooth.
  - This is a chef's restaurant recipe, so the sauce is generous. Taste it before topping with pastry and add more salt if needed.
