---
title: Lemon Yogurt Cake
description: A moist, tangy lemon loaf made with whole-milk yogurt and oil, soaked with warm lemon syrup and finished with a sweet lemon glaze.
category: Dessert
prepMinutes: 20
cookMinutes: 50
restMinutes: 60
restLabel: Cool
servings: 8 slices (1 loaf)
date: 2026-11-08
tags: [lemon, loaf-cake, baking]
adaptedFrom:
  name: Food Network (Ina Garten)
  url: https://www.foodnetwork.com/recipes/ina-garten/lemon-yogurt-cake-recipe-1947092
ingredients:
  - section: Cake
    items:
      - 1½ cups all-purpose flour
      - 2 teaspoons baking powder
      - ½ teaspoon kosher salt
      - 1 cup plain whole-milk yogurt
      - 1 cup sugar
      - 3 extra-large eggs
      - 2 teaspoons grated lemon zest (from about 2 lemons)
      - ½ teaspoon pure vanilla extract
      - ½ cup vegetable oil
  - section: Lemon syrup
    items:
      - ⅓ cup sugar
      - ⅓ cup fresh lemon juice
  - section: Glaze
    items:
      - 1 cup powdered sugar
      - 2 tablespoons fresh lemon juice
steps:
  - Heat the oven to 350°F. Grease an 8½ x 4¼ x 2½-inch loaf pan, line the bottom with a strip of parchment paper, then grease and flour the whole pan.
  - Sift the flour, baking powder and salt together into a bowl.
  - In a larger bowl, whisk the yogurt, 1 cup of sugar, eggs, lemon zest and vanilla until smooth.
  - Whisk the flour mixture into the yogurt mixture a little at a time, just until combined. Then fold in the oil with a rubber spatula until you can't see any streaks of it.
  - Scrape the batter into the pan and bake for about 50 minutes, until a toothpick poked into the center comes out clean.
  - While the cake bakes, warm the ⅓ cup of sugar and ⅓ cup of lemon juice in a small saucepan, stirring, until the sugar dissolves and the syrup turns clear. Set it aside.
  - Let the cake cool in the pan for 10 minutes. Turn it out onto a cooling rack set over a sheet pan to catch drips.
  - While the cake is still warm, slowly pour the lemon syrup over the top and let it soak in. Let the cake cool completely.
  - Whisk the powdered sugar with the 2 tablespoons of lemon juice until smooth, and pour it over the cooled cake.
tips:
  - Zest the lemons before you juice them. It's much easier.
  - A 9 x 5-inch loaf pan works too. The loaf will be a little flatter, so start checking it a few minutes early.
