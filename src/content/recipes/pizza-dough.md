---
title: Pizza Dough
description: A big batch of stand-mixer pizza dough made with bread flour and olive oil. Draft until the rise time, number of pizzas and baking steps are added.
category: Breads
prepMinutes: 20
cookMinutes: 15
servings: several pizzas
date: 2026-11-06
draft: true
tags: [pizza-night, make-ahead]
ingredients:
  - 1000 g bread flour
  - 570 ml warm water
  - 8 g yeast (2 teaspoons)
  - 1 teaspoon sugar
  - 15 g salt
  - 30 g olive oil
steps:
  - Stir the warm water, yeast and sugar together. Let it sit for 5 minutes.
  - Put the flour and salt in the mixer bowl.
  - Add the yeast water and mix.
  - Add the olive oil and mix for 10 minutes on speed 3.
  - "TO DO: rise time, how many pizzas it makes, oven temperature and bake time."
tips:
  - "For a 1½ batch: 1500 g bread flour, 855 ml warm water, 12 g yeast, 1½ teaspoons sugar, 22 g salt and 45 g olive oil."
  - "No kitchen scale? The base batch is roughly 8 cups bread flour, 2⅓ cups water, 2½ teaspoons fine salt and 2 tablespoons olive oil. Weighing is more accurate."
