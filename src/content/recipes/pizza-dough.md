---
title: Pizza Dough
description: A big batch of stand-mixer pizza dough made with bread flour and olive oil. It rises overnight in the fridge and makes 2 to 3 large pizzas.
category: Breads
prepMinutes: 20
cookMinutes: 15
restMinutes: 720
restLabel: Rise
servings: 2 to 3 large pizzas
date: 2026-11-06
tags: [pizza-night, make-ahead]
ingredients:
  - 1000 g bread flour
  - 570 ml warm water
  - 8 g yeast (2 teaspoons)
  - 1 teaspoon sugar
  - 15 g salt
  - 30 g olive oil
steps:
  - Stir the warm water, yeast and sugar together. Let it sit for 5 minutes.
  - Put the flour and salt in the stand mixer bowl.
  - Add the yeast water and mix with the dough hook.
  - Add the olive oil and mix for 10 minutes on speed 3.
  - Shape the dough into a ball and put it in a large oiled bowl or container. Cover it tightly and let it rise in the fridge overnight.
  - About 1 to 2 hours before baking, take the dough out and divide it into 2 or 3 pieces. Shape each piece into a ball, cover, and let them warm up on the counter so they stretch easily.
  - Stretch each ball into a large pizza and add your toppings. Bake at 475°F to 500°F for about 10 to 15 minutes, until the crust is browned and the cheese is bubbling.
tips:
  - Plan ahead and make the dough the night before pizza night.
  - Two pieces make bigger, thicker crusts. Three make thinner ones.
  - "For a 1½ batch: 1500 g bread flour, 855 ml warm water, 12 g yeast, 1½ teaspoons sugar, 22 g salt and 45 g olive oil."
  - "No kitchen scale? The base batch is roughly 8 cups bread flour, 2⅓ cups water, 2½ teaspoons fine salt and 2 tablespoons olive oil. Weighing is more accurate."
