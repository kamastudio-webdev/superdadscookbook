---
title: No-Cook Pizza Sauce
description: A sweet and savory pizza sauce stirred together from tomato paste, Parmesan, honey and dried herbs. No simmering, just a 30-minute rest.
category: Basics
prepMinutes: 10
cookMinutes: 0
restMinutes: 30
restLabel: Rest
servings: 2 large pizzas (about 1½ cups)
date: 2026-11-07
tags: [pizza-night, sauce, no-cook]
adaptedFrom:
  name: Allrecipes
  url: https://www.allrecipes.com/recipe/17319/exquisite-pizza-sauce/
ingredients:
  - 1 can (6 ounces) tomato paste
  - ¾ cup warm water (6 fluid ounces, about 110°F)
  - 3 tablespoons grated Parmesan cheese
  - 2 tablespoons honey
  - 1 teaspoon minced garlic
  - 1 teaspoon anchovy paste
  - ¾ teaspoon onion powder
  - ¼ teaspoon dried oregano
  - ¼ teaspoon dried marjoram
  - ¼ teaspoon dried basil
  - ¼ teaspoon ground black pepper
  - ⅛ teaspoon cayenne pepper
  - ⅛ teaspoon dried red pepper flakes
  - Salt to taste
steps:
  - Put everything in a medium bowl.
  - Stir until the sauce is smooth and evenly mixed, pressing out any lumps of Parmesan against the side of the bowl.
  - Let the sauce sit for 30 minutes so the garlic and herbs can flavor it. Then spread it over your pizza dough and add your toppings.
tips:
  - "Matt's amounts for one 16-inch large pizza: about 7 oz of sauce (a little over ¾ cup), 10 oz of shredded cheese (about 2½ cups), 5¼ oz of cooked sausage and 1¼ oz of pepperoni."
  - One batch is enough for about 2 large pizzas. For 3, make 1½ batches.
