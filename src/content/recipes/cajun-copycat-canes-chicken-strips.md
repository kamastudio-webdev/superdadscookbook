ingredients:
  - section: Chicken and marinade
    items:
      - 3 chicken breasts, cut into strips (or 1½ pounds chicken strips)
      - 1 cup buttermilk
      - 1 tablespoon Cajun or Creole seasoning
  - section: Breading
    items:
      - 1½ cups flour
      - 1 tablespoon cornstarch
      - 1 teaspoon baking soda
      - 1 cup breadcrumbs, seasoned or plain
  - section: Buttermilk dip
    items:
      - ¾ cup buttermilk
      - ½ tablespoon (1½ teaspoons) Cajun or Creole seasoning
  - section: For frying
    items:
      - 4 cups oil
  - section: Dipping sauce
    items:
      - ½ cup mayonnaise
      - ¼ cup ketchup
      - ¼ teaspoon Worcestershire sauce
      - ½ teaspoon ground black pepper
      - ½ teaspoon garlic powder
      - ¼ teaspoon Creole seasoning
steps:
  - Put the chicken strips in a zip-top bag with the 1 cup of buttermilk and 1 tablespoon of seasoning. Seal, squish it around so every strip is coated, and refrigerate for 3 hours or overnight.
  - Set up two shallow dishes. In the first, mix the flour, cornstarch, baking soda and breadcrumbs. In the second, whisk the ¾ cup of buttermilk with the 1½ teaspoons of seasoning.
  - Take the strips out of the bag and throw away the marinade. Coat each strip in the flour mix, dip it in the buttermilk, then coat it in the flour mix again. Dry, wet, dry is the order.
  - Lay the breaded strips on a wire rack and let them sit for 5 minutes so the coating sticks. Don't skip this; it's what gives the crunchy crust.
  - While they rest, heat the oil in a deep frying pan to about 375°F. Fry a few strips at a time, about 4 minutes per side, until golden brown and cooked through (165°F in the thickest part). Drain each batch on a paper-towel-lined tray.
  - Stir all the dipping sauce ingredients together in a small bowl until smooth and serve it alongside the hot chicken.
tips:
  - Fry in small batches. Crowding the pan drops the oil temperature and makes the breading greasy.
  - Make the sauce first and chill it while the chicken marinates. The flavors blend as it sits.
