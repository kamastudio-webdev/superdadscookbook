title: One-Pot Stovetop Mac and Cheese
description: Creamy, cheesy and done in about 20 minutes. The pasta cooks right in the milk, so there's no draining and only one pot.
category: Dinner

cookMinutes: 15
servings: 4 servings

ingredients:
  - 2 cups whole milk
  - 1½ cups water
  - 2 tablespoons butter
  - ½ teaspoon salt
  - 2 cups (8 ounces) dry elbow macaroni
  - 2 cups (8 ounces) shredded sharp cheddar cheese
  - ¼ teaspoon dry mustard powder (optional, it makes the cheese taste stronger)
  - Black pepper, to taste
steps:
  - Put the milk, water, butter and salt in a medium pot and bring to a gentle simmer over medium heat. Keep an eye on it; milk can boil over fast.
  - Stir in the macaroni. Simmer, stirring every minute or so so it doesn't stick to the bottom, until the pasta is tender and most of the liquid is absorbed, 10 to 12 minutes. Turn the heat down if it starts bubbling hard.
  - Take the pot off the heat. Add the cheese a handful at a time, stirring until it melts and the sauce is smooth. Stir in the mustard powder and a few grinds of pepper.
  - Let it sit for 2 minutes to thicken, then serve. If it gets too thick, stir in a splash of milk.
tips:
  - Shred your own cheese if you can. Bagged shredded cheese is coated in a powder that can make the sauce grainy.
  - Stir in a cup of frozen peas for the last 3 minutes of cooking for an easy vegetable.
  - Add a splash of milk before reheating leftovers to bring back the creaminess.
