  - Heat the oil in a large pot or deep skillet with a lid over medium heat. Add the rice and toast it, stirring often, for 5 minutes, until some grains turn golden and it smells nutty.
  - Add the onion and garlic and stir for a minute, then stir in the sauce, the packet of Sazón, the bouillon and the water.
  - Bring it to a boil, put the lid on, turn the heat to low and cook for 15 minutes.
  - Turn off the heat and let it rest, still covered, for 15 minutes so the rice finishes steaming.
