ingredients:
  - section: Original rub
    items:
      - 1 cup turbinado sugar
      - ¼ cup Morton kosher salt
      - 1 tablespoon McCormick granulated garlic
      - 1 tablespoon McCormick onion powder
      - 1 tablespoon McCormick chili powder
      - 1 tablespoon McCormick coarse-ground black pepper
      - 1 teaspoon McCormick cinnamon
      - 1 teaspoon McCormick dry mustard
      - ½ teaspoon McCormick cayenne pepper (40K heat)
  - section: Citrus rub
    items:
      - ½ cup turbinado sugar
      - ¼ cup kosher salt
      - 2 tablespoons lemon peel granules (from Spice Barn)
      - 2 tablespoons orange peel granules (from Spice Barn)
      - ½ teaspoon McCormick cayenne pepper (40K heat)
      - 2 tablespoons McCormick chili powder
      - 2 tablespoons McCormick oregano
      - 1 tablespoon McCormick onion powder
      - 1 tablespoon McCormick garlic powder
      - 1 tablespoon McCormick coarse-ground black pepper
  - section: Bold rub
    items:
      - ½ cup turbinado sugar
      - ¼ cup Morton kosher salt
      - 1 tablespoon McCormick minced garlic (dried)
      - 1 tablespoon McCormick chopped onion (dried)
      - 1 tablespoon McCormick oregano
      - 2 tablespoons McCormick chili powder
      - 1 tablespoon McCormick black pepper
      - 1 teaspoon McCormick red pepper flakes (40K heat)
      - 1 teaspoon McCormick dry mustard
      - ½ teaspoon McCormick cayenne pepper (40K heat)
steps:
  - Pick the rub you want to make and measure all of its ingredients into a food processor.
  - Pulse for 30 to 45 seconds, just until everything is evenly blended and the bigger bits (the sugar crystals and, in the Bold rub, the dried garlic and onion) are broken up a little. Don't run it so long that it turns to powder.
  - Spoon the rub into a jar with a tight lid and store it in a cool, dark cupboard.
tips:
  - The recipe calls for specific brands on purpose. You can swap in other brands, but the flavor will come out a little different.
  - Original is the sweetest of the three, Citrus adds lemon, orange and oregano, and Bold has more heat and texture from the dried garlic, onion and pepper flakes.
  - Lemon and orange peel granules are dried citrus peel. The original recipe orders them from spicebarn.com.
  - Shake or stir the jar before each use, since the sugar and salt can settle.
