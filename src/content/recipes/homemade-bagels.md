title: Homemade Bagels
description: Chewy, golden bagels made from a stiff bread-flour dough, boiled in a honey water bath and baked until deep brown.
category: Breads
prepMinutes: 40
cookMinutes: 25
restMinutes: 90
restLabel: Rise
servings: 8 bagels
date: 2026-11-07
tags: [baking, bagels, breakfast]
adaptedFrom:
  name: Sally's Baking Addiction

ingredients:
  - section: Dough
    items:
      - 1½ cups (360 ml) warm water, about 100 to 110°F
      - 2¾ teaspoons instant or active dry yeast
      - 4 cups (520 g) bread flour, spooned into the cup and leveled, plus a little more for your hands and the counter
      - 1 tablespoon granulated sugar or packed brown sugar (barley malt syrup also works)
      - 2 teaspoons salt
      - Nonstick spray or 2 teaspoons olive oil, for the bowl
  - section: Water bath
    items:
      - 2 quarts water
      - ¼ cup (60 g) honey (or barley malt syrup)
  - section: Egg wash
    items:
      - 1 egg white
      - 1 tablespoon water
steps:
  - Pour the warm water into the stand mixer bowl, whisk in the yeast, cover and let it sit for 5 minutes. No stand mixer? Use a large bowl and stir the dough with a sturdy wooden spoon instead.
  - Add the flour, sugar and salt. With the dough hook on low speed, mix for 2 minutes. The dough will be very stiff and look a little dry. That's normal.
  - Keep kneading on low for 6 to 7 more minutes, or turn the dough out and knead by hand on a lightly floured counter for the same time. If it sticks badly, add flour 1 teaspoon at a time, but stop as soon as it feels soft and just slightly tacky. It's ready when a finger poke slowly springs back, or when a golf-ball-sized piece stretches thin enough to see light through it without tearing.
  - Grease a large bowl, set the dough in it and turn it over so it's oiled all over. Cover with foil, plastic wrap or a clean towel and let it rise at room temperature until doubled, 60 to 90 minutes.
  - Line two large baking sheets with parchment paper or silicone mats.
  - Punch the dough down to let the air out and cut it into 8 roughly equal pieces. Roll each piece into a ball, push your finger through the middle and stretch the hole to about 1½ to 2 inches across. Cover the bagels loosely with a towel while you get the water ready.
  - Heat the oven to 425°F.
  - In a large, wide pot, whisk the honey into the 2 quarts of water and bring it to a boil, then turn the heat down to medium-high. Lower in 2 to 4 bagels at a time, only as many as can float without touching, and boil for 1 minute on each side. Lift them out onto the baking sheets, 4 per sheet.
  - Whisk the egg white with the 1 tablespoon of water and brush it over the top and sides of each bagel.
  - Bake for 20 to 25 minutes, turning the pans around halfway through, until the bagels are a deep golden brown. Let them cool on the pans for 20 minutes, then finish cooling on a wire rack.
tips:
  - Wrap leftover bagels tightly. They keep a few days on the counter or up to a week in the fridge.
  - The dough should stay stiff. Adding too much flour while kneading makes dry, dense bagels.
