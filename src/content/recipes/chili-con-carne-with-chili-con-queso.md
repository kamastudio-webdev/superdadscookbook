  name: Emeril Lagasse (Emerils.com)

ingredients:
  - section: Chili
    items:
      - 1 tablespoon bacon fat or vegetable oil
      - 1½ pounds ground beef
      - 2 cups chopped yellow onion
      - 1 cup chopped green bell pepper
      - 4 teaspoons minced garlic
      - 2 tablespoons chili powder
      - 1 tablespoon Essence (Creole seasoning, below)
      - ½ teaspoon salt
      - 2 teaspoons ground cumin
      - 1 teaspoon dried Mexican oregano
      - ¼ teaspoon cayenne
      - 1 can (15 ounces) whole peeled tomatoes
      - 3 tablespoons tomato paste
      - 1 teaspoon sugar
      - 2 cups water
      - 2 cans white beans, drained (Matt's addition)
  - section: Essence (Creole seasoning)
    items:
      - 2½ tablespoons paprika
      - 2 tablespoons salt
      - 2 tablespoons garlic powder
      - 1 tablespoon black pepper
      - 1 tablespoon onion powder
      - 1 tablespoon cayenne pepper
      - 1 tablespoon dried leaf oregano
      - 1 tablespoon dried thyme
  - section: Chili con queso
    items:
      - ½ pound fresh poblano or Anaheim chiles
      - 2 tablespoons vegetable oil
      - 1 cup chopped white onions
      - ¼ teaspoon cayenne
      - 2 tablespoons all-purpose flour
      - 1 large tomato, peeled, seeded and diced
      - 1 cup milk or cream
      - 1½ cups grated Cheddar
      - 1 cup grated Pepper Jack
  - section: For serving
    items:
      - Multicolored tortilla chips
steps:
  - "Make the Essence: stir all the seasoning ingredients together and keep the blend in an airtight jar. It makes about ⅔ cup, and the chili only needs 1 tablespoon."
  - Roast the chiles over an open gas flame, turning them often with tongs, until the skins are charred black all over, 7 to 10 minutes. (A broiler or a hot grill works too.) Close them up in a plastic or paper bag for about 15 minutes, until cool enough to handle. Peel off the skins, split them open, pull out the seeds and stems, and roughly chop them.
  - "Start the chili: heat the fat in a large, heavy pot over medium-high heat. Add the beef and break it up with a wooden spoon. Cook, stirring, until it's browned with no pink left, about 5 minutes."
  - Add the onion, bell pepper, garlic, chili powder, Essence, salt, cumin, oregano and cayenne. Cook, stirring, until the vegetables soften, about 4 minutes.
  - Put the tomatoes in a bowl and squeeze them with your hands to break them into pieces. Add them and their juice to the pot with the tomato paste, sugar, water and white beans. Stir well and bring to a boil.
  - Turn the heat down to medium-low and simmer, uncovered, for 30 minutes, stirring now and then so the chili doesn't stick to the bottom.
  - "While the chili simmers, make the queso: heat the oil in a large pot over medium heat. Add the onions and cayenne and cook, stirring, until very soft, about 6 minutes. Stir in the flour and cook until it thickens, 1 to 2 minutes."
  - Add the tomato and chopped chiles and cook for 1 minute. Pour in the milk or cream slowly, stirring, and cook until thick, 3 to 4 minutes. Add both cheeses and stir until they melt into a smooth sauce. Take it off the heat and taste for salt.
  - Ladle the chili into a large serving bowl, swirl the queso through the middle and serve right away with tortilla chips.
tips:
  - Matt adds 2 cans of white beans along with the tomatoes. His verdict on the printout was "Very good!"
  - Leftover Essence is good on chicken, pork, steaks and roasted vegetables.
  - The queso is also great on its own as a dip with chips.
