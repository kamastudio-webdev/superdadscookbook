---
title: Adam and Amy's Jambalaya
description: A quick weeknight jambalaya with browned chicken, andouille sausage and ham, the classic onion, pepper and celery, stewed tomatoes and Cajun seasoning, all stirred together with cooked rice.
category: Dinner
cuisine: Cajun
prepMinutes: 25
cookMinutes: 25
servings: 6 servings
date: 2026-11-08
tags: [chicken, sausage, rice, one-pot, family-recipe]
ingredients:
  - 1 lb andouille or other Cajun-style sausage
  - 4 chicken breast halves
  - Black pepper
  - 2 tablespoons peanut or vegetable oil
  - 1 large onion, finely chopped
  - 1 medium green pepper, chopped
  - ½ cup chopped celery
  - 5 cloves garlic, minced
  - 1 cup chopped cooked ham
  - 2 to 4 teaspoons Cajun seasoning
  - 1 can (14½ oz) stewed tomatoes, undrained
  - ½ cup chicken broth
  - ¼ teaspoon dried thyme
  - ¼ teaspoon ground red pepper
  - 3 cups hot cooked rice
  - 1 cup finely chopped green onions
steps:
  - Cut the sausage into bite-size pieces. Cut the chicken into small pieces and sprinkle them with black pepper.
  - Heat the oil in a large Dutch oven over medium-high heat. Add the chicken and cook, stirring often, for about 4 minutes, until browned. It will finish cooking later. Scoop it out and set it aside.
  - Add the sausage to the pot and cook, stirring constantly, for about 4 minutes, until browned. Scoop it out and set it aside with the chicken.
  - Put the onion, green pepper, celery and garlic in the pot. Cook uncovered for 5 to 7 minutes, until the vegetables are crisp-tender.
  - Stir in the chicken, sausage, ham, Cajun seasoning, stewed tomatoes with their juice, chicken broth, thyme, red pepper, rice and green onions.
  - Cook for about 7 minutes, stirring now and then, until everything is hot all the way through and the chicken is cooked (165°F).
tips:
  - Start with 2 teaspoons of Cajun seasoning and taste before adding more. Some blends are very salty.
  - About 1 cup of uncooked long-grain rice makes the 3 cups of cooked rice. Cook it first so it's ready to stir in.
