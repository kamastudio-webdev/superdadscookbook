---
title: Seafood Beer Batter
description: A light, puffy beer batter with a little dry mustard and cayenne for frying shrimp, fish and other seafood.
category: Basics
prepMinutes: 10
cookMinutes: 15
servings: About 2 cups of batter
date: 2026-11-08
tags: [batter, fried, seafood, family-recipe]
ingredients:
  - 1¼ cups flour
  - 2 teaspoons baking powder
  - ½ teaspoon salt
  - 1 tablespoon sugar
  - 1 teaspoon dry mustard
  - ¼ teaspoon cayenne pepper
  - 1 egg
  - 8 oz beer (1 cup), plus more as needed
steps:
  - In a medium bowl, stir together the flour, baking powder, salt, sugar, dry mustard and cayenne.
  - In a second bowl, beat the egg, then gently stir in the beer.
  - Pour the egg and beer slowly into the flour mixture, stirring as you go, just until there are no dry streaks. A few small lumps are fine.
  - Check the thickness. The batter should coat a spoon like thick pancake batter. If it's too thick, stir in a little more beer, a splash at a time.
  - Heat about 2 inches of oil in a heavy pot to 375°F. Pat the seafood dry, dip it in the batter, let the extra drip off, and fry a few pieces at a time until puffed and golden, about 2 to 4 minutes. Drain on a rack or paper towels.
tips:
  - Mix the batter right before you fry. The bubbles in the beer and the baking powder are what make the coating light and crisp.
  - Patting the seafood dry first helps the batter stick.
