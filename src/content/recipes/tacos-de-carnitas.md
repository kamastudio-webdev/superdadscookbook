---
title: Tacos de Carnitas
description: Pork shoulder simmered with orange zest, garlic and warm spices until tender, then fried in its own fat until crisp at the edges and piled into corn tortillas.
category: Dinner
prepMinutes: 15
cookMinutes: 135
servings: 6 to 8 servings (24 tacos)
date: 2026-11-07
tags: [pork, tacos, mexican, crowd-pleaser]
adaptedFrom:
  name: NYT Cooking (Kim Severson)
  url: https://cooking.nytimes.com/recipes/1012463-tacos-de-carnitas
ingredients:
  - section: Carnitas
    items:
      - 3 pounds pork shoulder (butt or picnic)
      - 7 strips orange zest
      - 5 garlic cloves, minced
      - 1 large onion, chopped
      - 1¼ teaspoons crushed red pepper flakes
      - 1 cinnamon stick (Mexican canela if you can find it)
      - 2 bay leaves
      - 1½ teaspoons dried oregano, crushed (Mexican oregano is best)
      - 1½ teaspoons kosher salt, plus more to taste
      - ¼ teaspoon ground cloves
  - section: For serving
    items:
      - 24 small corn tortillas, warmed
      - Finely chopped onion
      - Chopped cilantro
      - Salsa
steps:
  - Trim the thick fat off the outside of the pork, then cut the meat into 1-inch cubes. Throw away any pieces that are nothing but fat.
  - Put the pork in a large pot with the orange zest, garlic, chopped onion, red pepper flakes, cinnamon stick, bay leaves, oregano, 1½ teaspoons salt and cloves. Pour in enough water to cover everything by 2 inches.
  - Bring it to a boil, then turn the heat down to a steady simmer and skim off any foam that rises. Simmer uncovered until the pork is very tender, about 1½ hours. Add water as needed so the meat stays under the liquid.
  - Taste and add salt if it needs it. Keep simmering, without adding more water, until the liquid has cooked away, about 30 minutes more.
  - Once the pot is dry, the pork starts to fry in its own fat. Stir often and let it brown for a few minutes, longer if you like it crispier. If it sticks or looks close to burning, splash in a little water.
  - Pull out the bay leaves and cinnamon stick. Spoon a few tablespoons of carnitas onto each warm tortilla and top with chopped onion, cilantro and salsa.
tips:
  - Be patient at the end. The crispy browned bits only happen after the liquid is completely gone.
  - Leftover carnitas also make a great filling for burritos, tamales or empanadas.
