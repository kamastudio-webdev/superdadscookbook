title: The Best Buttercream Frosting
description: A simple, fluffy vanilla buttercream from four ingredients. It spreads and pipes well and goes on just about anything.
category: Basics
prepMinutes: 10
cookMinutes: 0
servings: About 2½ cups
date: 2026-11-07

adaptedFrom:
  name: Two Sisters Crafting

ingredients:
  - 1 cup (2 sticks) butter, slightly softened (salted butter works great)
  - 2 to 3 teaspoons vanilla extract
  - 1 pound powdered sugar (about 4 cups)
  - 1 to 2 tablespoons milk, as needed
steps:
  - Take the butter out of the fridge a little ahead of time. You want it partly softened, somewhere between fridge-cold and room temperature.
  - Put the butter and vanilla in the mixer bowl and beat on medium speed until completely blended, about 2 minutes. Scrape down the sides of the bowl.
  - Add the powdered sugar. Without a kitchen scale, 4 cups is about 1 pound.
  - Start the mixer on its lowest speed so the sugar doesn't puff out of the bowl, and mix until the butter and sugar come together, about 30 seconds. Stop and scrape the bowl.
  - Turn the mixer up to medium-high and beat for another 30 to 45 seconds, until the frosting turns light and fluffy.
  - If it looks dry or crumbly, add 1 teaspoon of milk and mix again. Keep adding milk a teaspoon at a time until it's as soft as you want. Taste it, and add a splash more vanilla if you'd like more flavor.
tips:
  - Try it on the Vanilla Cupcakes.
  - For more frosting, make a second batch rather than doubling this one. Most home mixers struggle with a double batch.
