title: Flaky Homemade Biscuits
description: Tall, flaky biscuits made with grated frozen butter, ready in about 30 minutes. This makes 6, and it doubles easily for a dozen.
category: Breads
prepMinutes: 15
cookMinutes: 12
servings: 6 biscuits
date: 2026-11-06
tags: [breakfast, side-dish, vegetarian]
adaptedFrom:
  name: Sugar Spun Run
  url: https://sugarspunrun.com/easy-homemade-biscuits/
ingredients:
  - 2 cups all-purpose flour
  - 1 tablespoon baking powder
  - 1 tablespoon granulated sugar
  - 1 teaspoon salt
  - 6 tablespoons unsalted butter, very cold
  - ¾ cup whole milk (buttermilk or 2% also work)
steps:
  - Put the butter in the freezer for 10 to 20 minutes. Very cold butter is what makes flaky layers.
  - Heat the oven to 425°F and line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
  - Mix the flour, baking powder, sugar and salt in a large bowl.
  - Grate the cold butter on a box grater right into the flour (or cut it in with a pastry cutter), and toss until it looks like coarse crumbs.
  - Stir in the milk with a wooden spoon just until it comes together. Don't overmix.
  - Turn the dough out onto a well-floured counter and press it together with your hands, adding a little flour if it's sticky. Fold it in half, press it flat, give it a quarter turn, and repeat 5 or 6 times.
  - Pat the dough about 1 inch thick with your hands, not a rolling pin. Dip a 2½-inch biscuit cutter in flour and press straight down without twisting. Set the biscuits close together on the pan, and press the scraps together for one or two more.
  - Bake for 12 minutes, until the tops are just turning golden. Brush with melted butter if you like and serve warm.
tips:
  - Press the cutter straight down. Twisting it seals the edges, and the biscuits won't rise as tall.
